## Teaching Point

The learner should stop translating "est/ye" word-by-word and instead choose the English entry block:

- `there is a problem`
- `there are problems`
- `there is water`
- `Are there any questions?`

## Copy Rules

- Learner-facing RU/UK copy avoids raw internal grammar labels.
- Feedback explains the visible decision: one thing, several things, or words like water/time/information.
- Keep have/has as a contrast only when ownership is the mistake.

## Structure

- Easy: one thing, one problem, several chairs.
- Contrast: people, questions, negative chairs, water, time, information.
- Mixed: question order, negative information, pair repair, full sentence repair.
