# Personal Plans Final Content Review Checklist

This is a recommended checklist.

It is non-blocking during generation. It is not the current gate. It is not production approval. It helps final review after the full generated corpus is assembled.

## Core Product Rules

- Personal Plans are separate tasks.
- Lessons are not plan tasks.
- Selected daily time chooses the initial visible workload.
- Every generated day keeps the full maximum task pool.
- 5/10/15/20 minute choices reveal different starting counts, then "Add more tasks" can reveal remaining tasks one by one.
- Different days should use a different progression role and content pressure.
- Return blockers instead of fake readiness.

## Phrase Quality

- Phrases sound alive and practical.
- A learner could realistically say the phrase in the plan scenario.
- The phrase is short enough for mobile practice.
- The phrase is not textbook-stiff, over-formal, or weirdly poetic.
- The phrase does not expose private/personal data.
- The plan keeps its own voice: Gavan is practical, Mitap is work-focused, Voyazh is travel-pressure, Impuls is spontaneous speech, Echo is listening-first.

## Translation Quality

- Translation is short and clear.
- Translation explains meaning, not every grammar detail.
- Translation matches the scenario.
- Translation does not add extra ideas that are not in the English phrase.

## Teaching Notes

- Every phrase has a useful teaching note.
- The note explains a real learner trap or useful nuance.
- The note does not mention internal systems, routes, generated shells, scaffolds, or implementation details.
- The note helps the user answer better next time.

## Daily Task Set

- Modes cover the maximum day pool where content exists.
- The initial visible task count matches the selected time tier.
- Remaining tasks are suitable for add-more reveal after the visible slice is complete.
- Review days actually recall previous plan phrases.
- Listening modes say what audio evidence is still needed when audio is not approved.
- Pronunciation modes say what scorer or recording evidence is still needed when pronunciation is not ready.

## Plan Fit

- Gavan: forms, address, doctor, bank, housing, city services.
- Mitap: meetings, next steps, deadlines, owners, blockers, follow-up.
- Voyazh: airport, hotel, transport, directions, urgent help.
- Impuls: quick answers, short story, because, opinion, recovery after mistakes.
- Echo: listening, repeat, heard, missed, time/place, short response.

## Recommended Reviewer Notes

For each reviewed packet, the reviewer can write:

- strongest phrases;
- weak phrases to rewrite;
- task modes that feel too heavy or too light;
- missing recall links;
- audio or pronunciation evidence still needed;
- whether the packet is ready to continue to internal quality gating.

This checklist alone does not approve runtime integration. Runtime/source writes still require the separate guarded integration path.
