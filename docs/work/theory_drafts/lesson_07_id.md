# Teori · Pelajaran 7 kursus baru — «Do you…? Does she…? I don't. Where do you…? usually, often» (ID)

> Sesi «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Bagian 01–11, kalimat bahasa Inggris, dan latihan sama dengan
> `lesson_07_ru.md`. Jebakan penutur bahasa Indonesia: «Apakah kamu bekerja…?» → Are you work…?,
> «tidak» di depan kata kerja (→ I not gossip), «tidak ada kurir» (→ There not courier), kata tanya
> di belakang («Kamu bekerja di mana?» → You work where?), kata kerja tidak berubah (→ Does he
> plays). Sapaan: kamu. Perlu dibaca ulang oleh penutur asli.

**Judul layar:** Teori · Bertanya dan bilang «tidak» tentang kebiasaan

## 01 · Apa yang akan kamu pelajari *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Di pelajaran 6 kamu bercerita apa yang dilakukan orang: I work, she cooks. Sekarang kamu belajar bertanya — «kamu bekerja di pabrik?», «kamu parkir di mana?» —, bilang «tidak» — «aku tidak bergosip» — dan menambahkan «biasanya» dan «sering».

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Do you work in a factory? | Kamu bekerja di pabrik? | Do |
| Does the salon close late? | Salonnya tutup malam? | Does |
| I don't gossip. | Aku tidak bergosip. | don't |
| Where do you park? | Kamu parkir di mana? | Where do |
| I usually recycle. | Aku biasanya memilah sampah. | usually |

---

## 02 · Pertanyaan dimulai dengan Do *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Kita bertanya dengan «apakah» atau nada saja. «Apakah» bukan Are: Are you work… salah. Untuk kegiatan, bahasa Inggris memakai Do kecil di depan: Do you work in a factory? Kegiatannya tidak berubah.

**formula**
`Do` · `you / they / we` · `kegiatan` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Do you work in a factory? | Kamu bekerja di pabrik? | Do |
| Do they send packages? | Mereka mengirim paket? | Do |
| Do you have a menu? | Ada menu? | Do |
| Do you work at night? | Kamu kerja malam? | Do |
| Do they check tickets here? | Di sini tiketnya diperiksa? | Do |

**fix**
- ~~Are you work in a factory?~~ → Do you work in a factory?
- ~~You work in a factory?~~ → Do you work in a factory?
- ~~Have menu?~~ → Do you have a menu?

**drill · word_bank**
- tugas: Tanyakan: «Kamu bekerja di pabrik?»
- jawaban: `Do` · `you` · `work` · `in` · `a` · `factory`
- label: tidak ada (kalimatnya panjang)
- kartu lebih: `Are` · `works`

---

## 03 · Does — untuk he, she, dan satu orang

**body**
Kalau bertanya tentang he, she, it, atau satu orang — the barber, the fan — Do diganti Does. Huruf s dari kegiatan (he works) pindah ke does dan hilang dari kegiatan: Does he work?

**formula**
`Does` · `he / she / it / satu orang` · `kegiatan tanpa s` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Does the barber work here? | Tukang cukurnya kerja di sini? | Does |
| Does the fan work? | Kipasnya nyala? | Does |
| Does he play chess? | Dia main catur? | Does |
| Does she play tennis? | Dia main tenis? | Does |
| Does the cafe serve sushi? | Kafenya menyajikan sushi? | Does |

**fix**
- ~~Does he plays chess?~~ → Does he play chess?
- ~~Do the fan work?~~ → Does the fan work?
- ~~Does she plays tennis?~~ → Does she play tennis?

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `Does the metro ___ late?`
- pilihan: `run` · `runs` · `running`
- jawaban: `run`
- kenapa: s sudah pindah ke does, kegiatannya tanpa s.

## 04 · Do atau penjepit?

**body**
Kalau ada kegiatan dalam pertanyaan — pakai Do atau Does: Do they fix lamps? Kalau tidak ada kegiatan dan kamu bertanya siapa atau seperti apa — pakai penjepit, seperti di pelajaran 2: Are you a plumber? Do dan penjepit tidak dipakai bersamaan.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Are you a plumber? | Kamu tukang ledeng? | Are |
| Do they fix lamps? | Mereka memperbaiki lampu? | Do |
| Is the lesson difficult? | Pelajarannya sulit? | Is |
| Does the vet start early? | Dokter hewannya mulai pagi? | Does |

**fix**
- ~~Do you a plumber?~~ → Are you a plumber?
- ~~Are they fix lamps?~~ → Do they fix lamps?

**drill · binary**
- pertanyaan: Kamu mau tahu apakah bengkel itu memperbaiki lampu. Mana yang benar?
- A: `Are they fix lamps?`
- B: `Do they fix lamps?`
- benar: B
- penjelasan: Ada kegiatan fix, jadi Do.

## 05 · «Tidak»: don't dan doesn't

**body**
Kita menaruh «tidak» tepat di depan kata kerja: «aku tidak bergosip». Bahasa Inggris perlu don't — dengan I, you, we, they — atau doesn't — dengan he, she, it, dan satu orang. Sesudah doesn't kegiatan tanpa s: He doesn't have a house.

**formula**
`siapa` · `don't / doesn't` · `kegiatan tanpa s`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I don't gossip. | Aku tidak bergosip. | don't |
| I don't brag. | Aku tidak pamer. | don't |
| I don't have money. | Aku tidak punya uang. | don't |
| He doesn't have a house. | Dia tidak punya rumah. | doesn't |
| The horn doesn't work. | Klaksonnya tidak bunyi. | doesn't |

**fix**
- ~~I not gossip.~~ → I don't gossip.
- ~~He don't have a house.~~ → He doesn't have a house.
- ~~He doesn't has a house.~~ → He doesn't have a house.
- ~~The horn doesn't works.~~ → The horn doesn't work.

**drill · spot_slip**
- kartu: `The` · `horn` · `doesn't` · `works`
- salah: `works` (indeks 3)
- petunjuk: Ada satu kata dengan huruf s yang berlebih. Ketuk kata itu.
- penjelasan: Sesudah doesn't kegiatan tanpa s: The horn doesn't work.

## 06 · There is no — «di sini tidak ada»

**body**
Untuk bilang sesuatu tidak ada di sini, pakai There is no: There is no courier here — «di sini tidak ada kurir». Untuk banyak benda — There are no. Sesudah no tidak pakai a atau any.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| There is no courier here. | Di sini tidak ada kurir. | no |
| There is no tape here. | Di sini tidak ada selotip. | no |
| There is no bus in the morning. | Pagi tidak ada bus. | no |
| There are no pliers here. | Di sini tidak ada tang. | no |
| There is no guard at the gate. | Di gerbang tidak ada satpam. | no |

**fix**
- ~~There not courier here.~~ → There is no courier here.
- ~~Here no courier.~~ → There is no courier here.
- ~~There is no a courier here.~~ → There is no courier here.

**tip**
There isn't a courier here artinya sama, kamu sudah tahu dari pelajaran 4. There is no terdengar sedikit lebih tegas.

## 07 · Di mana dan kapan: Where do…? When does…?

**body**
Kita sering menaruh kata tanya di belakang: «Kamu bekerja di mana?». Bahasa Inggris menaruh Where atau When di depan, lalu seperti pertanyaan biasa: do atau does, siapa, kegiatan. Where do you work?

**formula**
`Where / When` · `do / does` · `siapa` · `kegiatan` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Where do you work? | Kamu bekerja di mana? | Where do |
| Where do you park? | Kamu parkir di mana? | Where do |
| When does the rink close? | Arena seluncur tutup jam berapa? | When does |
| When do you have lunch? | Kamu makan siang jam berapa? | When do |
| When does she harvest apples? | Kapan dia memanen apel? | When does |

**fix**
- ~~You work where?~~ → Where do you work?
- ~~Where you work?~~ → Where do you work?
- ~~When does she harvests apples?~~ → When does she harvest apples?

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `When ___ the rink close?`
- pilihan: `do` · `does` · `is`
- jawaban: `does`
- kenapa: Arena seluncurnya satu, seperti he atau she, jadi does.

## 08 · usually dan often — di depan kegiatan

**body**
usually — «biasanya», often — «sering». Keduanya tepat di depan kegiatan: I usually recycle, I often iron shirts. Mirip bahasa Indonesia: «aku sering menyetrika kemeja».

**formula**
`siapa` · `usually / often` · `kegiatan`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I usually recycle. | Aku biasanya memilah sampah. | usually |
| I often iron shirts. | Aku sering menyetrika kemeja. | often |
| She often dusts the shelves. | Dia sering membersihkan debu di rak. | often |
| I usually unplug the kettle. | Aku biasanya mencabut colokan ketel. | usually |

**fix**
- ~~I iron often shirts.~~ → I often iron shirts.
- ~~She often dust the shelves.~~ → She often dusts the shelves.
- ~~I usually recycles.~~ → I usually recycle.

**tip**
usually dan often tidak mengubah apa pun: sesudah she, kegiatan tetap pakai s — she often dusts.

**drill · binary**
- pertanyaan: Kamu bercerita tentang dirimu. Mana yang benar?
- A: `I iron often shirts.`
- B: `I often iron shirts.`
- benar: B
- penjelasan: often tepat di depan kegiatan iron.

---

## 09 · «Kamu sering…?» — Do you often…?

**body**
Dalam pertanyaan, often tetap di tempat yang sama: di depan kegiatan, sesudah siapa. Do you often forget the key? Does your brother often miss the bus? Dulu Do atau Does, lalu siapa, lalu often, lalu kegiatan.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Do you often forget the key? | Kamu sering lupa kunci? | often |
| Does your brother often miss the bus? | Kakakmu sering ketinggalan bus? | often |

**fix**
- ~~Do often you forget the key?~~ → Do you often forget the key?
- ~~Does your brother often misses the bus?~~ → Does your brother often miss the bus?

---

## 10 · Kesalahan yang dibuat hampir semua orang

**fix**
- ~~Are you work in a factory?~~ → Do you work in a factory?
- ~~You work in a factory?~~ → Do you work in a factory?
- ~~Does he plays chess?~~ → Does he play chess?
- ~~I not gossip.~~ → I don't gossip.
- ~~He don't have a house.~~ → He doesn't have a house.
- ~~There not courier here.~~ → There is no courier here.
- ~~You work where?~~ → Where do you work?
- ~~I iron often shirts.~~ → I often iron shirts.

**tip**
Aturan utama: huruf s hanya di satu tempat. Ada does atau doesn't — kegiatan tanpa s. Tidak ada does — sesudah he dan she, kegiatan pakai s.

## 11 · Bekal untuk latihan

**body**
Bertanya — Do atau Does di depan. «Tidak» — don't atau doesn't di depan kegiatan. Sesudah does dan doesn't, tanpa s. «Di sini tidak ada» — There is no. Di mana, kapan — Where, When + do / does. «Biasanya», «sering» — tepat di depan kegiatan.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Do they work on weekends? | Mereka kerja di akhir pekan? | Do |
| Does the coach leave early? | Busnya berangkat pagi? | Does |
| He doesn't have a house. | Dia tidak punya rumah. | doesn't |
| When does the rink close? | Arena seluncur tutup jam berapa? | When does |
| She often dusts the shelves. | Dia sering membersihkan debu di rak. | often |
