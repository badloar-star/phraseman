## Teaching Point

The learner is not choosing by translation. They make two quick checks:

1. One thing or several things.
2. Close to the speaker/current moment or far from it.

Then the grid becomes:

- `this phone`
- `that phone`
- `these keys`
- `those houses`

## Copy Rules

- Learner-facing RU/UK copy avoids raw internal grammar labels.
- Feedback explains the visible reason: one/several, close/far, current/known time.
- Distractors are specific, not generic.

## Structure

- Easy: clear one-close, one-far, several-close, several-far.
- Contrast: this vs these, that vs those, current question, remembered day.
- Mixed: phrase pairs, standalone `these are`, `those are`, `this week`, `that evening`, full sentence repair.
