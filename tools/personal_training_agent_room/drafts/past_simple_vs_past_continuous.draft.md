# past_simple_vs_past_continuous draft

Status: Jesse reworked.

Training id: `past_simple_vs_past_continuous`
Category: `verb`
Priority: `43`

Single learner problem:
The learner sees one past translation in RU/UK and chooses tense by translation, not by the role of the action in the scene.

Core contrast:
- Past Simple = fact, short event, or sequence: `I worked yesterday`, `the phone rang`, `I entered the room`.
- Past Continuous = process at a past moment or background action: `I was working at 8`, `they were watching TV`.
- `when` often brings the event.
- `while` often brings the background process.

What this training must not become:
- Not the basic `was/were + -ing` training; that is `past_continuous_basic`.
- Not a broad past-tense grammar lesson.
- Not a question/negative lesson.
- Not a spelling lesson for `-ing`.

Learner-facing copy rule:
- RU/UK copy explains through action role: `факт / событие / процесс на фоне`, `факт / подія / процес на фоні`.
- Avoid internal labels such as `subject`, `object`, `base verb`, `main verb`, `auxiliary`, and `modal`.
- Keep the live contrasts visible: `I worked yesterday` vs `I was working at 8`.

Required contract anchors:
- `past_simple_cont_easy_001`: wrong `was working` feedback mentions `worked`.
- `past_simple_cont_easy_002`: wrong `worked` feedback mentions `was working`.
- `past_simple_cont_contrast_002`: wrong `was ringing` feedback mentions `rang`.
- `past_simple_cont_mixed_005`: wrong `I entered the room, and they watched TV.` feedback mentions `were watching`.

Final learner outcome:
The learner can choose by role:
`what happened?` -> Past Simple
`what was happening at that moment?` -> Past Continuous
`event + background` -> `entered / were watching`
`while` background -> `was working`
`when` event -> `called`
