# Teori · Pelajaran 20 kursus baru — «must, have to, should, ought to, mustn't, don't have to, needn't» (ID)

> Sesi «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 22.10.2026. Bagian 01–10, kalimat bahasa Inggris dan latihan sama seperti
> `lesson_20_ru.md`. Jebakan penutur Indonesia: «harus» untuk must dan have to, «tidak harus»
> (tidak wajib) tertukar dengan «tidak boleh» (dilarang) → mustn't padahal maksudnya don't have to,
> «sebaiknya» diberi to (→ should to). Sapaan: kamu. Perlu dibaca penutur asli.

**Judul layar:** Teori · Harus, sebaiknya, tidak boleh

---

## 01 · Apa yang akan kamu pelajari *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Pelajaran ini tentang aturan dan saran: «dia harus menunjukkan identitas», «saya harus membaca kontrak», «sebaiknya kamu tanya guru», «tidak boleh mengabaikan aturan», «saya tidak perlu beli perlengkapan». Yang terpenting: jangan tertukar «tidak boleh» dan «tidak perlu».

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She must show identification. | Dia harus menunjukkan identitas. | must |
| I have to read the contract. | Saya harus membaca kontraknya. | have to |
| You should consult a teacher. | Sebaiknya kamu tanya guru. | should |
| You mustn't ignore the rules. | Tidak boleh mengabaikan aturan. | mustn't |
| I don't have to buy supplies. | Saya tidak perlu beli perlengkapan. | don't have to |

---

## 02 · must — «harus, wajib» *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
must + kata kerja seperti di kamus — «harus», «wajib»: You must take your belongings. Must sama untuk semua, tanpa -s, dan setelahnya tidak pernah ada to.

**formula**
`siapa` · `must` · `kata kerja seperti di kamus`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| You must take your belongings. | Kamu harus membawa barang-barangmu. | must |
| She must show identification. | Dia harus menunjukkan identitas. | must |
| We must check the expiry date. | Kita harus cek tanggal kedaluwarsa. | must |
| We must obey the rules. | Kita wajib menaati aturan. | must |
| You must be respectful. | Kamu harus bersikap sopan. | must be |

**fix**
- ~~You must to take your belongings.~~ → You must take your belongings.
- ~~You musts take your belongings.~~ → You must take your belongings.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `She must ___ identification.`
- pilihan: `show` · `shows` · `to show`
- jawaban: `show`
- kenapa: Setelah must, kata kerja seperti di kamus: tanpa -s dan tanpa to.

---

## 03 · have to — «harus (karena keadaan)»

**body**
have to + kata kerja — «harus», «terpaksa», sering karena keadaan: I have to read the contract. Dengan he / she pakai has to. Tanpa to artinya lain: I have read — «saya sudah membaca».

**formula**
`I / you / we / they: have to + kata kerja` · `he / she: has to + kata kerja`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I have to read the contract. | Saya harus membaca kontraknya. | have to |
| She has to enter her occupation. | Dia harus mengisi pekerjaannya. | has to |
| I occasionally have to work late. | Kadang saya harus lembur. | have to |
| He has to follow the diversion. | Dia harus lewat jalan pengalihan. | has to |
| I have to show proof of purchase. | Saya harus menunjukkan bukti pembelian. | have to |

**fix**
- ~~I has to read the contract.~~ → I have to read the contract.
- ~~She have to enter her occupation.~~ → She has to enter her occupation.
- ~~I have read the contract.~~ *(harus membaca)* → I have to read the contract.

---

## 04 · should — «sebaiknya»

**body**
should + kata kerja adalah saran: «sebaiknya», «seharusnya». You should consult a teacher. Should juga tanpa -s dan tanpa to.

**formula**
`siapa` · `should` · `kata kerja seperti di kamus`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| You should consult a teacher. | Sebaiknya kamu tanya guru. | should |
| You should sleep on it. | Sebaiknya kamu pikirkan dulu semalam. | should |
| He should get some rest. | Sebaiknya dia istirahat sebentar. | should |
| We should use up the milk. | Sebaiknya susunya kita habiskan. | should |
| She should spend sensibly. | Sebaiknya dia belanja dengan bijak. | should |

**fix**
- ~~You should to consult a teacher.~~ → You should consult a teacher.
- ~~You shoulds consult a teacher.~~ → You should consult a teacher.

---

## 05 · ought to — «semestinya»

**body**
ought to juga saran, hampir sama dengan should, tapi pakai to: You ought to set a budget — «semestinya kamu buat anggaran». Di sini to wajib, -s tidak.

**formula**
`siapa` · `ought to` · `kata kerja seperti di kamus`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| You ought to set a budget. | Semestinya kamu buat anggaran. | ought to |
| We ought to find the cause. | Kita semestinya mencari penyebabnya. | ought to |
| You ought to keep in mind the departure time. | Kamu semestinya ingat jam keberangkatan. | ought to |
| We ought to express our gratitude. | Kita semestinya menyampaikan terima kasih. | ought to |

**fix**
- ~~You ought read the national news.~~ → You ought to read the national news.
- ~~You oughts to read the national news.~~ → You ought to read the national news.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `You ought ___ set a budget.`
- pilihan: `to` · `—` · `for`
- jawaban: `to`
- kenapa: Setelah ought selalu ada to — beda dengan should dan must.

---

## 06 · mustn't — «tidak boleh»

**body**
mustn't adalah larangan: «tidak boleh», «sama sekali jangan». You mustn't ignore the rules. Hati-hati: «tidak harus» itu don't have to, bukan mustn't. Setelah mustn't kata kerja tanpa to.

**formula**
`siapa` · `mustn't` · `kata kerja seperti di kamus`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| You mustn't ignore the rules. | Tidak boleh mengabaikan aturan. | mustn't |
| We mustn't reveal the answer. | Kita tidak boleh membocorkan jawabannya. | mustn't |
| You mustn't harm the animals. | Tidak boleh menyakiti hewan. | mustn't |
| We mustn't offend our neighbours. | Kita tidak boleh menyinggung tetangga. | mustn't |

**fix**
- ~~You mustn't to ignore the rules.~~ → You mustn't ignore the rules.

---

## 07 · don't have to dan needn't — «tidak perlu»

**body**
«Tidak harus, tidak perlu» — don't / doesn't have to atau needn't: I don't have to buy supplies; You needn't reply straight away. Ini bukan larangan! Larangan itu mustn't. Setelah needn't tanpa to, setelah don't have pakai to.

**formula**
`tidak harus: don't / doesn't have to + kata kerja` · `tidak perlu: needn't + kata kerja`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I don't have to buy supplies. | Saya tidak perlu beli perlengkapan. | don't have to |
| She doesn't have to bring a photocopy. | Dia tidak harus membawa fotokopi. | doesn't have to |
| He doesn't have to head back yet. | Dia belum harus pulang. | doesn't have to |
| You needn't reply straight away. | Kamu tidak perlu langsung membalas. | needn't |
| We needn't dress formally. | Kita tidak perlu berpakaian resmi. | needn't |

**fix**
- ~~She don't have to bring a photocopy.~~ → She doesn't have to bring a photocopy.
- ~~I don't has to buy supplies.~~ → I don't have to buy supplies.
- ~~You needn't to collect it personally.~~ → You needn't collect it personally.

**drill · binary**
- pertanyaan: Cat di kelas melukis disediakan panitia. Kamu tidak perlu beli, tapi juga tidak dilarang. Kamu bilang apa?
- A: `I mustn't buy supplies.`
- B: `I don't have to buy supplies.`
- benar: B
- penjelasan: «Tidak perlu» — don't have to. Mustn't artinya «tidak boleh».

## 08 · Could I…? — ulangan

**body**
Permintaan sopan dari pelajaran 19 muncul juga di sini: Could I pay separately? — «boleh saya bayar terpisah?». Could dulu, lalu I dan kata kerja tanpa to.

**formula**
`Could I` · `kata kerja seperti di kamus` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Could I pay separately? | Boleh saya bayar terpisah? | Could I |
| Could I take a look? | Boleh saya lihat sebentar? | Could I |
| Could I pay on arrival? | Boleh saya bayar saat tiba? | Could I |
| Could I send it by post? | Boleh saya kirim lewat pos? | Could I |

**fix**
- ~~Could I to pay separately?~~ → Could I pay separately?

**drill · word_bank**
- tugas: Minta: «Boleh saya coba?»
- jawaban: `Could` · `I` · `give` · `it` · `a` · `try`
- label kotak: tidak ada (kalimat panjang)
- kartu lebih: `to` · `did`

## 09 · Kesalahan yang dibuat hampir semua orang

**fix**
- ~~I mustn't buy supplies.~~ *(tidak perlu)* → I don't have to buy supplies.
- ~~You must to take your belongings.~~ → You must take your belongings.
- ~~I has to read the contract.~~ → I have to read the contract.
- ~~You ought read the national news.~~ → You ought to read the national news.
- ~~You needn't to collect it personally.~~ → You needn't collect it personally.

**tip**
to hanya ada di have to dan ought to. Setelah must, should, mustn't, dan needn't langsung kata kerja. Dilarang — mustn't, tidak perlu — don't have to atau needn't.

---

## 10 · Yang dibawa ke latihan

**body**
Wajib — must. Harus karena keadaan — have / has to. Saran — should atau ought to. Tidak boleh — mustn't. Tidak perlu — don't have to atau needn't.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We must go through the list. | Kita harus memeriksa daftarnya. | must |
| He should stand up for his friend. | Sebaiknya dia membela temannya. | should |
| You needn't justify your choice. | Kamu tidak perlu membenarkan pilihanmu. | needn't |
