## Сквозная логика

1. Уроки 1–7 строят минимальную речь «я/ты здесь и сейчас».
2. Урок 8 проверяет первый самостоятельный день без новой грамматики/лексики.
3. Уроки 9–15 добавляют место, количество, настоящее действие, прошлое и план.
4. Урок 16 проверяет самостоятельный бытовой выходной.
5. Уроки 17–23 переносят базу в поездку, сервис, еду, сравнение, опыт и правила.
6. Урок 24 проверяет восстановление поездки, когда что-то пошло не так.
7. Уроки 25–31 добавляют contrast, narrative, condition, reference и messages.
8. Урок 32 проверяет один независимый день без новых constructs.

Checkpoint lessons 8/16/24/32 не вводят новую grammar operation. Их scored
transfer использует уже введённые grammar/lexical sense IDs. Отдельный полезный
новый lexical sense допустим, только если он word-first grounded и не влияет на
mastery score; retrieval-only также допустим с явной причиной.

## Уроки 01–08 — начать разговор и прожить первый день

### 01. Hello, I’m…

- **Can-do:** поздороваться, назвать себя, страну/язык и завершить короткое
  знакомство.
- **Grammar boundary:** affirmative `I am / I'm` и `you are / you're`; fixed
  greeting/closing frames. Только subject `I/you`.
- **Lexical domains:** greetings, name, a small owner-approved set of countries
  and languages, polite closing.
- **Entry:** PRE_A1, никаких английских prerequisites.
- **Exit:** learner independently produces/understands a short affirmative
  I↔you introduction.
- **Forbidden:** `he/she/it`, `we/they`, negation, questions to `be`,
  `this/that`, articles, possession, lexical-verb grammar.

### 02. Who’s this? What’s that?

- **Can-do:** указать на одного человека/предмет и спросить/сказать, кто или что
  это.
- **Grammar boundary:** singular `this/that`; `is`; `Who is…? / What is…?` and
  contractions; initial `he/she/it` reference only where explicitly grounded.
- **Lexical domains:** familiar people/objects in the immediate scene.
- **Prerequisites:** L01 affirmative copular frame and greeting turn-taking.
- **Forbidden:** plurals `these/those`, possession, `have`, articles as a full
  system, lexical-verb questions.

### 03. My people, my things

- **Can-do:** назвать близкого человека/вещь и показать простую принадлежность.
- **Grammar boundary:** `my/your/his/her/its`; possessive `'s`; singular/plural
  noun recognition; limited `have/has` affirmative only after subjects exist.
- **Lexical domains:** family/close people, everyday personal objects.
- **Prerequisites:** L02 person/object reference, singular `is`, grounded
  `he/she/it`.
- **Forbidden:** `do/does` questions/negation, object pronouns, broad quantifier
  system, relative clauses.

### 04. What I like and want

- **Can-do:** сказать и спросить о простом предпочтении/желании.
- **Grammar boundary:** lexical-verb frame with `I/you`; `like/want`; `do` and
  `don't` for I/you; object slots; `want to + base verb` only as its own later
  operation inside this lesson.
- **Lexical domains:** simple activities, food/drink preferences, immediate
  wants.
- **Prerequisites:** L01 subject frame, L03 familiar nouns/possessives.
- **Forbidden:** third-person `-s`, `does`, routines/frequency, progressive,
  past or future.

### 05. My ordinary day

- **Can-do:** описать несколько обычных действий своего/чужого дня.
- **Grammar boundary:** Present Simple affirmative across known subjects;
  `we/they`; `he/she/it + -s`; selected irregular `have/has`, `do/does` forms.
- **Lexical domains:** wake, eat, work/study, go, come, sleep and daily places.
- **Prerequisites:** L04 lexical-verb frame; L02–03 personal reference.
- **Forbidden:** present continuous, frequency questions, full past, future.

### 06. When and how often?

- **Can-do:** спросить/сказать, когда и как часто происходит знакомое действие.
- **Grammar boundary:** Present Simple questions/negatives across persons;
  `when`, `what time`, `how often`; frequency adverbs/expressions and position;
  basic time prepositions limited to routine contexts.
- **Lexical domains:** clock/calendar basics, days, routine frequency.
- **Prerequisites:** L05 Present Simple affirmative and agreement.
- **Forbidden:** progressive time contrast, past/future time reference,
  duration `for/since`.

### 07. Can you help me?

- **Can-do:** попросить простую помощь, ответить и дать короткую инструкцию.
- **Grammar boundary:** `can/can't` ability; `Can you…?` request; base verb after
  `can`; positive/negative imperative; polite `please` placement.
- **Lexical domains:** common actions, help, simple directions/actions.
- **Prerequisites:** L04–06 base verbs and question turn-taking.
- **Forbidden:** `could/would`, obligation, permission system, conditionals.

### 08. Checkpoint: First day here

- **Can-do:** самостоятельно познакомиться, понять окружение, рассказать о себе
  и попросить помощь в одном коротком сценарии.
- **Grammar:** review/transfer of L01–07 only.
- **Lexicon:** scored transfer uses unlocked senses from L01–07; optional new
  contextual senses stay outside the mastery probe.
- **Forbidden:** any new grammar operation; ungrounded new word inside score.

## Уроки 09–16 — повседневная жизнь, настоящее, прошлое и план

### 09. My home and neighborhood

- **Can-do:** сказать, что есть/где находится дома и рядом.
- **Grammar boundary:** `there is/there are`; basic place prepositions; `Is/Are
  there…?`; limited `some/any` only for existence.
- **Lexical domains:** rooms, furniture, nearby places.
- **Prerequisites:** known `is/are`, singular/plural nouns, basic questions.
- **Forbidden:** quantity questions, non-count system, relative `where` clause.

### 10. At the shop

- **Can-do:** спросить цену, количество и наличие; купить нужное.
- **Grammar boundary:** count/non-count operational distinction; `a/an` in
  buying units; plural quantities; `how much/how many`; `some/any` in
  requests/availability; container/measure frames.
- **Lexical domains:** common goods, numbers/prices, sizes, units.
- **Prerequisites:** L03 noun number, L09 existence/some-any base.
- **Forbidden:** comparatives, superlatives, present perfect.

### 11. What’s happening now?

- **Can-do:** сказать/спросить, что происходит сейчас.
- **Grammar boundary:** Present Continuous affirmative, negative and questions;
  `be + verb-ing`; selected spelling changes only as whole-word forms.
- **Lexical domains:** visible actions and immediate surroundings.
- **Prerequisites:** all forms of `be`, known action verbs.
- **Forbidden:** Simple/Continuous contrast, past continuous, future
  arrangement interpretation.

### 12. Yesterday: where and what

- **Can-do:** сказать, где кто был и что существовало вчера.
- **Grammar boundary:** past `was/were`; questions/negatives; `there was/were`;
  finished-past anchors.
- **Lexical domains:** yesterday locations, simple states/events.
- **Prerequisites:** present `be`, there is/are, time anchors.
- **Forbidden:** lexical-verb Past Simple, `did`, past continuous.

### 13. My weekend story

- **Can-do:** рассказать короткую последовательность законченных действий.
- **Grammar boundary:** Past Simple affirmative; selected regular/irregular
  verbs; `did/didn't` questions/negatives; basic sequence markers.
- **Lexical domains:** weekend activities, movement, social events.
- **Prerequisites:** L12 finished-past time frame; present lexical verbs.
- **Forbidden:** past continuous, Present Perfect, reported speech.

### 14. I don’t feel well

- **Can-do:** назвать простое самочувствие/симптом и понять базовый совет.
- **Grammar boundary:** `have/has` symptoms; `feel + adjective`; `should/
  shouldn't` advice; limited reason `because` after two familiar clauses.
- **Lexical domains:** body, common symptoms, basic self-care.
- **Prerequisites:** Present Simple agreement/questions, adjective predicate.
- **Forbidden:** diagnosis, obligation system, complex cause clauses.

### 15. Let’s make a plan

- **Can-do:** предложить совместное действие и назвать простой будущий план.
- **Grammar boundary:** `Let's + base`; accept/decline frames; `be going to`
  intention; `want/need to + base` as separately explained operations; basic
  future time anchors.
- **Lexical domains:** leisure plans, meeting time/place, next actions.
- **Prerequisites:** `be`, base verbs, want-frame, time/place questions.
- **Forbidden:** `will` as general future, conditionals, future time clauses.

### 16. Checkpoint: Weekend with a friend

- **Can-do:** описать место/покупку/текущее событие/прошлый выходной и
  согласовать следующий план.
- **Grammar:** review/transfer of L09–15 plus prerequisites from L01–07.
- **Lexicon:** scored transfer uses unlocked senses; optional new contextual
  senses stay outside the mastery probe.
- **Forbidden:** new grammar; ungrounded new word inside score.

## Уроки 17–24 — поездка, сервис, еда, сравнение, опыт и правила

### 17. At the station or airport

- **Can-do:** найти путь, платформу/выход и понять короткую инструкцию.
- **Grammar boundary:** movement imperatives; `go/turn/take + place/path`;
  `from/to`; sequence instructions; `Which…?` selection within known choices.
- **Lexical domains:** station/airport zones, tickets, directions, times.
- **Prerequisites:** L07 imperatives/requests, L09 place, L10 numbers.
- **Forbidden:** relative clauses, passive announcements, future conditional.

### 18. Checking in

- **Can-do:** зарегистрироваться, сообщить данные и вежливо попросить нужное.
- **Grammar boundary:** `I'd like…`; `Could you…?` as polite request;
  `I have…` possession/reservation; article/determiner choices limited to service
  nouns; spelling a name is a real-life action, never a letter-assembly task.
- **Lexical domains:** hotel/flight check-in, booking, documents, room/seat.
- **Prerequisites:** L03 have/possession, L07 requests, L10 quantities.
- **Forbidden:** full conditional meaning of `would/could`, passive voice.

### 19. A meal that works for me

- **Can-do:** заказать еду с ограничениями и проверить состав/количество.
- **Grammar boundary:** count/non-count transfer; `a little/a few`; `enough` in
  bounded food frames; `with/without`; preference/restriction clauses using
  already known forms.
- **Lexical domains:** menu, ingredients, allergies/preferences, portions.
- **Prerequisites:** L04 likes/wants, L10 quantity, L14 have/feel.
- **Forbidden:** relative clauses despite lesson title, passive ingredients.

### 20. Which one is better?

- **Can-do:** сравнить знакомые варианты и выбрать подходящий.
- **Grammar boundary:** comparative adjective forms; `more`; irregular
  `better/worse`; `than`; `one/ones`; limited superlative only after comparative
  mastery and as separate operations.
- **Lexical domains:** price, size, speed, comfort, quality.
- **Prerequisites:** adjective predicates, `which`, familiar option nouns.
- **Forbidden:** complex comparison clauses, double comparatives.

### 21. Have you ever…?

- **Can-do:** спросить/сказать о жизненном опыте и уточнить законченный эпизод.
- **Grammar boundary:** Present Perfect experience; `have/has + selected past
  participle`; `ever/never`; short answers; contrast with finished Past Simple
  in a separately planned stage.
- **Lexical domains:** travel/life experiences and common achievements.
- **Prerequisites:** have/has, Past Simple, selected participle forms.
- **Forbidden:** duration `for/since`, full result perfect, perfect continuous.

### 22. Rules and permission

- **Can-do:** понять/выразить разрешение, запрет, обязанность и отсутствие
  обязанности.
- **Grammar boundary:** permission `can/may`; `must/mustn't`; `have to/don't
  have to`; `should` contrast; meanings separated before interleaving.
- **Lexical domains:** public rules, travel/accommodation rules, safety.
- **Prerequisites:** can, Present Simple, advice should.
- **Forbidden:** past modals, deduction, hypothetical permission.

### 23. Keep the conversation going

- **Can-do:** показать интерес, попросить повторить/уточнить и связать короткие
  мысли.
- **Grammar boundary:** follow-up wh-questions; object pronouns; connectors
  `and/but/because/so`; short echo/clarification frames; refer back without
  introducing relative clauses.
- **Lexical domains:** conversation management, opinions, reactions.
- **Prerequisites:** established question families and simple clauses.
- **Forbidden:** question tags as a full system, reported speech, relative
  clauses.

### 24. Checkpoint: Travel day goes wrong

- **Can-do:** восстановить поездку: найти путь, зарегистрироваться, выбрать,
  объяснить ограничение, понять правило и поддержать разговор.
- **Grammar:** review/transfer of L17–23 plus relevant prior constructs.
- **Lexicon:** scored transfer uses unlocked senses; optional new contextual
  senses stay outside the mastery probe.
- **Forbidden:** new grammar; ungrounded new word inside score.

## Уроки 25–32 — связный рассказ и самостоятельное решение задач

### 25. My work or study day

- **Can-do:** различить обычный распорядок и временную ситуацию сейчас/на этой
  неделе.
- **Grammar boundary:** Present Simple vs Present Continuous meaning contrast;
  stative limits only for the required high-frequency verbs; present continuous
  future arrangement only after the core contrast and as separate operation.
- **Lexical domains:** work/study tasks, schedules, temporary projects.
- **Prerequisites:** L05–06 Present Simple; L11 Present Continuous.
- **Forbidden:** past continuous, broad stative-verb taxonomy.

### 26. What happened while…?

- **Can-do:** описать фоновое действие и событие, которое его прервало.
- **Grammar boundary:** Past Continuous; `while` background; `when` event;
  Past Continuous vs Past Simple contrast.
- **Lexical domains:** everyday incidents, travel/work interruptions.
- **Prerequisites:** L11 continuous frame, L13 Past Simple.
- **Forbidden:** Past Perfect, complex narrative subordination.

### 27. Solve a service problem

- **Can-do:** ясно назвать проблему, желаемое исправление и ответить на решение.
- **Grammar boundary:** `doesn't work/isn't working` contrast in context;
  `too + adjective` and `not + adjective + enough`; `need + noun / need to +
  verb`; polite request reuse with problem–solution frames.
- **Lexical domains:** broken/missing/wrong service items, refund/change/help.
- **Prerequisites:** present contrasts, service requests, comparison language.
- **Forbidden:** causative `have/get something done`, passive complaint system.

### 28. If this happens…

- **Can-do:** назвать реальное условие и практический результат/действие.
- **Grammar boundary:** zero conditional for stable instructions only where
  useful; first conditional `if + present, will/can/imperative`; clause order
  and punctuation as separate form detail.
- **Lexical domains:** contingencies, delays, safety, plans and alternatives.
- **Prerequisites:** Present Simple, will-function taught inside this lesson,
  can/imperatives, future plans.
- **Forbidden:** second/third/mixed conditionals; `will` inside the if-clause.

### 29. The person or place I mean

- **Can-do:** уточнить, какого человека/предмет/место имеет в виду говорящий.
- **Grammar boundary:** defining relative clauses with `who/that/where`;
  subject relatives first; object omission only if separately planned and
  inside scope.
- **Lexical domains:** people, roles, places and distinguishing features.
- **Prerequisites:** two-clause comprehension, reference words, known nouns.
- **Forbidden:** non-defining relatives, whose/whom system, reduced relatives.

### 30. Messages and what people said

- **Can-do:** передать содержание короткого сообщения/просьбы без претензии на
  дословную цитату.
- **Grammar boundary:** `say/tell` complement frames; reported present/past
  meaning in a deliberately bounded set; reported requests with familiar
  infinitive frame only after explanation.
- **Lexical domains:** calls, texts, plans, delays, requests.
- **Prerequisites:** simple clauses, object pronouns, Past Simple, `to + base`.
- **Forbidden:** full tense backshift table, reported questions system,
  conditionals inside reports unless already known.

### 31. My story and next step

- **Can-do:** связно представить себя, рассказать о прошлом опыте/изменении и
  назвать следующий реалистичный шаг.
- **Grammar boundary:** narrative sequencing and reference using only taught
  tenses; controlled choice between Past Simple, Present Perfect experience and
  future plan; discourse connectors/retrieval rather than a new tense system.
- **Lexical domains:** personal milestones, learning/work/travel goals.
- **Prerequisites:** all narrative/future operations through L30.
- **Forbidden:** new tense/aspect, hypothetical autobiography, unsupported
  complex clauses.

### 32. Final Checkpoint: One independent day

- **Can-do:** пройти один самостоятельный день: общаться, ориентироваться,
  решать бытовые/поездочные проблемы, рассказывать и планировать.
- **Grammar:** cumulative retrieval/transfer of owner-approved course graph.
- **Lexicon:** scored transfer uses unlocked core senses; optional new
  contextual senses stay outside the mastery probe.
- **Forbidden:** any new grammar operation; ungrounded new word inside score.

- ни один construct не появляется раньше prerequisite;
- каждый construct имеет только одно introduction место;
- checkpoint lessons не вводят новую grammar operation; их scored probe не

- урок 1 сохраняет жёсткую owner boundary;

- каждая глава показывает регулярный lexical growth и запланированное
  закрепление, но не механическую квоту новых слов в каждой session;

  систему;
- owner manually reviews the full 224-chapter / 1 792-packet projection before
