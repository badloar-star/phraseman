# Theory · Lesson 1 of the new course — «I am, you are, it is» (EN)

> Session «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Same sections (01–14), English phrases and drills as
> `lesson_01_ru.md`. This is for learners who use the app in English: their own language is
> unknown, so the text is in very plain English (short words, short sentences), and the traps are
> the ones most learners make whatever their language: dropping am, I is / you is, Is cold,
> I no cold. The second column of examples explains the meaning in plain English instead of a
> translation. Needs a native speaker's read-through before release.

**Screen title:** Theory · I am, you are, it is

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
In this lesson you learn to talk about yourself, the person in front of you, other people and things: «I am ready», «you are right», «she is upstairs», «it is heavy». Every one of these sentences has a small word in the middle: am, is or are.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am ready. | You can start now. | am |
| You are right. | You agree with the other person. | are |
| She is upstairs. | She is on a higher floor. | is |
| It is heavy. | It is hard to lift. | is |

**tip**
Think of am, is and are as clips. They don't add meaning, but without them the sentence falls apart.

---

## 02 · The main pattern *(open at start)*

**body**
Almost every sentence in this lesson is built the same way: first who or what, then am, is or are, then a word about them: where they are, what they are like, how they feel.

**formula**
`who / what` · `am / is / are` · `where, what like, how`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am here. | I have arrived. | am |
| You are kind. | You are nice to people. | are |
| He is tall. | He is a big person. | is |
| It is open. | You can go in. | is |
| We are together. | We came as one group. | are |
| They are asleep. | They are sleeping. | are |

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `I ___ ready.`
- options: `am` · `is` · `are`
- answer: `am`
- why: When you talk about yourself, I always goes with am.

## 03 · I am — when you talk about yourself

**body**
About yourself, it is always I am. In many languages you can say «I tired» or just «tired». In English that sounds broken. Say the full thing: I am tired.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am here. | I have arrived. | am |
| I am tired. | I need a rest. | am |
| I am lost. | I don't know where I am. | am |
| I am new. | It is my first day here. | am |
| I am home. | I got back to my home. | am |
| I am done. | I have finished. | am |

**fix**
- ~~I tired.~~ → I am tired.
- ~~Am here.~~ → I am here.

**tip**
I is always a capital letter, even in the middle of a sentence.

---

## 04 · How to say «not»

**body**
To say «not», put not right after am, is or are: I am not hungry. Don't use no here. I no cold sounds broken; I am not cold is fine.

**formula**
`who` · `am / is / are` · `not` · `where, what like, how`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am not hungry. | I don't want to eat. | not |
| I am not cold. | The room is fine for me. | not |
| I am not sure. | Maybe, maybe not. | not |
| You are not lazy. | You work hard. | not |
| It is not heavy. | It is easy to lift. | not |

**fix**
- ~~I no cold.~~ → I am not cold.
- ~~I not hungry.~~ → I am not hungry.

**drill · spot_slip**
- tiles: `I` · `am` · `no` · `cold`
- wrong: `no` (index 2)
- hint: One word is wrong here. Tap it.
- explanation: After am you need not, not no: I am not cold.

---

## 05 · You are — when you talk to someone

**body**
When you tell a person something about them, use you are. English has only one you: for a friend, for your boss, and for a whole group. After you, it is always are.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| You are right. | I agree with you. | are |
| You are fast. | You did it quickly. | are |
| You are safe. | Nothing bad can happen now. | are |
| You are not slow. | You are quick enough. | not |
| You are early. | You came before the time. | are |

**fix**
- ~~You is right.~~ → You are right.
- ~~You am kind.~~ → You are kind.

**drill · binary**
- question: You want to tell someone they are right. Which is correct?
- A: `You is right.`
- B: `You are right.`
- correct: B
- explanation: After you it is always are, for one person or many.

---

## 06 · He is, she is — when you talk about another person

**body**
For a man, say he. For a woman, say she. After both, use is. You use them when you talk about a person, not to them: «he is tall», «she is upstairs».

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| He is tall. | He is a big person. | is |
| She is upstairs. | She is on a higher floor. | is |
| He is strict. | He wants rules followed. | is |
| She is busy. | She has no time now. | is |
| He is not angry. | He is calm. | not |

**fix**
- ~~She are busy.~~ → She is busy.
- ~~He am tall.~~ → He is tall.

**tip**
You are kind — you say it to the person. He is kind — you tell someone else about him.

---

## 07 · It is — for a thing, a place or the weather

**body**
For a thing, a room, a door or the weather, use it is. Many languages just say «Cold» or «Open». English needs a start: It is cold, It is open. Is cold on its own is not a full sentence.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It is open. | You can go in. | is |
| It is heavy. | It is hard to lift. | is |
| It is crowded. | There are lots of people. | is |
| It is sunny. | The sun is out. | is |
| It is closed. | You can't go in. | is |
| It is not heavy. | It is easy to lift. | not |

**fix**
- ~~Is cold.~~ → It is cold.
- ~~It are heavy.~~ → It is heavy.

---

## 08 · We are, they are — for more than one

**body**
For you and other people together, say we are. For other people or for several things, say they are. After we and they it is always are, however many there are.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We are together. | We came as one group. | are |
| We are late. | We should be there already. | are |
| They are asleep. | They are sleeping. | are |
| They are nearby. | They are close to us. | are |
| They are tight. | The new shoes are too small. | are |

**fix**
- ~~We is late.~~ → We are late.
- ~~They is asleep.~~ → They are asleep.

**tip**
They is for people and for things. You can say They are tight about new shoes.

## 09 · Who — and which clip goes with them

**body**
The whole lesson fits into one short table. If you're not sure, first find who the sentence is about, then take their clip.

**examples** 🔊
| en | example |
|---|---|
| I → am | I am ready. |
| you → are | You are right. |
| he / she → is | She is busy. |
| it → is | It is open. |
| we → are | We are together. |
| they → are | They are asleep. |

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `They ___ nearby.`
- options: `am` · `is` · `are`
- answer: `are`
- why: After they it is always are.

**drill · word_bank**
- task: She isn't downstairs. Say where she is.
- answer: `She` · `is` · `upstairs`
- slot labels: who · clip · where
- extra tiles: `are` · `am`

## 10 · Short forms: I'm, you're, it's

**body**
When people speak, they almost always shorten the clip: I'm for I am, it's for it is. Same meaning, just faster. The ' shows where letters are missing.

**examples** 🔊
| en | full form | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I'm shy. | I am shy. | I'm |
| You're great. | You are great. | You're |
| He's stuck. | He is stuck. | He's |
| She's upstairs. | She is upstairs. | She's |
| It's locked. | It is locked. | It's |
| We're soaked. | We are soaked. | We're |
| They're tight. | They are tight. | They're |

**tip**
Both forms are correct. The short one sounds more natural when you talk; the full one sounds firmer.

---

## 11 · Where you are: home, inside, upstairs — no extra words

**body**
The clip also works for places: I am home, she is upstairs. With here, home, back, inside, outside, upstairs and nearby you don't need any other word. I am in home sounds wrong.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am home. | I got back to my home. | am |
| I am back. | I have returned. | am |
| We are inside. | We went in already. | are |
| She is upstairs. | She is on a higher floor. | is |
| They are nearby. | They are close to us. | are |

**fix**
- ~~I am in home.~~ → I am home.
- ~~She is in upstairs.~~ → She is upstairs.

---

## 12 · Feelings use am, is, are too

**body**
In many languages you «have» cold or hunger, or the cold is «to you». English just uses the clip: I am cold, I am hungry, I am sorry. Learn these as whole phrases.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am cold. | I want a warm jacket. | am |
| I am hot. | I want to open the window. | am |
| I am hungry. | I want to eat. | am |
| I am late. | I should be there already. | am |
| I am sorry. | I apologise. | am |
| They are asleep. | They are sleeping. | are |

**fix**
- ~~I have cold.~~ → I am cold.
- ~~I have hungry.~~ → I am hungry.

---

## 13 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I tired.~~ → I am tired.
- ~~I no cold.~~ → I am not cold.
- ~~I is ready.~~ → I am ready.
- ~~You is right.~~ → You are right.
- ~~She are busy.~~ → She is busy.
- ~~Is cold.~~ → It is cold.
- ~~We is late.~~ → We are late.

**tip**
Almost all of these come down to two things: the clip is missing, or it's the wrong one. Check: who is the sentence about, and which clip goes with them?

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `We ___ not late.`
- options: `am` · `is` · `are`
- answer: `are`
- why: After we it is always are, and not doesn't change that.

---

## 14 · What to take into practice

**body**
One pattern for the whole lesson: who + am / is / are + a word about them. Never skip the clip. To say «not», put not after it. When you talk, shorten it: I'm, it's, they're.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am ready. | You can start now. | am |
| She is not angry. | She is calm. | not |
| It's locked. | You need a key. | It's |
| They're tight. | The shoes are too small. | They're |
