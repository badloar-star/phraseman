# Draft: preposition_time_place

## Scope

One concrete mistake type: the learner chooses `in`, `on`, or `at` by direct
translation of "в/на" instead of first deciding whether the phrase is about
place or time.

## User-Facing Diagnosis

The learner is not failing all prepositions. The repeated trap is smaller:
they see one native-language word and try to map it to one English word.
This training repairs that by teaching a short decision move:

1. Is this place or time?
2. If place: inside, surface, or point?
3. If time: period, day/date, or exact time?

## Beginner Standard

- No grammar lecture.
- No unexplained English grammar terms in RU/UK learner text.
- Examples stay concrete: `in the room`, `on Monday`, `at 8`.
- Every wrong answer has specific feedback tied to that chosen option.
- Tone stays calm: "почти", "ловушка", "готовый блок".

## Exercise Arc

1. Easy place pictures: room, table, door.
2. Time contrast: Monday, 8, 1990.
3. Place/time mixed review: room vs Monday, table vs 8, full sentence.
