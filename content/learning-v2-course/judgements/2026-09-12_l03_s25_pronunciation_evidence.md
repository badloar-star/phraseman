## Grammar boundary

S25 introduces `this` for one nearby object in the present scene. Cambridge says this/that are singular and this/these most commonly point to things close to the speaker; this also has discourse/time/emotional uses and can modify uncountables. Scope the explanation to this concrete count-object use, never “always/only nearby”. In `This is a phone`, `this` is a pronoun and `a phone` the predicate noun phrase; do not imply `this a phone`.

[Cambridge demonstratives](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/this-that-these-).

Rows26–29 reserve `that`, `Is this/that…?` and the `it/this` contrast. S25 does not teach/target `that`, `these`, `those` or `Is this?`. Its planned builders remain `This is a phone.`, `It is a charger.`, `They are not cups.`, `This is a cable.`

## Listening triples

- **phone / bone / cone**: UK /fəʊn bəʊn kəʊn/, US /foʊn boʊn koʊn/. Shared rhyme; positive initial cues: /f/ continuous airflow between lower lip and upper teeth; /b/ both lips close and release, with voicing; /k/ back of tongue closes against soft palate and releases. Phone: телефон; bone: кость / кістка; cone: конус. [phone pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/phone), [bone pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/bone), [cone pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/cone), [cone meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/cone), [bone meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/bone), [phone meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/phone).
- **charger / charmer / larger**: charger UK /ˈtʃɑː.dʒər/, US /ˈtʃɑːr.dʒɚ/; charmer UK /ˈtʃɑː.mər/, US /ˈtʃɑːr.mɚ/. Larger is the ordinary comparative adjective, not a noun: expected UK /ˈlɑː.dʒə(r)/, US /ˈlɑːr.dʒɚ/, derived from documented large plus comparative. Charger starts /tʃ/ and has a medial voiced affricate /dʒ/: closure then voiced noisy release. Charmer shares the beginning but its medial /m/ closes both lips with nasal airflow. Larger starts /l/: tongue contact near alveolar ridge with air around the sides. No r-only contrast. Charger: зарядное устройство / зарядний пристрій; charmer: обаятельный человек / приваблива людина; larger: более крупный / більший. [charger pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/charger), [charmer pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/charmer), [Oxford large and comparative larger](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/large_1), [Cambridge large](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/large), [charger meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/charger), [charmer meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/charmer).
- **cable / table / label**: /ˈkeɪ.bəl ˈteɪ.bəl ˈleɪ.bəl/ in UK/US references; initial /k t l/. Cable: back-tongue closure/release. Table: tongue-tip/alveolar closure/release. Label: tongue contact with side airflow. Cable means covered wire(s), кабель; table: стол / стіл; label: attached information tag, этикетка / етикетка.
