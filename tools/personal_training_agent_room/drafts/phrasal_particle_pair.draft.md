# phrasal_particle_pair

Jesse rework pass for phrasal pairs where the learner chooses the second part by sound instead of meaning.

## Scope

- One microdiagnosis only: `phrasal_particle_pair`.
- Replace mojibake-heavy legacy copy.
- Keep the lesson focused on ready-made pairs, not isolated words.

## Teaching Point

The learner should choose the full pair by meaning:

1. `turn on` = switch on
2. `turn off` = switch off
3. `look up` = find information
4. `look for` = search for someone or something
5. `pick up` = lift or collect a person
6. `give up` = stop trying
7. `find out` = discover information
8. `run out of` = have none left

## Copy Rules

- Visible RU/UK copy uses ASCII transliteration.
- Avoid raw internal grammar labels in learner-facing copy.
- Keep the message simple: first choose meaning, then choose the pair.

## Structure

- Easy: turn on/off, look up/for.
- Contrast: pick up, give up, find out, run out of.
- Mixed: put on, take off, carry on, fill in.
