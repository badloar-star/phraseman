# Lingman Topic Pitch 001 - English Prepositions

## Routing Snapshot

```text
ROUTE:
ideas before full script

REASON:
The user asked for a full script about English prepositions, but "prepositions" is too broad for one strong 10-minute video.

ACTIVE PROTOCOLS:
Topic Pitch Gate / high-confusion grammar / Phraseman conversion

MAIN RISK:
Writing a broad lesson that promises too much and teaches too little.
```

## Top 3 Recommended First

```text
1. IN / ON / AT Are Not "В / НА / У"
Best first video because it has the strongest pain recognition for Russian-speaking learners.

2. TO vs FOR: The English Preposition That Breaks Russian Logic
Best second video because it teaches direction vs benefit, a very repeatable mental model.

3. Good AT, Interested IN, Afraid OF
Best for search and saves because adjective + preposition combinations are painful and practical.
```

## 10 Topic Angles

### 1. IN / ON / AT Are Not "В / НА / У"

```text
Title direction:
IN / ON / AT Are Not "В / НА / У"

Thumbnail:
В / НА / У? NO.

Learning promise:
Teach the camera model: inside, surface/system/day, exact point.

Phraseman fit:
Strong. Practice chunks like in the morning, on Monday, at 5, in the car, on the bus.

Risk:
Must not promise all prepositions.
```

### 2. TO vs FOR: Direction Or Benefit?

```text
Title direction:
TO vs FOR Finally Makes Sense

Thumbnail:
TO -> / FOR = benefit

Learning promise:
TO = direction/target. FOR = benefit/purpose.

Phraseman fit:
Strong. Practice phrase pairs: give it to me / do it for me.

Risk:
Needs careful examples because both can appear near people.
```

### 3. Good AT, Interested IN, Afraid OF

```text
Title direction:
Why English Says Good AT But Interested IN

Thumbnail:
GOOD IN? NO.

Learning promise:
Teach adjective + preposition chunks as phrase patterns, not logic puzzles.

Phraseman fit:
Very strong. Perfect for flashcards and mistake trainer.

Risk:
Can become a list unless grouped by emotion, skill, and reaction.
```

### 4. FOR vs SINCE: Stop Translating "Уже"

```text
Title direction:
FOR vs SINCE: The "Уже" Trap

Thumbnail:
FOR 3 YEARS / SINCE 2021

Learning promise:
FOR = duration. SINCE = starting point.

Phraseman fit:
Strong. Practice paired phrases with Present Perfect.

Risk:
Needs tense support, not only prepositions.
```

### 5. BY vs WITH: Method Or Tool?

```text
Title direction:
BY vs WITH: Why "By My Hands" Sounds Wrong

Thumbnail:
BY? WITH?

Learning promise:
BY = method/process. WITH = tool/object/person.

Phraseman fit:
Solid. Practice work and daily phrases.

Risk:
Some examples overlap; needs scope fence.
```

### 6. AT School vs IN School vs AT Work

```text
Title direction:
AT School or IN School? English Location Logic

Thumbnail:
AT school?

Learning promise:
Teach institution vs physical place vs student status.

Phraseman fit:
Strong. Useful common chunks.

Risk:
US/UK differences must be named carefully.
```

### 7. ON The Internet, IN The App, AT The Website?

```text
Title direction:
Prepositions For Online Life

Thumbnail:
ON INTERNET?

Learning promise:
Teach online platforms, apps, websites, chats, pages.

Phraseman fit:
Strong for modern phrases and work messages.

Risk:
Needs naturalness more than grammar theory.
```

### 8. ABOUT / OF / FROM: Three Ways To Connect Ideas

```text
Title direction:
ABOUT vs OF vs FROM: Stop Guessing

Thumbnail:
ABOUT? OF? FROM?

Learning promise:
ABOUT = topic. OF = connection/part. FROM = origin/source.

Phraseman fit:
Solid. Practice short message chunks.

Risk:
Could get abstract; needs many concrete phrases.
```

### 9. Prepositions After Verbs: Listen TO, Wait FOR, Depend ON

```text
Title direction:
Listen TO, Wait FOR, Depend ON: Why?

Thumbnail:
LISTEN MUSIC? NO.

Learning promise:
Teach verb + preposition chunks and why direct Russian translation fails.

Phraseman fit:
Very strong. Ideal for chunk practice.

Risk:
Must not become a 50-item list.
```

### 10. Prepositions In Work Messages

```text
Title direction:
English Prepositions That Make Work Messages Sound Natural

Thumbnail:
IN / ON / BY?

Learning promise:
Use prepositions in deadlines, meetings, follow-ups, documents.

Phraseman fit:
Very strong. Full-message practice mission.

Risk:
Needs workplace-message protocol, not grammar-only structure.
```

## Recommended First Script

```text
Start with:
IN / ON / AT Are Not "В / НА / У"

Reason:
It is the strongest gateway episode. It fixes a high-pain Russian translation trap, has a clean visual model, strong title/thumbnail, natural Phraseman practice gap, and can become episode 1 of a preposition series.
```
