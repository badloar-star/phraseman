# Theory · Lesson 13 of the new course — «Did you…? I didn't. First of all…» (EN)

> Session «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Same sections (01–11), English phrases and drills as
> `lesson_13_ru.md`. Plain English for learners whose own language is not in the app. Common traps:
> You cleaned the kitchen? (no Did), Did she tried…?, I not bought / I didn't bought, Did you
> slowed down?, run out milk, At the end (for «finally»). The second column explains the meaning
> instead of translating. Needs a native speaker's read-through.

**Screen title:** Theory · Questions and «not» about the past

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
In lesson 12 you said what you did: I cleaned, I went. Now you'll learn to ask about the past — «did you clean the kitchen?» —, to say «not» — «I didn't buy the shoes» — and to tell a story in order: «first of all… then… in the end».

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did you clean the kitchen? | You ask if the kitchen is clean now. | Did |
| I didn't buy the shoes. | I left the shoes in the shop. | didn't |
| First of all, I checked the address. | The first thing I did was check it. | First of all |
| In the end, I took the bus. | After everything, I took the bus. | In the end |

---

## 02 · Questions about the past: Did…? *(open at start)*

**body**
Make the question like in lesson 7, but use Did instead of Do or Does — and Did is the same for everyone: Did you…? Did she…? After Did, the action is as in the dictionary, with no -ed: Did you clean, not Did you cleaned.

**formula**
`Did` · `who` · `action as in the dictionary` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did you clean the kitchen? | You ask if the kitchen is clean now. | Did |
| Did you wash the coffee mug? | You ask if the mug is clean. | Did |
| Did she wash the tea strainer? | You ask if she cleaned the tea strainer. | Did |
| Did you try a fig? | You ask if they ate a fig. | Did |
| Did you go sightseeing? | You ask if they visited famous places. | Did |

**fix**
- ~~You cleaned the kitchen?~~ → Did you clean the kitchen?
- ~~Did she tried a blackberry?~~ → Did she try a blackberry?
- ~~Does she wash the tea strainer yesterday?~~ → Did she wash the tea strainer yesterday?

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `Did she ___ a blackberry?`
- options: `try` · `tried` · `tries`
- answer: `try`
- why: Did already shows the past, so the action is as in the dictionary.

## 03 · «Not» in the past: didn't

**body**
To say «not» about the past, put didn't before the action — the same for everyone. Again, the action is as in the dictionary: I didn't buy, not I didn't bought. Only did shows the past.

**formula**
`who` · `didn't` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I didn't buy the shoes. | I left the shoes in the shop. | didn't |
| I didn't lie down. | I stayed sitting or standing. | didn't |
| I didn't stay in. | I went out. | didn't |
| I didn't come back early. | I came back late. | didn't |
| I didn't fill in the form. | The form is still empty. | didn't |

**fix**
- ~~I didn't bought the shoes.~~ → I didn't buy the shoes.
- ~~I not bought the shoes.~~ → I didn't buy the shoes.
- ~~I don't buy the shoes yesterday.~~ → I didn't buy the shoes yesterday.

**drill · spot_slip**
- tiles: `I` · `didn't` · `bought` · `the` · `shoes`
- wrong: `bought` (index 2)
- hint: One word is in the wrong form. Tap it.
- explanation: After didn't, the action is as in the dictionary: I didn't buy the shoes.

## 04 · Show the past only once

**body**
The main rule of the lesson: show the past in one place only. Either in the action (I cleaned) or in did (Did you clean? I didn't clean). Not twice: Did you cleaned is wrong.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I cleaned the kitchen. | The past is in «cleaned». | cleaned |
| Did you clean the kitchen? | The past is in «Did». | Did |
| I didn't speed up. | The past is in «didn't». | didn't |
| Did you slow down? | The past is in «Did». | Did |

**fix**
- ~~Did you slowed down?~~ → Did you slow down?
- ~~Did she spoke kindly?~~ → Did she speak kindly?

**drill · binary**
- question: You ask your friend if they drove more slowly. Which is correct?
- A: `Did you slowed down?`
- B: `Did you slow down?`
- correct: B
- explanation: The past is already in Did, so slow has no -ed.

## 05 · Two-word actions

**body**
Many English actions are two words: pick up, put away, run out of, slow down. The second word changes the meaning, so you can't drop it.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did you pick up the parcel? | Did you collect the parcel? | pick up |
| Did you put away the clothes? | Did you put the clothes in their place? | put away |
| Did you run out of milk? | Is there no milk left? | run out of |
| I didn't give back the book. | I still have the book. | give back |
| I didn't fall over. | I stayed on my feet. | fall over |

**fix**
- ~~Did you pick the parcel?~~ *(meaning «collect»)* → Did you pick up the parcel?
- ~~Did you run out milk?~~ → Did you run out of milk?

**tip**
Learn these actions as a whole — both words together, like one new word.

## 06 · When: yesterday, afterwards, beforehand

**body**
Questions and «not» about the past often come with a time: yesterday, afterwards (after that), beforehand (before, early). These words go at the end.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did you call yesterday? | Was your call yesterday? | yesterday |
| Did you call afterwards? | Did you call after that? | afterwards |
| Did you buy the tickets beforehand? | Did you buy them early, before the day? | beforehand |
| Did you move in last week? | Did you start living there last week? | last week |

**fix**
- ~~Did you buy beforehand the tickets?~~ → Did you buy the tickets beforehand?

---

## 07 · Feeling words: definitely, luckily

**body**
To show you're sure or how you feel: definitely, certainly, luckily. definitely and certainly go before the action; luckily goes at the start.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I definitely called yesterday. | I'm sure I called yesterday. | definitely |
| I certainly paid yesterday. | Of course I paid yesterday. | certainly |
| Luckily, I caught the bus. | I was lucky — I got on the bus in time. | Luckily |

**fix**
- ~~I called definitely yesterday.~~ → I definitely called yesterday.

---

## 08 · In order: First of all, Then, In the end

**body**
To tell a story step by step: First of all, At first, Then, In the end. They go at the start. Careful: for «finally, after everything», say In the end, not At the end.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| First of all, I checked the address. | The first thing I did was check it. | First of all |
| At first, I waited outside. | In the beginning, I waited outside. | At first |
| Then we stopped off at a café. | After that we visited a café for a short time. | Then |
| In the end, I took the bus. | After everything, I took the bus. | In the end |

**fix**
- ~~At the end, I took the bus.~~ *(meaning «finally»)* → In the end, I took the bus.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `___, I took the bus.` *(after trying everything else)*
- options: `At first` · `In the end` · `First of all`
- answer: `In the end`
- why: «Finally, after everything» is In the end.

---

## 09 · «How» it was done: politely, patiently

**body**
Words ending in -ly say how something was done: politely, patiently, kindly. They go after the action and the thing: Did she wait patiently?

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did she ask politely? | Did she ask in a nice, polite way? | politely |
| Did she wait patiently? | Did she wait calmly, without getting angry? | patiently |
| Did she speak kindly? | Did she speak in a kind way? | kindly |

**fix**
- ~~Did she waited patiently?~~ → Did she wait patiently?
- ~~Did she ask polite?~~ → Did she ask politely?

---

## 10 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~Did she tried a blackberry?~~ → Did she try a blackberry?
- ~~You cleaned the kitchen?~~ → Did you clean the kitchen?
- ~~I didn't bought the shoes.~~ → I didn't buy the shoes.
- ~~I not bought the shoes.~~ → I didn't buy the shoes.
- ~~Did you run out milk?~~ → Did you run out of milk?
- ~~At the end, I took the bus.~~ → In the end, I took the bus.

**tip**
If there's did or didn't, the action is as in the dictionary. If there's no did, the action has -ed or is a special word. Show the past only once.

---

## 11 · What to take into practice

**body**
Questions about the past: Did + who + action as in the dictionary. «Not»: didn't + action as in the dictionary. Two-word actions: learn them whole. Telling a story: First of all, Then, In the end.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did you try a nectarine? | You ask if they ate a nectarine. | Did |
| I didn't save up for a bike. | I didn't keep money for a bike. | didn't |
| Did you set off early? | Did you start your trip early? | set off |
| In the end, I took the bus. | After everything, I took the bus. | In the end |
