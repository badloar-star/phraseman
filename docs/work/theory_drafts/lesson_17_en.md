**Screen title:** Theory · How many, how much

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open from the start)*

**body**
This lesson is about amounts: how many nuts you need, a little free time you have, too little privacy, two bags of rice. The key question is always: can you count it one by one?

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| How many pecans do we need? | asking a number | many |
| We have a little leisure time. | some free time, not much | a little |
| We have little privacy. | not enough privacy | little |
| We need two bags of rice. | rice counted in bags | bags of |

---

## 02 · Things you count and things you don't *(open from the start)*

**body**
Some things you count one by one: a bookend, two ottomans — a or an for one, -s after two. Others are a mass or a group word: crockery, gravel, housework. They take no a and no -s.

**formula**
`one: a / an + thing` · `more: two + thing + s` · `mass: no a, no -s`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This is a bookend. | one item | a |
| There are two ottomans in the room. | two items | ottomans |
| This crockery is new. | all the plates and cups | crockery |
| This is an errand. | one small job to do | an |
| This housework is tiring. | cleaning and cooking at home | housework |

**fix**
- ~~This is bookend.~~ → This is a bookend.
- ~~There are two ottoman in the room.~~ → There are two ottomans in the room.
- ~~There are a two ottomans in the room.~~ → There are two ottomans in the room.

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `There are two ___ in the bowl.`
- options: `peppercorns` · `peppercorn` · `a peppercorns`
- answer: `peppercorns`
- why: You count peppercorns: -s after two, and no a.

## 03 · many or much — “how many? how much?”

**body**
Things you count — many: How many pecans? Things you don't — much: How much polenta? With not too: We don't have much semolina. Never put a before many or much.

**formula**
`count: How many + thing + s?` · `mass: How much + thing?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| How many pecans do we need? | a number of nuts | many |
| How much polenta do we need? | an amount, not pieces | much |
| We don't have much semolina. | only a small amount | much |
| How many commuters use this train? | a number of people | many |
| How much congestion is there today? | how bad the traffic is | much |

**fix**
- ~~How much pecans do we need?~~ → How many pecans do we need?
- ~~How many polenta do we need?~~ → How much polenta do we need?
- ~~How a many pecans do we need?~~ → How many pecans do we need?

**drill · binary**
- question: You are counting course assignments — you can count them one by one. How do you ask?
- A: `How much assignments do we have?`
- B: `How many assignments do we have?`
- correct: B
- explain: You count assignments: many.

## 04 · a few, a little — “there is some”

**body**
Not much, but there is some: a few for things you count, a little for things you don't. We have a few housemates. We have a little leisure time.

**formula**
`count: a few + thing + s` · `mass: a little + thing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We have a few housemates. | some people live with us | a few |
| We have a little leisure time. | some free time | a little |
| We received a few compliments. | some nice words | a few |
| We have a little quinoa. | some is left | a little |

**fix**
- ~~We have a little housemates.~~ → We have a few housemates.
- ~~We have a few leisure time.~~ → We have a little leisure time.

---

## 05 · few, little — “not enough”

**body**
Take away a and the meaning changes: less than you would like. We have little privacy. I have few contact numbers. a few / a little — there is some; few / little — not enough.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We have little privacy. | not enough time alone | little |
| We received little encouragement. | almost no support | little |
| I have few contact numbers. | not many numbers | few |
| We have little buckwheat left. | almost none left | little |
| I received a few voice messages. | some messages came | a few |

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `We have ___ floor space.` *(there is almost no room on the floor for guests)*
- options: `little` · `a little` · `few`
- answer: `little`
- why: Mass and not enough: little, no a.

## 06 · some and any

**body**
In a normal sentence use some: We sent some job applications. With not and in questions use any: We don't have any contact details; Do you need any childcare? Both work for things you count and things you don't.

**formula**
`normal: some` · `with not: not … any` · `question: any`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We sent some job applications. | we applied for jobs | some |
| We don't have any contact details. | no phone, no email | any |
| Do you need any childcare? | someone to look after children | any |
| There aren't any service charges. | nothing extra to pay | any |
| Is there any public parking here? | a place anyone can park | any |

**fix**
- ~~We sent any job applications.~~ → We sent some job applications.
- ~~We don't have some contact details.~~ → We don't have any contact details.
- ~~We sent a job applications.~~ → We sent some job applications.

## 07 · Two bags, two litres: two bags of…

**body**
You cannot count a mass in pieces, so count the packs and measures: two bags of rice, two litres of milk, two pieces of advice. The pack takes -s, and of always comes between the pack and the food.

**formula**
`two` · `pack / measure + s` · `of` · `what`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We need two tubes of toothpaste. | two tubes | tubes of |
| We need two bars of soap. | two pieces of soap | bars of |
| We need two bags of rice. | two bags | bags of |
| We need two litres of milk. | a measure | litres of |
| I need two pieces of advice. | advice cannot take -s | pieces of |

**fix**
- ~~We need two tube of toothpaste.~~ → We need two tubes of toothpaste.
- ~~We need two tubes toothpaste.~~ → We need two tubes of toothpaste.

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Say that you need two jars of jam.
- answer: `We` · `need` · `two` · `jars` · `of` · `jam`
- slot labels: none (long phrase)
- extra tiles: `jar` · `much`

## 08 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~This is bookend.~~ → This is a bookend.
- ~~How much pecans do we need?~~ → How many pecans do we need?
- ~~We have a few leisure time.~~ → We have a little leisure time.
- ~~We don't have some contact details.~~ → We don't have any contact details.
- ~~We need two tubes toothpaste.~~ → We need two tubes of toothpaste.

**tip**
First ask: can I count it one by one? Yes — a, -s, many, a few. No — no a, no -s, much, a little, and to count it, a pack with of.

## 09 · What to take into practice

**body**
Count — many, a few, few. Mass — much, a little, little. With a — there is some; without a — not enough. Some in normal sentences, any with not and in questions. Pack + s + of.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| How much knowledge do we need? | an amount | much |
| I need a little personal space. | some time alone | a little |
| We need two slices of cheese. | two thin pieces | slices of |
