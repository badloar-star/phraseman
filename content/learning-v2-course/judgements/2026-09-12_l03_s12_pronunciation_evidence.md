# L3S12 pronunciation preparation

Independent read-only Sol medium reviewer /root/s10_sound_evidence. Cambridge primary sources, bounded initial search plus explicit vase refinement. No S12 learner source authored; no actual audio/TTS reviewed.

## Targets

- [vase](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/vase): UK /vɑːz/, US /veɪs/. Container for cut flowers. Initial /v/ → a vase. Do not assume the actual voice selects either variety.
- [clock](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/clock): UK /klɒk/, US /klɑːk/. Time-showing device; initial /k/ → a clock.
- [radio](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/radio): UK /ˈreɪ.di.əʊ/, US /ˈreɪ.di.oʊ/. Count sense broadcast receiver; initial /r/ → a radio, without equating English /r/ to Cyrillic р.

## Recommended options after refinement

**vase / voice / vice**. [voice](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/voice) /vɔɪs/, meaning голос. [vice](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/vice) /vaɪs/, meaning порок. One syllable, /v/ onset, alveolar-fricative ending. US strongly close: same /v…s/, differing diphthongs. UK moderately close: vowel and final voicing differ. Truthful cross-variety feedback: voice has ой-like /ɔɪ/; vice has ай-like /aɪ/; neither is the vowel in either documented vase variant. Do not claim universal rhyme, a single vase vowel, or universal final-voicing difference.

Initial face/base suggestion required US /veɪs/; UK bars/cars was provisional. The refined voice/vice set avoids that accent dependency. Actual clips remain unverified.

**clock / lock / block**. [lock](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/lock) UK /lɒk/, US /lɑːk/; [block](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/block) UK /blɒk/, US /blɑːk/. Lock lacks initial /k/; block starts /bl/ rather than /kl/. Strong in both varieties.

**radio / radial / radiant**. [radial](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/radial) /ˈreɪ.di.əl/, [radiant](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/radiant) /ˈreɪ.di.ənt/. Shared first portion /ˈreɪ.di/; contrast radial final /l/ and radiant final /nt/, absent in radio. Do not claim the entire third-vowel onset is identical across accents: US radio /oʊ/ differs from /ə/. Unknown adjectives are wrong sound options only with immediate native meanings, not assumed explanatory vocabulary.

The is not an incorrect a/an sound variant; do not use it as a sound-rule error.
