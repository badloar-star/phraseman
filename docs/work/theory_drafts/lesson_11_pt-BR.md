# Teoria · Lição 11 do curso novo — «I was, they were, there was» (PT-BR)

> Sessão «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Seções 01–11, frases em inglês e exercícios como em
> `lesson_11_ru.md`. Armadilhas de um brasileiro: «estava em casa» sem sujeito (→ Was at home),
> «tinha dois prendedores» (→ Had two / There was two), pergunta pela voz (→ It was warm?),
> «no Natal» (→ in Christmas), «no domingo» (→ in Sunday). Tratamento: você.
> Precisa de revisão de um falante nativo.

**Título da tela:** Teoria · O que foi ontem

---

## 01 · O que você vai aprender a dizer *(aberta de início)*

**body**
Até agora você falava do que é agora: I am at home. Agora vai contar o que foi: «ontem eu estava em casa», «no Natal a gente estava junto», «tinha dois prendedores na sacola». Para isso a peça tem uma forma no passado.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Ontem eu estava em casa. | was |
| We were together at Christmas. | No Natal a gente estava junto. | were |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | Tinha dois prendedores na sacola. | There were |
| Was it warm in spring? | Fez calor na primavera? | Was |

---

## 02 · was e were *(aberta de início)*

**body**
A peça tem duas formas no passado. Onde era am ou is, agora é was: I was, she was, it was. Onde era are, agora é were: you were, we were, they were. Servem para «era», «estava», «foi», «esteve» — em inglês não precisa escolher. E sempre com quem no começo.

**formula**
`I / he / she / it` · `was` — `you / we / they` · `were`

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Ontem eu estava em casa. | was |
| She was in London last year. | Ano passado ela esteve em Londres. | was |
| We were at home last night. | Ontem à noite a gente estava em casa. | were |
| They were in London at New Year. | No Ano-Novo eles estavam em Londres. | were |
| The hairdresser was busy yesterday. | Ontem a cabeleireira estava ocupada. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Was at home yesterday.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~I were at home yesterday.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.

**drill · choice**
- frase: `We ___ at the train station yesterday.`
- opções: `was` · `were` · `are`
- resposta: `were`
- por quê: Com we, no passado, were.

---

## 03 · Marcas do passado: yesterday, last night, on Sunday

**body**
Quase sempre há uma palavra que marca o passado: yesterday — «ontem», last night — «ontem à noite», last year — «ano passado». Para festas, at: at Christmas, at New Year. Para dias, on: on Sunday.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home on Sunday. | No domingo eu estava em casa. | on Sunday |
| We were together at Christmas. | No Natal a gente estava junto. | at Christmas |
| They were in London at New Year. | No Ano-Novo eles estavam em Londres. | at New Year |
| The sports centre was closed yesterday. | Ontem o centro esportivo estava fechado. | yesterday |

**fix**
- ~~We were together in Christmas.~~ → We were together at Christmas.
- ~~I was at home in Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.

---

## 04 · Ontem — was, agora — is

**body**
Se é sobre ontem, was ou were. Se é sobre agora, is ou are, como antes. A palavra yesterday ou now já indica: Yesterday my towel was wet. The shower curtain is dry now.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday my beach towel was wet. | Ontem minha toalha de praia estava molhada. | was |
| Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty. | Ontem meus chinelos estavam sujos. | were |
| The shower curtain is dry now. | Agora a cortina do box está seca. | is |
| The bathroom mirror was dirty yesterday. | Ontem o espelho do banheiro estava sujo. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~Yesterday my flip-flops was dirty.~~ → Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty.

**drill · binary**
- pergunta: Ontem a toalha estava molhada. Como dizer?
- A: `Yesterday my beach towel is wet.`
- B: `Yesterday my beach towel was wet.`
- certa: B
- explicação: Ontem é passado: was.

## 05 · O que não se conta em unidades — was

**body**
Como na lição 4, o que não se conta em unidades — protetor, comida, suco — vai com a forma de um: was, não were. Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag. | Ontem o protetor solar estava na minha bolsa. | was |
| The apple juice was cold yesterday. | Ontem o suco de maçã estava gelado. | was |
| The hot chocolate was too sweet yesterday. | Ontem o chocolate quente estava doce demais. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday the sun cream were in my bag.~~ → Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.
- ~~The baby food were in the cupboard.~~ → The baby food was in the cupboard.

---

## 06 · «Não estava»: was not, were not

**body**
Em português o «não» vem antes: «não estava». Em inglês o not vem depois da peça: It was not my day off. The fried egg was not cold. Para vários, were not.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It was not my day off. | Não era minha folga. | was not |
| The fried egg was not cold. | O ovo frito não estava frio. | was not |
| The peanut butter was not on the table. | A pasta de amendoim não estava na mesa. | was not |

**fix**
- ~~It no was my day off.~~ → It was not my day off.
- ~~The fried egg didn't cold.~~ → The fried egg was not cold.

---

## 07 · Pergunta: Was…? Were…?

**body**
A pergunta se monta como na lição 2: a peça na frente. Was it warm in spring? Were you at home in autumn? A resposta curta também com a peça: Yes, it was. No, it was not. Aqui não vai do nem did.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `quem / o quê` · `…` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Was it warm in spring? | Fez calor na primavera? | Was |
| Were you at home in autumn? | No outono você estava em casa? | Were |
| Was your pancake hot? | Sua panqueca estava quente? | Was |
| Were the scrambled eggs hot? | Os ovos mexidos estavam quentes? | Were |
| Yes, it was. | Estava, sim. | was |

**fix**
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Were your pancake hot?~~ → Was your pancake hot?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.

**drill · word_bank**
- tarefa: Pergunte: «Fez calor na primavera?»
- resposta: `Was` · `it` · `warm` · `in` · `spring`
- rótulos: nenhum (a frase é longa)
- fichas a mais: `Were` · `Did`

## 08 · «Tinha»: there was, there were

**body**
Em português «tinha» serve para um e para vários, e é o verbo ter. Em inglês não se usa have: There was para uma coisa, There were para várias. There was a towel rail in the bathroom. There were two clothes pegs in the bag.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| There was a bed sheet on the bed. | Tinha um lençol na cama. | There was |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | Tinha dois prendedores na sacola. | There were |
| There was a public toilet near the station. | Tinha um banheiro público perto da estação. | There was |
| There were two taxi drivers near the station. | Tinha dois taxistas perto da estação. | There were |
| There was fruit salad in the bowl. | Tinha salada de frutas na tigela. | There was |

**fix**
- ~~Had two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~There was two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.

**drill · choice**
- frase: `There ___ two duvet covers in the cupboard.`
- opções: `was` · `were` · `is`
- resposta: `were`
- por quê: São duas capas de edredom, então There were.

## 09 · Opinar sobre o que passou

**body**
Com was é fácil opinar sobre algo que passou: That was a silly idea — «foi uma ideia boba», The plan was sensible — «o plano era sensato». Para várias coisas, were: The rules were fair.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| That was a silly idea. | Foi uma ideia boba. | was |
| The plan was sensible. | O plano era sensato. | was |
| The rules were fair. | As regras eram justas. | were |

---

## 10 · Os erros que quase todo mundo comete

**fix**
- ~~Was at home yesterday.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~Had two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.
- ~~I was at home in Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.

**tip**
Toda a gramática nova são duas formas: was para I, he, she, it e uma coisa; were para you, we, they e várias. O resto é igual a am, is, are.

## 11 · O que levar para a prática

**body**
Foi, estava: was ou were, sempre com quem. Não estava: was not, were not. Pergunta: Was…? Were…?, resposta: Yes, it was. Tinha: There was / There were. Ontem: was; agora: is.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She was in London last year. | Ano passado ela esteve em Londres. | was |
| It was not my day off. | Não era minha folga. | was not |
| Were you at home in autumn? | No outono você estava em casa? | Were |
| There was a soap dish next to the washbasin. | Tinha uma saboneteira do lado da pia. | There was |
