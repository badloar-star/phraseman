First two triples are strong onset contrasts. For fixed target vacuum the
researcher ranked vacuous first and volume second for sound similarity.
Vacuous is formal unfamiliar vocabulary, permitted only as a wrong option;
it must never be used as unintroduced explanation support or correct target.
Vaccine (/ˈvæk.siːn/ UK; /vækˈsiːn/ US) shares beginning but changes ending and
US stress; backroom also changes onset, medial consonant and stress. Those
alternatives are weaker and not recommended for the draft.

Actual learner feedback must be written plainly, not copied as an IPA lesson.
Simple exact distinguishing consonants are available. Do not equate English
/r/ with Cyrillic р. Independent truth reviewer must judge authored feedback.
