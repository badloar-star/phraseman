# Teori · Pelajaran 15 kursus baru — «I am going to…, I am meeting… tomorrow» (ID)

> Sesi «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Bagian 01–07, kalimat bahasa Inggris dan latihan sama seperti
> `lesson_15_ru.md`. Jebakan penutur Indonesia: «akan / mau» satu kata sehingga penjepit hilang
> (→ I going to), «mau pergi» jadi I am go to, to hilang (→ I am going paint), kata kerja tidak
> berubah sehingga «besok saya ketemu» jadi I meet tomorrow. Sapaan: kamu. Perlu dibaca penutur asli.

**Judul layar:** Teori · Rencana dan janji temu

---

## 01 · Apa yang akan kamu pelajari *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Di pelajaran ini kamu belajar bicara tentang masa depan: «saya mau membersihkan kerak ketel», «wastafelnya mau meluap», «besok saya ketemu perawat». Caranya ada dua, dan keduanya sudah setengah kamu kenal.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am going to descale the kettle. | Saya mau membersihkan kerak ketel. | going to |
| The sink is going to overflow. | Wastafelnya mau meluap. | going to |
| I am meeting the carer tomorrow. | Besok saya ketemu perawat. | am meeting |

---

## 02 · going to — «mau», sudah diputuskan *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Kalau kamu sudah memutuskan melakukan sesuatu, bilang am / is / are going to + kata kerja seperti di kamus: I am going to repaint the wall — «saya mau mengecat ulang dinding». Penjepit, going, dan to wajib ada.

**formula**
`siapa` · `am / is / are going to` · `kata kerja seperti di kamus`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am going to descale the kettle. | Saya mau membersihkan kerak ketel. | going to |
| She is going to repaint the wall. | Dia mau mengecat ulang dinding. | going to |
| We are going to alter the dress. | Kami mau mengecilkan gaunnya. | going to |
| I am going to hang the bunting. | Saya mau memasang bendera hias. | going to |

**fix**
- ~~I going to descale the kettle.~~ → I am going to descale the kettle.
- ~~I am go to descale the kettle.~~ → I am going to descale the kettle.
- ~~I am going descale the kettle.~~ → I am going to descale the kettle.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `She is going ___ repaint the wall.`
- pilihan: `to` · `—` · `for`
- jawaban: `to`
- kenapa: Setelah going selalu ada to.

---

## 03 · going to — «mau terjadi», kelihatan

**body**
Bentuk yang sama dipakai untuk menebak kalau tandanya sudah kelihatan: air sudah sampai bibir — The sink is going to overflow; menara sudah miring — The tower is going to topple.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| The sink is going to overflow. | Wastafelnya mau meluap. | going to |
| The tower is going to topple. | Menaranya mau roboh. | going to |
| The shelf is going to collapse. | Raknya mau ambruk. | going to |
| The jumper is going to shrink. | Sweternya bakal menyusut. | going to |
| The boards are going to rot. | Papan-papannya bakal lapuk. | going to |

**fix**
- ~~The tower going to topple.~~ → The tower is going to topple.
- ~~The boards is going to rot.~~ → The boards are going to rot.

**drill · binary**
- pertanyaan: Air di wastafel sudah di bibir, keran masih terbuka. Kamu bilang apa?
- A: `The sink overflows.`
- B: `The sink is going to overflow.`
- benar: B
- penjelasan: Tandanya sudah kelihatan, sebentar lagi terjadi: is going to.

## 04 · Janji temu: I am meeting… tomorrow

**body**
Kalau kamu sudah janjian dengan seseorang dan ada waktunya — «besok jam enam» — pakai bentuk dari pelajaran 9: am / is / are + -ing dan waktu. I am meeting the carer tomorrow. Tanpa waktu, kalimatnya terdengar seperti «sekarang».

**formula**
`siapa` · `am / is / are` · `kata kerja + ing` · `kapan`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am meeting the carer tomorrow. | Besok saya ketemu perawat. | am meeting |
| We are meeting the babysitter at six tomorrow. | Besok jam enam kami ketemu pengasuh anak. | are meeting |
| We are visiting a car boot sale on Sunday. | Hari Minggu kami ke pasar loak bagasi mobil. | are visiting |
| They are meeting at the book club tonight. | Malam ini mereka ketemu di klub buku. | are meeting |

**fix**
- ~~I meet the carer tomorrow.~~ *(sudah janjian)* → I am meeting the carer tomorrow.
- ~~I am meet the carer tomorrow.~~ → I am meeting the carer tomorrow.

---

## 05 · Niat atau janji?

**body**
going to — kamu memutuskan sendiri: I am going to renew my pass. -ing dengan waktu — sudah janjian dengan orang lain, ada tanggalnya: We are paying for our membership on Friday. Keduanya tentang masa depan; bedanya, ada kesepakatan atau tidak.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am going to renew my pass. | Saya mau memperpanjang kartu masuk. | going to |
| I am bringing my appointment card tomorrow. | Besok saya bawa kartu janji temu. | am bringing |
| I am going to attend a christening. | Saya mau datang ke acara pembaptisan. | going to |
| We are attending a hen party on Saturday. | Hari Sabtu kami datang ke pesta lajang. | are attending |

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `We ___ the quince tomorrow.` *(sudah janjian dengan tetangga, dia menunggu)*
- pilihan: `are collecting` · `collect` · `are going collect`
- jawaban: `are collecting`
- kenapa: Ada janji dan waktu: am / is / are + -ing.

## 06 · Kesalahan yang dibuat hampir semua orang

**fix**
- ~~I going to descale the kettle.~~ → I am going to descale the kettle.
- ~~I am go to descale the kettle.~~ → I am going to descale the kettle.
- ~~I am going descale the kettle.~~ → I am going to descale the kettle.
- ~~He are going to stumble.~~ → He is going to stumble.
- ~~I meet the carer tomorrow.~~ *(sudah janjian)* → I am meeting the carer tomorrow.

**tip**
«Mau» dalam bahasa Indonesia satu kata, dalam bahasa Inggris empat bagian: siapa + am / is / are + going + to. Jangan ada yang hilang.

## 07 · Yang dibawa ke latihan

**body**
Memutuskan sendiri — am / is / are going to + kata kerja. Kelihatan mau terjadi — juga going to. Sudah janjian, ada waktunya — am / is / are + -ing + kapan.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We are going to apply an undercoat. | Kami mau memoles cat dasar. | going to |
| The scarf is going to unravel. | Syalnya mau terurai. | going to |
| They are meeting the cleaner tomorrow morning. | Besok pagi mereka ketemu petugas kebersihan. | are meeting |
