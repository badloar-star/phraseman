Recommended Direction: Black Cinema Relic Glass

Midnight
electric blue to violet, flagship cool spectrum
Feels premium, calm, most universal. Best default for serious cinematic pack.
Ember
amber to crimson, warm prestige and tension
Warmer and more dramatic. Works for rewards, streaks, hard lessons, and premium moments.
Aurora
mint to azure, clean intelligence and motion
Freshest of the set. Good for learning/trainer assets without becoming playful.
Volt
lime to emerald, high energy, successor to neon
Most energetic. Needs restrained glow so it feels premium rather than arcade.

Style Options Before Final Generation

A. Relic Glass

Recommended. Dark glass plaques, metallic relic silhouettes, controlled bloom. Strongest fit for current Black Cinema UI.

B. Monolith

More severe and less decorative. Good if we want the themes to feel like premium OS skins rather than game rewards.

C. Signal Crest

Sharper, more competitive. Better for arena/quiz medals, but could feel too aggressive if used everywhere.
