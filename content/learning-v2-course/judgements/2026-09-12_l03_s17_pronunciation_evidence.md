## Singular targets and regular plurals

- `ticket` /ˈtɪk.ɪt/: count noun, proof of payment. `ticket + s → tickets`, derived /ˈtɪk.ɪts/, still two syllables. [Cambridge](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/ticket).
- `coin` /kɔɪn/: count noun, metal money. `coin + s → coins`, derived /kɔɪnz/, one syllable. [Meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/coin), [pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/coin).
- `card` UK /kɑːd/, US /kɑːrd/: count noun, card/plastic used for payment or access. Select one exact useful sense in authoring. `card + s → cards`, derived UK /kɑːdz/, US /kɑːrdz/, one syllable. [Meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/card), [pronunciation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/card).

Full plural transcriptions are rule-derived, not independently listened-to clips. Primary spelling rule: [noun forms](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/nouns-form). Endings: [s](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/s), [shared plural/possessive pronunciation rules](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/possession). Only tickets ends /s/; coins/cards end /z/; none adds a syllable. Do not teach all written plural s as the same sound.

## Real nonhomophone sound sets

`ticket / cricket / picket`: /ˈtɪk.ɪt/, /ˈkrɪk.ɪt/, /ˈpɪk.ɪt/. Two syllables, first stress, shared /ɪk.ɪt/; different onset. Choose one precise wrong-option meaning and gloss it immediately: cricket sport or insect, picket protester or fence stake. [Cricket IPA](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/cricket), [meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/cricket), [picket IPA](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/picket), [meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/picket).

`coin / coil / join`: /kɔɪn/, /kɔɪl/, /dʒɔɪn/. Coil differs at final /l/ versus /n/; join differs at initial /dʒ/ versus /k/. Coil=моток or спираль; join=соединять / з’єднувати (choose one context-accurate gloss). [Coil](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/coil), [join IPA](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/join), [join meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/join).

`card / cart / guard`: UK /kɑːd/, /kɑːt/, /ɡɑːd/; US rhotic /r/ retained in each corresponding form. Card/cart differ in final /d/ versus /t/; card/guard in initial /k/ versus /g/. Cart=тележка / візок; guard=охранник / охоронець. [Cart IPA](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/cart), [meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/cart), [guard IPA](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/guard), [meaning](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/guard).

Native feedback must explain usable contrasts without asserting English /r/=Cyrillic р or English /g/=Ukrainian г. Definitions and scope require independent review. Exact plan remains S17 regular plural -s only; no -es/-ies or future countability system. No actual-audio PASS.
