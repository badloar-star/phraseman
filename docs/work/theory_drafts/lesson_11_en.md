## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
So far you talked about now: I am at home. Now you'll learn to talk about the past: «I was at home yesterday», «we were together at Christmas», «there were two clothes pegs in the bag». The clip has a past form for this.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Yesterday, my place was home. | was |
| We were together at Christmas. | At Christmas, we were in one place. | were |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | The bag had two pegs in it. | There were |
| Was it warm in spring? | Did spring have warm weather? | Was |

## 02 · was and were *(open at start)*

**body**
The clip has two past forms. Where you used am or is, now use was: I was, she was, it was. Where you used are, now use were: you were, we were, they were.

**formula**
`I / he / she / it` · `was` — `you / we / they` · `were`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Yesterday, my place was home. | was |
| She was in London last year. | Last year, she was in London. | was |
| We were at home last night. | Last night, we stayed at home. | were |
| They were in London at New Year. | At New Year, they were in London. | were |
| The hairdresser was busy yesterday. | Yesterday, the hairdresser had no free time. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday I am at home.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~I were at home yesterday.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `We ___ at the train station yesterday.`
- options: `was` · `were` · `are`
- answer: `were`
- why: With we in the past, use were.

## 03 · Past time words: yesterday, last night, on Sunday

**body**
Usually there's a word that shows the past: yesterday, last night, last year. For holidays, use at: at Christmas, at New Year. For days, use on: on Sunday.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home on Sunday. | Last Sunday, I stayed home. | on Sunday |
| We were together at Christmas. | At Christmas, we were in one place. | at Christmas |
| They were in London at New Year. | At New Year, they were in London. | at New Year |
| The sports centre was closed yesterday. | Yesterday, the sports centre didn't open. | yesterday |

**fix**
- ~~I was at home in Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.
- ~~We were together on Christmas.~~ → We were together at Christmas.

---

## 04 · Yesterday — was, now — is

**body**
For yesterday, use was or were. For now, use is or are, as before. The word yesterday or now tells you which: Yesterday my towel was wet. The shower curtain is dry now.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday my beach towel was wet. | It had water in it yesterday. | was |
| Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty. | They had dirt on them yesterday. | were |
| The shower curtain is dry now. | It has no water on it now. | is |
| The bathroom mirror was dirty yesterday. | It wasn't clean yesterday. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~Yesterday my flip-flops was dirty.~~ → Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty.

**drill · binary**
- question: Yesterday the towel was wet. How do you say it?
- A: `Yesterday my beach towel is wet.`
- B: `Yesterday my beach towel was wet.`
- correct: B
- explanation: Yesterday is the past, so was.

## 05 · Things you don't count — was

**body**
As in lesson 4, things you don't count one by one — cream, food, juice — take the form for one: was, not were. Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag. | The cream for the sun was in my bag. | was |
| The apple juice was cold yesterday. | The juice was cold yesterday. | was |
| The hot chocolate was too sweet yesterday. | It had too much sugar yesterday. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday the sun cream were in my bag.~~ → Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.
- ~~The baby food were in the cupboard.~~ → The baby food was in the cupboard.

---

## 06 · «Not»: was not, were not

**body**
Put not right after the clip, as always: It was not my day off. The fried egg was not cold. For more than one, use were not.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It was not my day off. | That day I had to work. | was not |
| The fried egg was not cold. | The egg was still warm. | was not |
| The peanut butter was not on the table. | It was somewhere else. | was not |

**fix**
- ~~It not was my day off.~~ → It was not my day off.
- ~~The fried egg didn't cold.~~ → The fried egg was not cold.

---

## 07 · Questions: Was…? Were…?

**body**
Make questions like in lesson 2: put the clip first. Was it warm in spring? Were you at home in autumn? Short answers use the clip too: Yes, it was. No, it was not. Don't use do or did here.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `who / what` · `…` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Was it warm in spring? | Did spring have warm weather? | Was |
| Were you at home in autumn? | In autumn, did you stay at home? | Were |
| Was your pancake hot? | Did your pancake feel hot? | Was |
| Were the scrambled eggs hot? | Did the eggs feel hot? | Were |
| Yes, it was. | A short «yes» about one thing. | was |

**fix**
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Were your pancake hot?~~ → Was your pancake hot?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Ask if it was warm in spring.
- answer: `Was` · `it` · `warm` · `in` · `spring`
- slot labels: none (the sentence is long)
- extra tiles: `Were` · `Did`

## 08 · There was, there were

**body**
To say something was somewhere, take there is from lesson 4 and change is to was: There was a towel rail in the bathroom. For more than one thing, use There were: There were two clothes pegs in the bag.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| There was a bed sheet on the bed. | The bed had a sheet on it. | There was |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | The bag had two pegs in it. | There were |
| There was a public toilet near the station. | A toilet for everyone was near the station. | There was |
| There were two taxi drivers near the station. | Two taxi drivers were near the station. | There were |
| There was fruit salad in the bowl. | The bowl had fruit salad in it. | There was |

**fix**
- ~~There was two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~There are two clothes pegs in the bag yesterday.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `There ___ two duvet covers in the cupboard.`
- options: `was` · `were` · `is`
- answer: `were`
- why: There are two covers, so There were.

## 09 · Saying what you thought of something

**body**
With was, it's easy to judge something in the past: That was a silly idea. The plan was sensible. For more than one thing, use were: The rules were fair.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| That was a silly idea. | The idea wasn't clever. | was |
| The plan was sensible. | The plan was a good, careful one. | was |
| The rules were fair. | The rules were the same for everyone. | were |

## 10 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday I am at home.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~Yesterday the sun cream were in my bag.~~ → Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.
- ~~There was two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.
- ~~I was at home in Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.

**tip**
All the new grammar in this lesson is two forms: was for I, he, she, it and one thing; were for you, we, they and more than one. Everything else works like am, is, are.

## 11 · What to take into practice

**body**
In the past: was or were. Not: was not, were not. Questions: Was…? Were…?, answer: Yes, it was. Something was there: There was / There were. Yesterday: was; now: is.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She was in London last year. | Last year, she was in London. | was |
| It was not my day off. | That day I had to work. | was not |
| Were you at home in autumn? | In autumn, did you stay at home? | Were |
| There was a soap dish next to the washbasin. | A soap dish was by the sink. | There was |
