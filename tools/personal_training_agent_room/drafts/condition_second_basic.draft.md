## Teaching Point

The learner should not think "past tense" first. The learner should hear the meaning:

1. real plan: `If I have time, I will call`
2. imagined situation: `If I had time, I would call`
3. advice block: `If I were you, I would wait`

## Copy Rules

- Visible RU/UK copy uses ASCII transliteration.
- Avoid learner-facing grammar labels such as `base verb`.
- Keep tested English anchors exactly.
- Contrast set and Smart Trainer keep the contract labels.

## Structure

- Easy: imagined result with `would`.
- Contrast: no `will/would` after `if`, no `to` after `would`.
- Mixed: `If I were you`, real vs imagined pair, full sentence repair.
