**Screen title:** Theory · Can, could, may I?

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open from the start)*

**body**
This lesson is about what you can do: things you are able to do now, things you could do as a child, polite ways to ask, and what you will be able to do. Almost always the action after the key word stays as in the dictionary, with no to.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I can remove this stain. | I am able to do it now | can |
| At ten, I could read fluently. | an ability in the past | could |
| May I have a sample? | a polite request | May I |
| We will be able to achieve this goal. | we can do it in the future | will be able to |

---

## 02 · can — I am able to *(open from the start)*

**body**
can + the action as in the dictionary: I can remove this stain. can is the same for everyone — he can, she can, no -s. Not able: cannot — We cannot talk in private. Never put to after can.

**formula**
`who` · `can / cannot` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I can remove this stain. | I know how to clean it | can |
| She can replace the battery. | she is able to change it | can |
| We can arrange a meeting. | it is possible for us | can |
| Noise can affect your sleep. | it is possible that it does | can |
| We cannot talk in private. | it is not possible here | cannot |

**fix**
- ~~I can to describe the place.~~ → I can describe the place.
- ~~I can describes the place.~~ → I can describe the place.
- ~~She cans suggest a restaurant.~~ → She can suggest a restaurant.

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `She can ___ the battery.`
- options: `replace` · `replaces` · `to replace`
- answer: `replace`
- why: After can: the action as in the dictionary, no -s, no to.

## 03 · could — I was able to, in the past

**body**
To say what you were able to do in general in the past, use could: At ten, I could read fluently. could is also the same for everyone and also has no to after it.

**formula**
`when` · `who` · `could` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| At ten, I could read fluently. | I read well as a child | could |
| At six, I could write the alphabet. | I knew all the letters | could |
| At twelve, we could travel independently. | we went places alone | could |
| At twenty, I could learn a new skill. | learning was easy then | could |

**fix**
- ~~At ten, I can read fluently.~~ → At ten, I could read fluently.
- ~~At ten, I could to read fluently.~~ → At ten, I could read fluently.

**drill · binary**
- question: You remember your childhood: at ten you already read well. What do you say?
- A: `At ten, I can read fluently.`
- B: `At ten, I could read fluently.`
- correct: B
- explain: An ability in the past: could.

## 04 · May I…? — asking for permission

**body**
To ask for permission politely, say May I + the action? May I have a sample? May comes first, before I, and the action after I has no to.

**formula**
`May I` · `action as in the dictionary` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| May I have a sample? | a small piece to try | May I |
| May I make a comment? | I want to say something | May I |
| May I change the channel? | a different TV channel | May I |
| May I disturb you for a moment? | sorry to stop you | May I |
| May I check the exact price? | the price to the penny | May I |

**fix**
- ~~I may have a sample?~~ → May I have a sample?
- ~~May I to have a sample?~~ → May I have a sample?

---

## 05 · Could I…? — another polite request

**body**
Could I…? is another polite way to ask: Could I pay in advance? Here could is not about the past; it is a request. Same order: Could first, then I and the action with no to.

**formula**
`Could I` · `action as in the dictionary` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Could I discuss my diet? | talk about what I eat | Could I |
| Could I bring my sibling? | my brother or sister | Could I |
| Could I pay in advance? | pay before, not after | Could I |
| Could I start right away? | start now | Could I |
| Could I have it in writing? | on paper, not just said | Could I |

**fix**
- ~~I could discuss my diet?~~ → Could I discuss my diet?
- ~~Could I to discuss my diet?~~ → Could I discuss my diet?

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Ask politely if you can pay all the money at once.
- answer: `Could` · `I` · `pay` · `in` · `full`
- slot labels: none (long phrase)
- extra tiles: `to` · `did`

## 06 · be able to — now and in the future

**body**
am / is able to + action means the same as can, with am / is and to: I am able to cope with the stress. For the future use will be able to: We will be able to achieve this goal. Never say will can.

**formula**
`now: am / is able to + action` · `future: will be able to + action`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am able to cope with the stress. | I can deal with it | am able to |
| She is able to recover at home. | she can get better there | is able to |
| I am able to concentrate here. | I can think well here | am able to |
| We will be able to achieve this goal. | we will reach it | will be able to |
| We will be able to pay this debt. | we will pay it back | will be able to |

**fix**
- ~~We will can achieve this goal.~~ → We will be able to achieve this goal.
- ~~I able to cope with the stress.~~ → I am able to cope with the stress.
- ~~I am able cope with the stress.~~ → I am able to cope with the stress.

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `We will ___ resolve the problem.`
- options: `be able to` · `can` · `able to`
- answer: `be able to`
- why: In the future: will be able to. will can does not exist.

## 07 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I can to describe the place.~~ → I can describe the place.
- ~~She cans suggest a restaurant.~~ → She can suggest a restaurant.
- ~~I may have a sample?~~ → May I have a sample?
- ~~I am able cope with the stress.~~ → I am able to cope with the stress.
- ~~We will can achieve this goal.~~ → We will be able to achieve this goal.

**tip**
After can, could, May I and Could I comes the action as in the dictionary, with no to. In able to you do need to, with am / is or will be before it.

## 08 · What to take into practice

**body**
Able now — can. Able in the past — could. Asking politely — May I…? or Could I…?. Able in the future — will be able to. No to after can and could; to after able.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I can keep up with you. | I can go as fast as you | can |
| May I offer an apology? | I want to say sorry | May I |
| She is able to participate in the class. | she can take part | is able to |
