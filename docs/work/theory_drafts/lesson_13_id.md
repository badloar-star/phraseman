# Teori · Pelajaran 13 kursus baru — «Did you…? I didn't. First of all…» (ID)

> Sesi «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Bagian 01–11, kalimat bahasa Inggris, dan latihan sama dengan
> `lesson_13_ru.md`. Jebakan penutur bahasa Indonesia: «Apakah kamu sudah…?» → Are you cleaned? /
> You cleaned?, «tidak / belum» di depan kata kerja (→ I not buy / I didn't bought), «sudah» jadi
> did di kalimat biasa (→ I did clean), kata kedua pick up, run out of hilang. Sapaan: kamu.
> Perlu dibaca ulang oleh penutur asli.

**Judul layar:** Teori · Bertanya dan bilang «tidak» tentang masa lalu

## 01 · Apa yang akan kamu pelajari *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Di pelajaran 12 kamu bercerita apa yang sudah kamu lakukan: I cleaned, I went. Sekarang kamu belajar bertanya tentang masa lalu — «kamu sudah membersihkan dapur?», bilang «tidak» — «aku tidak membeli sepatu itu» — dan bercerita berurutan: «pertama… lalu… akhirnya».

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did you clean the kitchen? | Kamu sudah membersihkan dapur? | Did |
| I didn't buy the shoes. | Aku tidak membeli sepatu itu. | didn't |
| First of all, I checked the address. | Pertama-tama, aku mengecek alamatnya. | First of all |
| In the end, I took the bus. | Akhirnya, aku naik bus. | In the end |

## 02 · Bertanya tentang masa lalu: Did…? *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Kita bertanya «Apakah kamu sudah…?» tanpa mengubah urutan. Bahasa Inggris memakai Did di depan, sama untuk semua: Did you…? Did she…? Sesudah Did, kegiatan seperti di kamus, tanpa -ed: Did you clean, bukan Did you cleaned. Juga bukan Are you cleaned.

**formula**
`Did` · `siapa` · `kegiatan seperti di kamus` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did you clean the kitchen? | Kamu sudah membersihkan dapur? | Did |
| Did you wash the coffee mug? | Kamu sudah mencuci cangkir kopi? | Did |
| Did she wash the tea strainer? | Dia sudah mencuci saringan teh? | Did |
| Did you try a fig? | Kamu sudah mencoba buah tin? | Did |
| Did you go sightseeing? | Kamu jalan-jalan keliling kota? | Did |

**fix**
- ~~Are you cleaned the kitchen?~~ → Did you clean the kitchen?
- ~~You cleaned the kitchen?~~ → Did you clean the kitchen?
- ~~Did she tried a blackberry?~~ → Did she try a blackberry?

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `Did she ___ a blackberry?`
- pilihan: `try` · `tried` · `tries`
- jawaban: `try`
- kenapa: Did sudah menunjukkan masa lalu, kegiatannya seperti di kamus.

---

## 03 · «Tidak» di masa lalu: didn't

**body**
Kita menaruh «tidak» di depan kata kerja: «aku tidak membeli». Bahasa Inggris menaruh didn't di depan kegiatan, sama untuk semua. Kegiatannya tetap seperti di kamus: I didn't buy, bukan I not buy atau I didn't bought.

**formula**
`siapa` · `didn't` · `kegiatan seperti di kamus`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I didn't buy the shoes. | Aku tidak membeli sepatu itu. | didn't |
| I didn't lie down. | Aku tidak berbaring. | didn't |
| I didn't stay in. | Aku tidak tinggal di rumah. | didn't |
| I didn't come back early. | Aku tidak pulang cepat. | didn't |
| I didn't fill in the form. | Aku tidak mengisi formulirnya. | didn't |

**fix**
- ~~I not buy the shoes.~~ → I didn't buy the shoes.
- ~~I didn't bought the shoes.~~ → I didn't buy the shoes.
- ~~I don't buy the shoes yesterday.~~ → I didn't buy the shoes yesterday.

**drill · spot_slip**
- kartu: `I` · `didn't` · `bought` · `the` · `shoes`
- salah: `bought` (indeks 2)
- petunjuk: Ada satu kata dalam bentuk yang salah. Ketuk kata itu.
- penjelasan: Sesudah didn't, kegiatannya seperti di kamus: I didn't buy the shoes.

## 04 · Masa lalu ditandai sekali saja

**body**
Aturan utama: masa lalu ditandai di satu tempat saja. Di kegiatan (I cleaned), atau di did (Did you clean? I didn't clean). Dua kali — Did you cleaned — salah. Kalimat biasa tidak perlu did: I cleaned, bukan I did clean.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I cleaned the kitchen. | Aku sudah membersihkan dapur. | cleaned |
| Did you clean the kitchen? | Kamu sudah membersihkan dapur? | Did |
| I didn't speed up. | Aku tidak mempercepat laju. | didn't |
| Did you slow down? | Kamu memperlambat laju? | Did |

**fix**
- ~~Did you slowed down?~~ → Did you slow down?
- ~~I did clean the kitchen.~~ → I cleaned the kitchen.

**drill · binary**
- pertanyaan: Kamu bertanya apakah temanmu memperlambat laju. Mana yang benar?
- A: `Did you slowed down?`
- B: `Did you slow down?`
- benar: B
- penjelasan: Masa lalu sudah ada di Did, jadi slow tanpa -ed.

## 05 · Kegiatan dua kata

**body**
Banyak kegiatan bahasa Inggris terdiri dari dua kata: pick up — «mengambil», put away — «menyimpan», run out of — «kehabisan», slow down — «memperlambat». Kata kedua mengubah arti, jangan dibuang.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did you pick up the parcel? | Kamu sudah mengambil paketnya? | pick up |
| Did you put away the clothes? | Kamu sudah menyimpan bajunya? | put away |
| Did you run out of milk? | Kamu kehabisan susu? | run out of |
| I didn't give back the book. | Aku belum mengembalikan bukunya. | give back |
| I didn't fall over. | Aku tidak jatuh. | fall over |

**fix**
- ~~Did you pick the parcel?~~ *(«mengambil»)* → Did you pick up the parcel?
- ~~Did you run out milk?~~ → Did you run out of milk?

**tip**
Hafalkan kegiatan seperti ini utuh, dua kata bersama, seperti satu kata baru.

## 06 · Kapan: yesterday, afterwards, beforehand

**body**
Pertanyaan dan kalimat «tidak» tentang masa lalu sering memakai waktu: yesterday — «kemarin», afterwards — «sesudahnya», beforehand — «sebelumnya». Kata-kata ini di akhir kalimat.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did you call yesterday? | Kamu menelepon kemarin? | yesterday |
| Did you call afterwards? | Kamu menelepon sesudahnya? | afterwards |
| Did you buy the tickets beforehand? | Kamu membeli tiketnya sebelumnya? | beforehand |
| Did you move in last week? | Kamu pindah minggu lalu? | last week |

**fix**
- ~~Did you buy beforehand the tickets?~~ → Did you buy the tickets beforehand?

## 07 · Kata penilaian: definitely, luckily

**body**
Untuk menambah kepastian atau perasaan: definitely — «pasti», certainly — «tentu», luckily — «untungnya». definitely dan certainly di depan kegiatan, luckily di awal kalimat.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I definitely called yesterday. | Aku pasti menelepon kemarin. | definitely |
| I certainly paid yesterday. | Aku tentu sudah membayar kemarin. | certainly |
| Luckily, I caught the bus. | Untungnya, aku sempat naik bus. | Luckily |

**fix**
- ~~I called definitely yesterday.~~ → I definitely called yesterday.

---

## 08 · Berurutan: First of all, Then, In the end

**body**
Untuk bercerita langkah demi langkah: First of all — «pertama-tama», At first — «awalnya», Then — «lalu», In the end — «akhirnya». Kata-kata ini di awal kalimat.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| First of all, I checked the address. | Pertama-tama, aku mengecek alamatnya. | First of all |
| At first, I waited outside. | Awalnya, aku menunggu di luar. | At first |
| Then we stopped off at a café. | Lalu kami mampir ke kafe. | Then |
| In the end, I took the bus. | Akhirnya, aku naik bus. | In the end |

**fix**
- ~~At the end, I took the bus.~~ *(«akhirnya»)* → In the end, I took the bus.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `___, I took the bus.` *(setelah semua usaha)*
- pilihan: `At first` · `In the end` · `First of all`
- jawaban: `In the end`
- kenapa: «Akhirnya» adalah In the end.

## 09 · «Bagaimana» dilakukan: politely, patiently

**body**
Kata berakhiran -ly memberi tahu bagaimana sesuatu dilakukan: politely — «dengan sopan», patiently — «dengan sabar», kindly — «dengan ramah». Letaknya sesudah kegiatan dan bendanya: Did she wait patiently?

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did she ask politely? | Dia memintanya dengan sopan? | politely |
| Did she wait patiently? | Dia menunggu dengan sabar? | patiently |
| Did she speak kindly? | Dia bicara dengan ramah? | kindly |

**fix**
- ~~Did she waited patiently?~~ → Did she wait patiently?
- ~~Did she ask polite?~~ → Did she ask politely?

## 10 · Kesalahan yang dibuat hampir semua orang

**fix**
- ~~Are you cleaned the kitchen?~~ → Did you clean the kitchen?
- ~~Did she tried a blackberry?~~ → Did she try a blackberry?
- ~~I not buy the shoes.~~ → I didn't buy the shoes.
- ~~I didn't bought the shoes.~~ → I didn't buy the shoes.
- ~~Did you run out milk?~~ → Did you run out of milk?
- ~~At the end, I took the bus.~~ → In the end, I took the bus.

**tip**
Ada did atau didn't — kegiatan seperti di kamus. Tidak ada did — kegiatan pakai -ed atau kata khusus. Masa lalu ditandai sekali saja.

## 11 · Bekal untuk latihan

**body**
Bertanya tentang masa lalu — Did + siapa + kegiatan seperti di kamus. «Tidak» — didn't + kegiatan seperti di kamus. Kegiatan dua kata — dihafal utuh. Bercerita berurutan — First of all, Then, In the end.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Did you try a nectarine? | Kamu sudah mencoba nektarin? | Did |
| I didn't save up for a bike. | Aku tidak menabung untuk sepeda. | didn't |
| Did you set off early? | Kamu berangkat pagi? | set off |
| In the end, I took the bus. | Akhirnya, aku naik bus. | In the end |
