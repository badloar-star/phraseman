## Approved Direction: Biomyhtical Species

Every subject starts with a readable real-animal body plan, then receives one coherent fictional adaptation. The adaptation is part of anatomy or natural surface growth—not detached decoration. Examples include a mineral shoulder mantle, petal scales, lantern markings embedded in fur, ribbon fins, or a single organic crest.

The creature must remain traceable as one living body. Hybrids are allowed only when the anatomy is coherent at first glance. A prompt must never request a collage of unrelated animals.

## Dark/Light Pair Contract

- Dark and light are separate prompts and separate generations, never recolors, mirrors, or negatives.
- Both variants depict the same species identity but use different poses and material distributions.
- Dark artwork is shown on the warm light card; light artwork is shown on the dark navy card.
- Each pair must remain visibly related at 20 px without sharing the same alpha silhouette.

## Non-Negotiable Generation Gates

Before promotion, every image must pass all of these gates:

1. **Anatomy:** one traceable body; correct limb/wing/tentacle count stated in the prompt; one head unless explicitly designed otherwise; exactly one tail where applicable.
2. **Framing:** complete silhouette and generous empty margin on all four sides; no part touches the frame.
3. **Extraction:** no green, white, grey, or checkerboard fringe; no uncut triangle beneath tails, wings, legs, or coils; verify on both warm-light and navy backgrounds.
4. **Geometry:** no fused limbs, duplicated paws, detached wings, forked torso, ambiguous coil, broken tail, or floating decorative fragment.
5. **Style:** animal only; no human/humanoid, robot, machine, vehicle, wearable armor, pedestal, text, logo, or scenery.
6. **Value:** the tier reads from silhouette and craftsmanship in grayscale, not merely color or rainbow effects.
7. **Pair:** two independently generated alpha masks and matching species identity.

Automated validation is necessary but never sufficient. A variant is promoted only after manual anatomy review of the processed 512×512 result, not the raw generation alone.

## Workflow

1. Archive the previous regeneration queue as history; do not delete its sources or backups.
2. Create a new 107-item queue from this exact creature map.
3. Work strictly one image at a time to protect the shared PC and Codex session.
4. Generate → checkpoint source → extract → validate → composite on two backgrounds → manually review anatomy → promote.
5. Complete dark and light before advancing to the next ID.
6. After each five completed pairs, produce a browser contact sheet grouped by price for owner review.
7. A rejected image remains only in ignored temp storage and never stays active while work continues.
