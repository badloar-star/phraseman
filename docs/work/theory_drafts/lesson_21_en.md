# Theory · New course, lesson 21 — «enjoy doing, want to do, want you to do, to = in order to» (EN)

> Session «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 22.10.2026. Sections 01–07, English phrases and drills as in

> column explains the meaning instead of translating. Common slips: enjoy to do, want that you,
> want you do without to, for learn instead of to learn.

**Screen title:** Theory · Enjoy doing, want to do

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open from the start)*

**body**
This lesson is about joining two actions: what you like doing, what you want to do, what you want someone else to do, and why you do something. The second action takes -ing or to — the first word decides which.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I enjoy exploring new places. | I like it | exploring |
| I intend to stay here. | my plan | to stay |
| I want you to read aloud. | I want this from you | you to read |
| I wear a coat to keep warm. | the reason | to keep |

---

## 02 · enjoy, finish, avoid + -ing *(open from the start)*

**body**
After enjoy, finish, avoid and do not mind, the second action takes -ing: I enjoy exploring new places. Do not put to here.

**formula**
`enjoy / finish / avoid / do not mind` · `action + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I enjoy exploring new places. | I like discovering them | enjoy exploring |
| I must finish writing the summary. | complete it | finish writing |
| I avoid causing conflict. | I try not to start arguments | avoid causing |
| I do not mind receiving criticism. | it is OK for me | mind receiving |
| I cannot risk losing my job. | it is too dangerous | risk losing |

**fix**
- ~~I enjoy to explore new places.~~ → I enjoy exploring new places.
- ~~I enjoy explore new places.~~ → I enjoy exploring new places.
- ~~I do not mind to receive criticism.~~ → I do not mind receiving criticism.

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `I enjoy ___ in bookshops.`
- options: `browsing` · `to browse` · `browse`
- answer: `browsing`
- why: After enjoy the second action takes -ing.

## 03 · want, hope, plan + to

**body**
After want, need, hope, plan, intend, expect, promise and offer, the second action takes to and stays as in the dictionary: I intend to stay here. After to: no -ing, no -s.

**formula**
`want / need / hope / plan / intend / promise` · `to` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I intend to stay here. | my plan is to stay | intend to |
| I hope to start over. | I would like a new start | hope to |
| I plan to travel light. | with a small bag | plan to |
| I promise to keep you posted. | I will tell you the news | promise to |
| I do not want to lose touch. | I want us to stay in contact | want to |

**fix**
- ~~I intend to staying here.~~ → I intend to stay here.
- ~~I intend to stays here.~~ → I intend to stay here.

---

## 04 · want you to — what you want from someone

**body**
To say what you want another person to do: want / need + person + to + action. I want you to read aloud. Not want that you, and not your — use the person: you, him, her.

**formula**
`I want / need` · `person` · `to` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I want you to read aloud. | please read it out | you to |
| I need you to straighten the rug. | please fix the rug | you to |
| I want you to tell the truth. | please be honest | you to |
| I need you to pay attention. | please listen carefully | you to |
| I want you to wait your turn. | please do not go first | you to |

**fix**
- ~~I want that you read aloud.~~ → I want you to read aloud.
- ~~I want your to approve the plan.~~ → I want you to approve the plan.
- ~~I want you approve the plan.~~ → I want you to approve the plan.

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Say that you want the other person to read aloud.
- answer: `I` · `want` · `you` · `to` · `read` · `aloud`
- slot labels: none (long phrase)
- extra tiles: `that` · `your`

## 05 · to — the reason

**body**
To say why you do something, just use to + action: I wear a coat to keep warm. Do not use for here: for learn is a mistake.

**formula**
`what I do` · `to` · `why: action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I wear a coat to keep warm. | so I am not cold | to keep |
| I join a study group to learn English. | that is the reason I join | to learn |
| I leave early to beat the traffic. | before the roads are busy | to beat |
| I compare prices to save money. | so I pay less | to save |
| I take notes to remember the ideas. | so I do not forget | to remember |

**fix**
- ~~I join a study group for learn English.~~ → I join a study group to learn English.
- ~~I join a study group to learning English.~~ → I join a study group to learn English.

**drill · binary**
- question: It is cold outside. Explain why you wear a coat.
- A: `I wear a coat for keep warm.`
- B: `I wear a coat to keep warm.`
- correct: B
- explain: The reason is to + action; for does not work here.

## 06 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I enjoy to explore new places.~~ → I enjoy exploring new places.
- ~~I intend to staying here.~~ → I intend to stay here.
- ~~I want that you read aloud.~~ → I want you to read aloud.
- ~~I join a study group for learn English.~~ → I join a study group to learn English.
- ~~The no smoking sign means you mustn't to smoke.~~ → The no smoking sign means you mustn't smoke.

**tip**
-ing or to? The first word decides. enjoy, finish, avoid, mind — -ing. want, need, hope, plan, promise — to. Learn them in pairs: enjoy exploring, want to stay.

---

## 07 · What to take into practice

**body**
Enjoy, finish, avoid — + -ing. Want, hope, plan — + to. What you want from someone — want you to. The reason — to. After to, always the action as in the dictionary.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I enjoy making friends. | I like meeting new people | enjoy making |
| I need to check the timetable. | look at the times | need to |
| I want to make you laugh. | I want you to be happy | want to |
