# Theory · Lesson 14 of the new course — «I was reading when you called» (EN)

> Session «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Same sections (01–10), English phrases and drills as
> `lesson_14_ru.md`. Plain English for learners whose own language is not in the app. Common traps:
> I was read / I reading, They was…, I didn't playing, Did you playing…?, I cleaned … when you were
> calling, when / while mixed up. The second column explains the meaning instead of translating.
> Needs a native speaker's read-through.

**Screen title:** Theory · What was happening when…

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
In lesson 9 you said what is happening now: I am reading. Now you'll say what was happening at a moment in the past: «I was reading a comic book», «I was cleaning my boots when you called», «she was sniffing the flowers while I was talking».

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was reading a comic book. | At that moment, I was in the middle of reading. | was reading |
| They were doing a jigsaw puzzle. | At that moment, they were putting the puzzle together. | were doing |
| I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called. | Your call came in the middle of my cleaning. | when |
| She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking. | Both things went on at the same time. | while |

---

## 02 · was / were + action with -ing *(open at start)*

**body**
This is lesson 9 moved into the past: use was / were instead of am / is / are, and keep the -ing. I was reading — I was in the middle of reading. They were doing a puzzle.

**formula**
`who` · `was / were` · `action + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was reading a comic book. | At that moment, I was in the middle of reading. | was reading |
| She was using a colouring book. | She was colouring pictures. | was using |
| I was cooking fried rice. | I was in the middle of cooking rice. | was cooking |
| We were eating a spring roll. | We were in the middle of eating. | were eating |
| They were watching the soap bubbles. | Their eyes were on the bubbles. | were watching |

**fix**
- ~~I was read a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~I reading a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~They was doing a jigsaw puzzle.~~ → They were doing a jigsaw puzzle.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `They ___ doing a jigsaw puzzle.`
- options: `was` · `were` · `are`
- answer: `were`
- why: They in the past takes were; the -ing is already there.

## 03 · «Not»: wasn't, weren't

**body**
Put «not» after the clip, as always: I wasn't playing, they weren't watching. wasn't means was not, weren't means were not. The -ing stays.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle. | I was doing something else at that time. | wasn't |
| I wasn't writing in the homework diary. | My homework diary stayed closed. | wasn't |
| I wasn't watching the robin. | My eyes weren't on the bird. | wasn't |

**fix**
- ~~I didn't playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.
- ~~I wasn't play on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.

---

## 04 · Questions: Was…? Were…?

**body**
Put the clip first, like in lessons 2 and 9: Were you playing in the paddling pool? Was he watching the owl? Don't use did here: Did you playing is wrong.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `who` · `action + ing` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Were you playing in the paddling pool? | You ask what they were doing at that time. | Were |
| Was he jumping on the trampoline? | You ask what he was doing at that time. | Was |
| Were you using the workbook? | You ask if the workbook was open in front of them. | Were |
| Was he watching the owl? | You ask if his eyes were on the owl. | Was |

**fix**
- ~~Did you playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~You were playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?

**drill · binary**
- question: Ask if he was watching the owl at that time. Which is correct?
- A: `Did he watching the owl?`
- B: `Was he watching the owl?`
- correct: B
- explanation: With -ing, the question starts with was or were, not did.

## 05 · The background of a story

**body**
Use this form to paint the background: what people were doing when the story started. I was changing a flat tyre. It's not a single event, but something that was going on.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was changing a flat tyre. | I was in the middle of putting on a new tyre. | was changing |
| He was checking the windscreen wiper. | He was looking at the wiper on the car window. | was checking |
| We were cleaning the rear-view mirror. | We were in the middle of cleaning the mirror. | were cleaning |
| She was packing the nappy bag. | She was putting things into the baby bag. | was packing |

## 06 · when — something happened in the middle

**body**
If something happened in the middle of an action, the long action uses was / were + -ing, and the short event uses the simple past after when: I was cleaning my boots when you called. The call was short and stopped the cleaning.

**formula**
`was / were + ing` · `when` · `who` · `past event`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called. | Your call came in the middle of my cleaning. | when you called |
| She was packing her ballet flats when I called. | My call came while she was packing. | when I called |
| We were looking for an insole when you arrived. | You came in while we were looking. | when you arrived |
| She was drawing a piece of driftwood when I arrived. | I came in while she was drawing. | when I arrived |

**fix**
- ~~I cleaned my ankle boots when you were calling.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.
- ~~I was cleaning my ankle boots when you were call.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Say that you were looking for an insole when they arrived.
- answer: `We` · `were` · `looking` · `for` · `an` · `insole` · `when` · `you` · `arrived`
- slot labels: none (the sentence is long)
- extra tiles: `was` · `arriving`

## 07 · while — two actions at the same time

**body**
while means «during the time that». Use it when two actions were going on at the same time. Both parts use was / were + -ing: She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking.

**formula**
`was / were + ing` · `while` · `who` · `was / were + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was looking up while you were taking a photo. | Both things happened at the same time. | while |
| She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking. | Both things happened at the same time. | while |
| We were giggling while he was telling the story. | We laughed quietly during his story. | while |
| I was cutting celery while you were cooking. | We were both busy in the kitchen at once. | while |

**fix**
- ~~She was sniffing the flowers while I talked.~~ *(all the time)* → She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking.

---

## 08 · when or while?

**body**
A short event that interrupts: when + simple past — when you called. A long action at the same time: while + was / were + -ing — while you were cooking.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We were watching a wasp when you called. | Your call came in the middle. | when |
| I was watching a butterfly while you were taking a photo. | Both things went on together. | while |
| I was turning around when you called my name. | You said my name as I turned. | when |

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `I was cutting celery ___ you were cooking.`
- options: `when` · `while` · `did`
- answer: `while`
- why: Two actions were going on at the same time: while.

---

## 09 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I was read a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~They was doing a jigsaw puzzle.~~ → They were doing a jigsaw puzzle.
- ~~I didn't playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.
- ~~Did you playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~Are you using the workbook?~~ *(about the past)* → Were you using the workbook?
- ~~I cleaned my ankle boots when you were calling.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.

**tip**
An action going on in the past: was / were + -ing. A short event that interrupted it: simple past after when. did doesn't go with -ing.

## 10 · What to take into practice

**body**
Going on in the past: was / were + -ing. «Not»: wasn't / weren't. Questions: Was…? Were…? Interrupted: when + simple past. At the same time: while + was / were + -ing.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was filling a spray bottle. | I was putting water into a spray bottle. | was filling |
| Were you taking a photo of the woodpecker? | You ask what they were doing at that time. | Were |
| I was looking at a starfish when you called. | Your call came while I was looking. | when |
| I was using a glue stick while you were drawing. | We were both busy at the same time. | while |
