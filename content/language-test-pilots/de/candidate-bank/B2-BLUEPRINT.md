## B2 boundary

- Reading keys depend on integrating explicit relations, stance, evidence, or one recoverable assumption; C1-level dense multi-source critique remains out of scope.
- Pragmatic keys satisfy three or four named constraints and use broadly standard professional German.
- Grammar requires discourse-sensitive multi-clause control but not literary or archaic constructions.
- Vocabulary is precise and register-aware without specialist law, science, or local bureaucracy deciding correctness.
- Distractors remain locally plausible and fail one documented relation or component.
- EN/RU service copy and individually authored Russian distractor rationales are mandatory.
- `reviewStatus` remains `self_checked`; qualified external review remains `pending`.
- B2 cannot enter `completedLevels` before item-by-item linguistic review, structural gates, and adaptive-fit review pass.
