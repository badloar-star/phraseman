# future_present_continuous_arrangements draft

Status: Jesse reworked.

Training id: `future_present_continuous_arrangements`
Category: `verb`
Priority: `45`

Single learner problem:
The learner treats `am/is/are + -ing` only as an action happening now and misses that a future time marker can turn it into an arranged future plan.

Core contrast:
- `I am meeting John tomorrow` = arranged future plan.
- `I am calling him now` = action happening now.
- `I will answer it` = decision made now.
- `I am going to start` = intention.

What this training must not become:
- Not a broad future tenses lesson.
- Not the main `will` vs `going to` training.
- Not a spelling lesson for `-ing`.
- Not a timetable Present Simple lesson.

Learner-facing copy rule:
- RU/UK copy explains through time marker and arrangement: `завтра/сьогодні ввечері/наступного тижня`, `уже договорено/вже домовлено`.
- Avoid internal labels such as `subject`, `object`, `base verb`, `main verb`, `auxiliary`, and `modal`.
- Keep the live contrasts visible: `I am meeting tomorrow`, `I am calling now`, `I will answer it`, `I am going to start`.

Required contract anchors:
- `future_pc_easy_001`: wrong `meeting` feedback mentions `am meeting`.
- `future_pc_contrast_001`: wrong `are flying` feedback mentions `is`.
- `future_pc_contrast_004`: wrong `Will` feedback mentions `Are you working`.
- `future_pc_mixed_002`: wrong `am answering` feedback mentions `will answer`.

Final learner outcome:
The learner can decide:
`tomorrow/tonight/on Monday + arranged plan` -> `am/is/are + -ing`
`now` -> current action
`decision as you speak` -> `will`
`intention` -> `going to`
