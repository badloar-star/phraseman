## C2 boundary

- Every key requires near-native control of scope, stance, information structure, idiomaticity, or institutional interaction; specialist trivia is never the deciding factor.
- Reading answers must be recoverable from the supplied text, while sophisticated distractors fail one exact scope, voice, causal, or inferential constraint.
- Pragmatic keys coordinate all named constraints and remain plausible under high-stakes professional use.
- Grammar tests discourse-conditioned acceptability, not obscure prescriptive shibboleths.
- Vocabulary tests precise standard usage and register, not rare-word recognition alone.
- EN/RU service copy and individually authored Russian distractor rationales are mandatory.
- `reviewStatus` remains `self_checked`; qualified independent linguistic review remains `pending`.
- C2 cannot enter `completedLevels` before item-by-item review, structural gates, adaptive-fit review, C1/C2 boundary review, and production integration audit pass.
