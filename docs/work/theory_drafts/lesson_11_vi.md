# Lý thuyết · Bài 11 của khóa mới — «I was, they were, there was» (VI)

> Phiên «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Mục 01–11, câu tiếng Anh và bài tập giống `lesson_11_ru.md`.
> Bẫy của người Việt: tiếng Việt không đổi động từ cho quá khứ, chỉ thêm «hôm qua» (→ Yesterday I am
> at home — lỗi chính), «có» (→ Yesterday have two pegs), câu hỏi «có … không?» giữ trật tự
> (→ It was warm?), «vào Chủ nhật» (→ in Sunday). Xưng hô: bạn. Cần người bản ngữ đọc lại.

**Tiêu đề màn hình:** Lý thuyết · Hôm qua thế nào

---

## 01 · Bạn sẽ học nói gì *(mở sẵn)*

**body**
Đến giờ bạn nói về hiện tại: I am at home. Giờ bạn sẽ học kể về quá khứ: «hôm qua tôi ở nhà», «Giáng sinh chúng tôi ở cùng nhau», «trong túi có hai cái kẹp». Tiếng Anh đổi từ nối sang dạng quá khứ.

**examples** 🔊
| en | vi | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Hôm qua tôi ở nhà. | was |
| We were together at Christmas. | Giáng sinh chúng tôi ở cùng nhau. | were |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | Trong túi có hai cái kẹp phơi đồ. | There were |
| Was it warm in spring? | Mùa xuân có ấm không? | Was |

---

## 02 · was và were *(mở sẵn)*

**body**
Tiếng Việt chỉ thêm «hôm qua», động từ không đổi. Tiếng Anh thì phải đổi: am và is thành was, are thành were. I was, she was — you were, we were, they were. Không nói Yesterday I am.

**formula**
`I / he / she / it` · `was` — `you / we / they` · `were`

**examples** 🔊
| en | vi | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Hôm qua tôi ở nhà. | was |
| She was in London last year. | Năm ngoái cô ấy ở London. | was |
| We were at home last night. | Tối qua chúng tôi ở nhà. | were |
| They were in London at New Year. | Dịp năm mới họ ở London. | were |
| The hairdresser was busy yesterday. | Hôm qua thợ làm tóc bận. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday I am at home.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~I were at home yesterday.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.

**drill · choice**
- câu: `We ___ at the train station yesterday.`
- lựa chọn: `was` · `were` · `are`
- đáp án: `were`
- vì sao: Với we ở quá khứ là were.

---

## 03 · Dấu hiệu quá khứ: yesterday, last night, on Sunday

**body**
Thường trong câu có từ chỉ quá khứ: yesterday — «hôm qua», last night — «tối qua», last year — «năm ngoái». Ngày lễ dùng at: at Christmas, at New Year. Thứ trong tuần dùng on: on Sunday.

**examples** 🔊
| en | vi | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home on Sunday. | Chủ nhật tôi ở nhà. | on Sunday |
| We were together at Christmas. | Giáng sinh chúng tôi ở cùng nhau. | at Christmas |
| They were in London at New Year. | Dịp năm mới họ ở London. | at New Year |
| The sports centre was closed yesterday. | Hôm qua trung tâm thể thao đóng cửa. | yesterday |

**fix**
- ~~I was at home in Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.
- ~~We were together in Christmas.~~ → We were together at Christmas.

---

## 04 · Hôm qua — was, bây giờ — is

**body**
Nói về hôm qua — was hoặc were. Nói về bây giờ — is hoặc are, như trước. Từ yesterday hay now gợi ý ngay: Yesterday my towel was wet. The shower curtain is dry now.

**examples** 🔊
| en | vi | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday my beach towel was wet. | Hôm qua khăn tắm biển của tôi bị ướt. | was |
| Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty. | Hôm qua dép tông của tôi bị bẩn. | were |
| The shower curtain is dry now. | Rèm tắm bây giờ khô rồi. | is |
| The bathroom mirror was dirty yesterday. | Hôm qua gương phòng tắm bị bẩn. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~Yesterday my flip-flops was dirty.~~ → Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty.

**drill · binary**
- câu hỏi: Hôm qua khăn tắm bị ướt. Bạn nói thế nào?
- A: `Yesterday my beach towel is wet.`
- B: `Yesterday my beach towel was wet.`
- đúng: B
- giải thích: Hôm qua là quá khứ, nên dùng was.

## 05 · Thứ không đếm được — was

**body**
Giống bài 4, thứ không đếm từng cái — kem, đồ ăn, nước ép — đi với dạng số ít: was, không phải were. Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.

**examples** 🔊
| en | vi | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag. | Hôm qua kem chống nắng ở trong túi tôi. | was |
| The apple juice was cold yesterday. | Hôm qua nước táo lạnh. | was |
| The hot chocolate was too sweet yesterday. | Hôm qua sô-cô-la nóng ngọt quá. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday the sun cream were in my bag.~~ → Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.
- ~~The baby food were in the cupboard.~~ → The baby food was in the cupboard.

---

## 06 · «Không»: was not, were not

**body**
«Không» đặt ngay sau was hoặc were: It was not my day off. The fried egg was not cold. Không nói It not was.

**examples** 🔊
| en | vi | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It was not my day off. | Hôm đó không phải ngày nghỉ của tôi. | was not |
| The fried egg was not cold. | Trứng ốp la không bị nguội. | was not |
| The peanut butter was not on the table. | Bơ đậu phộng không ở trên bàn. | was not |

**fix**
- ~~It not was my day off.~~ → It was not my day off.
- ~~The fried egg didn't cold.~~ → The fried egg was not cold.

---

## 07 · Câu hỏi: Was…? Were…?

**body**
Tiếng Việt hỏi «có … không?» và giữ trật tự. Tiếng Anh đưa was hay were lên đầu: Was it warm in spring? Were you at home in autumn? Trả lời ngắn cũng bằng từ nối: Yes, it was. Không dùng did.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `ai / cái gì` · `…` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | vi | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Was it warm in spring? | Mùa xuân có ấm không? | Was |
| Were you at home in autumn? | Mùa thu bạn có ở nhà không? | Were |
| Was your pancake hot? | Bánh kếp của bạn có nóng không? | Was |
| Were the scrambled eggs hot? | Trứng bác có nóng không? | Were |
| Yes, it was. | Có. | was |

**fix**
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Were your pancake hot?~~ → Was your pancake hot?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.

**drill · word_bank**
- nhiệm vụ: Hãy hỏi: «Mùa xuân có ấm không?»
- đáp án: `Was` · `it` · `warm` · `in` · `spring`
- nhãn ô: không có (câu dài)
- thẻ thừa: `Were` · `Did`

---

## 08 · «Có» trong quá khứ: there was, there were

**body**
Tiếng Việt nói «trong túi có hai cái kẹp» cho cả hiện tại lẫn quá khứ. Tiếng Anh đổi: There was cho một vật, There were cho nhiều vật. Không dùng have.

**examples** 🔊
| en | vi | hi |
|---|---|---|
| There was a bed sheet on the bed. | Trên giường có một tấm ga. | There was |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | Trong túi có hai cái kẹp phơi đồ. | There were |
| There was a public toilet near the station. | Gần ga có nhà vệ sinh công cộng. | There was |
| There were two taxi drivers near the station. | Gần ga có hai tài xế taxi. | There were |
| There was fruit salad in the bowl. | Trong bát có sa-lát trái cây. | There was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday have two pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~There was two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.

**drill · choice**
- câu: `There ___ two duvet covers in the cupboard.`
- lựa chọn: `was` · `were` · `is`
- đáp án: `were`
- vì sao: Có hai cái vỏ chăn, nên There were.

---

## 09 · Đánh giá chuyện đã qua

**body**
Với was dễ nhận xét chuyện đã qua: That was a silly idea — «đó là một ý ngớ ngẩn», The plan was sensible — «kế hoạch hợp lý». Nhiều thứ — were: The rules were fair.

**examples** 🔊
| en | vi | hi |
|---|---|---|
| That was a silly idea. | Đó là một ý ngớ ngẩn. | was |
| The plan was sensible. | Kế hoạch đó hợp lý. | was |
| The rules were fair. | Luật lệ công bằng. | were |

---

## 10 · Những lỗi gần như ai cũng mắc

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday I am at home.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~Yesterday have two pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.
- ~~I was at home in Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.

**tip**
Ngữ pháp mới của bài chỉ có hai dạng: was cho I, he, she, it và một vật; were cho you, we, they và nhiều vật. Phần còn lại giống am, is, are.

## 11 · Mang gì vào phần luyện tập

**body**
Đã ở, đã là — was hoặc were. Không — was not, were not. Hỏi — Was…? Were…?, trả lời — Yes, it was. Đã có — There was / There were. Hôm qua — was, bây giờ — is.

**examples** 🔊
| en | vi | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She was in London last year. | Năm ngoái cô ấy ở London. | was |
| It was not my day off. | Hôm đó không phải ngày nghỉ của tôi. | was not |
| Were you at home in autumn? | Mùa thu bạn có ở nhà không? | Were |
| There was a soap dish next to the washbasin. | Cạnh bồn rửa có một cái đĩa xà phòng. | There was |
