## C1 boundary

- Reading keys depend on discourse relations, evidential scope, stance, or multi-step inference explicitly recoverable from the supplied text.
- Pragmatic keys satisfy every named component; distractors remain sophisticated and fail one decisive component.
- Grammar requires discourse-sensitive form selection or compression, not isolated conjugation.
- Vocabulary must be precise, broadly standard, and interpretable without specialist law or academic trivia.
- EN/RU service copy and individually authored Russian distractor rationales are mandatory.
- `reviewStatus` remains `self_checked`; qualified external review remains `pending`.
- C1 cannot enter `completedLevels` before item-by-item linguistic review, structural gates, adaptive-fit review, and B2/C1 boundary review pass.
