## Recommended real non-homophone sets

earring / hearing / steering. hearing /ˈhɪə.rɪŋ~ˈhɪr.ɪŋ/ adds initial /h/; steering /ˈstɪə.rɪŋ~ˈstɪr.ɪŋ/ adds initial /st/. Shared rest of word in corresponding varieties. Sources: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/hearing and https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/steering-wheel . The latter attests the word inside a compound. Give accurate meanings in wrong feedback, not before the choice.

necklace / reckless / legless. reckless /ˈrek.ləs/ differs in initial /r/ vs /n/; legless /ˈleɡ.ləs/ shares stress, vowel and final /ləs/, differs /l…g/ vs /n…k/. Sources: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/reckless and https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/legless . Exclude neckless (homophone). Do not equate English /r/ to Cyrillic р; describe honest contrast naturally.

Sources: https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/pronunciation/english/bracing and https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/graceless . Second is moderately close, first stronger.
