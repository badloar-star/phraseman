# Theory · Lesson 10 of the new course — «I usually buy… / I am buying… today» (EN)

> Session «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Same sections (01–13), English phrases and drills as
> `lesson_10_ru.md`. Plain English for learners whose own language is not in the app. Common traps:
> I buy … today (instead of am buying), I am swimming once a week, I am need / I am agree, I agree
> you, I am have a sore throat, one time a week, Is minus two, Good night as a greeting. The second
> column explains the meaning instead of translating. Needs a native speaker's read-through.

**Screen title:** Theory · Usually or now

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
In this lesson you put together what you already know and learn to choose: «I usually buy tinned tomatoes» or «I am buying a ready-made meal today». You also learn to say what hurts, what the weather is like and how to ask politely.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I usually buy tinned tomatoes. | That's my habit. | usually buy |
| I am buying a ready-made meal today. | Just today, not usually. | am buying |
| I have a sore throat. | My throat hurts. | have |
| It is quite cold. | It's rather cold. | quite |

---

## 02 · Usually — or now, for a while *(open at start)*

**body**
If it's a habit, use the form from lesson 6: I often travel abroad. If it's happening now or only for a while — on this trip, today — use the form from lesson 9: We are staying at the seaside.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I often travel abroad. | I go to other countries many times. | travel |
| We are staying at the seaside. | We're living by the sea for now. | are staying |
| I usually buy tinned tomatoes. | That's my habit. | buy |
| I am buying a ready-made meal today. | Just today, not usually. | am buying |

**fix**
- ~~I buy a ready-made meal today.~~ → I am buying a ready-made meal today.
- ~~I am often travelling abroad.~~ *(a habit)* → I often travel abroad.

**drill · binary**
- question: You usually cook, but today you got a ready-made meal. What do you say?
- A: `I buy a ready-made meal today.`
- B: `I am buying a ready-made meal today.`
- correct: B
- explanation: today is about today, not a habit: am buying.

---

## 03 · Clues in the sentence

**body**
Often the sentence itself tells you which form to use. often, usually, once a week, twice a week mean a habit — no -ing. today, now mean right now — am / is / are + -ing.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I swim once a week. | One time every week. | once a week |
| I swim twice a week. | Two times every week. | twice a week |
| I often get lost. | I can't find my way many times. | often |
| I am checking the forecast. | I'm looking at the weather news now. | am checking |

**fix**
- ~~I swim one time a week.~~ → I swim once a week.
- ~~I am swimming once a week.~~ → I swim once a week.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `I ___ twice a week.`
- options: `swim` · `am swimming` · `swimming`
- answer: `swim`
- why: twice a week is a habit, so no -ing.

## 04 · need, know, agree — no am, no -ing

**body**
Some words almost never take -ing or am: need, know, agree. Even if it's true right now, just say I need, I know, I agree. And agree with someone needs with: I agree with you.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I need a return ticket. | I must have a ticket there and back. | need |
| I only need milk. | Milk is the one thing I need. | need |
| I know the address. | I have the address in my head. | know |
| I agree with you. | I think the same as you. | agree |

**fix**
- ~~I am need a bin bag.~~ → I need a bin bag.
- ~~I am agree with you.~~ → I agree with you.
- ~~I agree you.~~ → I agree with you.

**drill · spot_slip**
- tiles: `I` · `am` · `agree` · `with` · `you`
- extra: `am` (index 1)
- hint: One word is extra. Tap it.
- explanation: agree is an action, no am: I agree with you.

---

## 05 · What hurts — have or has

**body**
To say part of your body hurts, use have with the problem: I have a sore throat. No am. With he and she, use has: She has earache, He has backache. Before earache, backache and toothache we usually don't put a.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I have a sore throat. | My throat hurts. | have |
| She has earache. | Her ear hurts. | has |
| He has backache. | His back hurts. | has |
| I have toothache. | My tooth hurts. | have |

**fix**
- ~~I am have a sore throat.~~ → I have a sore throat.
- ~~He have backache.~~ → He has backache.
- ~~He is has backache.~~ → He has backache.

---

## 06 · Small words: also, only, just, almost

**body**
also, only, just and almost go before an action: I also need bread, I only need milk. With the clip, they go after it: I am just looking, It is almost ready.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I also need bread. | I need bread too. | also |
| I only need milk. | Milk and nothing else. | only |
| I am just looking. | I'm not buying, only looking. | just |
| It is almost ready. | It's very nearly ready. | almost |

**fix**
- ~~I need also bread.~~ → I also need bread.
- ~~I just am looking.~~ → I am just looking.

---

## 07 · probably and perhaps

**body**
probably means «I think so». It goes after the clip: He is probably at home. perhaps means «maybe». It goes first: Perhaps he is on the bus. quite means «rather»: It is quite cold.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| He is probably at home. | I think he's at home. | probably |
| Perhaps he is on the bus. | Maybe he's on the bus. | Perhaps |
| It is quite cold. | It's rather cold. | quite |

**fix**
- ~~He probably is at home.~~ → He is probably at home.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `He is ___ at home.`
- options: `probably` · `perhaps` · `quite`
- answer: `probably`
- why: After is, use probably; perhaps goes at the start.

## 08 · When: at lunchtime, before bedtime

**body**
For a time of day, use at: at nine, at lunchtime, at midnight. For «before», use before: before bedtime. For «during the day», use in the daytime. Time goes at the end of the sentence.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I walk at lunchtime. | I walk in the middle of the day. | at lunchtime |
| I read before bedtime. | I read just before I sleep. | before bedtime |
| I work in the daytime. | I work during the day. | in the daytime |
| We reach the station at nine. | We get to the station at nine o'clock. | at nine |

**fix**
- ~~I walk in lunchtime.~~ → I walk at lunchtime.
- ~~I work at the daytime.~~ → I work in the daytime.

---

## 09 · Weather and temperature: It is…

**body**
For weather and temperature, use It is, as in lesson 1: It is quite cold, It is one degree, It is minus two. Don't leave out It.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It is one degree. | The temperature is 1°. | It is |
| It is minus two. | The temperature is −2°. | It is |
| It is quite cold. | It's rather cold. | It is |

**fix**
- ~~Is minus two.~~ → It is minus two.
- ~~It is one degrees.~~ → It is one degree.

---

## 10 · Polite questions: Can I…? Can you…?

**body**
To ask for something for yourself: Can I…? — Can I have your phone number? To ask someone to do something: Can you…? — Can you repeat that? To ask the price: How much does it cost? After can and does, the action has no to and no s.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Can I have your phone number? | Please give me your number. | Can I |
| Can you repeat that? | Please say it again. | Can you |
| How much does it cost? | What is the price? | does |

**fix**
- ~~Can I to have your phone number?~~ → Can I have your phone number?
- ~~Can you repeats that?~~ → Can you repeat that?
- ~~How much does it costs?~~ → How much does it cost?

---

## 11 · Greetings by time of day

**body**
Good morning is for the morning, Good afternoon for the afternoon, Good evening for the evening. Good night is not a greeting — you say it when you leave at night or go to bed.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Good morning. | Hello in the morning. | morning |
| Good afternoon. | Hello in the afternoon. | afternoon |
| Good evening. | Hello in the evening. | evening |
| Good night. | Goodbye before sleep. | night |

**fix**
- ~~Good night.~~ *(when you arrive in the evening)* → Good evening.

## 12 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I buy a ready-made meal today.~~ → I am buying a ready-made meal today.
- ~~I am swimming once a week.~~ → I swim once a week.
- ~~I am need a bin bag.~~ → I need a bin bag.
- ~~I agree you.~~ → I agree with you.
- ~~I am have a sore throat.~~ → I have a sore throat.
- ~~I need also bread.~~ → I also need bread.
- ~~How much does it costs?~~ → How much does it cost?

**tip**
The main question in this lesson: habit or now? Habit — like lesson 6. Now or for a while — am / is / are + -ing. need, know, agree and «what hurts» — never with am.
