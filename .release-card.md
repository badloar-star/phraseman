# Release card

## Type

fix

## Goal

Keep pearl purchase and gift balances fresh and bound to the active account so stale reads, delayed cloud receipts, or rapid repeated taps cannot charge or display the wrong account balance.

## Allowed changes

- Mobile TypeScript changes for pearl balance hydration, purchase/paywall revalidation, friend-gift spending, account-generation guards, and daily reward reconciliation.
- Focused mobile contract and runtime tests for those flows.
- Android-only production OTA publication on the current `16f7c046f` source lineage and runtime declared by the app (`1.6.4`).

## Forbidden changes

- No Firebase Functions, Firestore rules, indexes, Hosting, or admin deployment.
- No backend or admin source changes.
- No iOS OTA: the current iOS production update is on a separate hotfix lineage that is not yet integrated here.
- No unrelated cleanup, feature removal, native build, store submission, or rollback.

## Risk level

high — pearl spending and account transitions affect user-owned virtual value. A stale cross-account balance or duplicate action could charge the wrong account, and the OTA must remain Android-only to avoid regressing the separate iOS recovery lineage.

## Manual tests

- Smoke: after publication, open the Android production client, confirm the pearl balance renders without a zero flash, and open a pearl purchase sheet.
- Regression area: buy a flashcard pack, attempt a friend gift with sufficient and insufficient pearls, and claim the daily reward once.
- Upgrade from previous version: update an Android installation currently on the production store `9c753f172` lineage and verify the active account balance is retained.
- User type(s): existing free and paid Android users, including account-switch and poor-network cases.
- Platform-specific checks: confirm the EAS update group contains Android only and runtime `1.6.4`; confirm no iOS update was created.
- Performance check: confirm first-frame balance hydration and purchase-sheet opening remain immediate after the OTA applies.

## Diff review

- Mobile source changes are limited to balance hydration/revalidation, account-generation-safe writes, purchase/gift interaction locking, and their focused tests.
- Main risks are stale account-A work committing under account B, duplicate purchase/gift taps, and malformed or delayed daily receipts resolving the wrong journal entry.
- Automated focused and full release gates must pass before publication; post-publication Android smoke must cover purchase, gift, account switch, and offline recovery behavior.
