# Draft: verb_present_simple_negative_question

Status: draft for Jesse Pinkman room
Owner scope: this training id only

## Focus

One repair target: questions and negatives with `do`, `does`, `don't`, `doesn't`.

Target patterns:

- `Do you...?`
- `Does he...?`
- `I don't...`
- `He doesn't...`

Boundary:

- Do not teach broad tense theory.
- Do not expand into statements except as a tiny contrast: `He works.` vs `Does he work?`
- Do not train `am/is/are`, `can`, `did`, or long question-word order.

## Learner Explanation

`do/does` не переводится как делать; оно помогает построить вопрос или отрицание.

Use this learner-facing wording:

- С `I/you/we/they` в вопросе: `Do you work?`
- С `he/she/it` в вопросе: `Does he work?`
- С `I/you/we/they` в отрицании: `I don't work.`
- С `he/she/it` в отрицании: `He doesn't work.`
- После `does` и `doesn't` основной глагол идет без `-s`: `Does he work?`, `He doesn't work.`

## What The User Must Learn

- `Do you...?` starts a yes/no question about `you`.
- `Does he...?` starts a yes/no question about `he`.
- `I don't...` makes a negative sentence about `I`.
- `He doesn't...` makes a negative sentence about `he`.
- `Does he works?` is wrong because `does` already shows the `he` pattern; the next verb must be `work`.
- `He doesn't works.` is wrong for the same reason; after `doesn't`, use `work`.
- `Do you...?` is not `Are you...?` when the next word is an action like `work`, `like`, `live`, `know`.
- `He doesn't...` is not `He don't...`; `he` needs `doesn't`.

## Examples

| Correct | Learner note |
| --- | --- |
| `Do you work here?` | `you` uses `do` in a question. |
| `Does he work here?` | `he` uses `does`; after it, use `work`, not `works`. |
| `I don't work here.` | `I` uses `don't` in a negative. |
| `He doesn't work here.` | `he` uses `doesn't`; after it, use `work`, not `works`. |
| `Do they like tea?` | `they` uses `do` in a question. |
| `She doesn't like tea.` | `she` uses `doesn't`; after it, use `like`, not `likes`. |

## Exercise Set

Each task is single-choice. Every wrong option has specific feedback for that option.

### 1. Question With You

Sentence: `___ you work from home?`

Translation: `Ты работаешь из дома?`

Correct: `Do`

Wrong options:

- `Does` - `Does` is for `he/she/it`. Here the subject is `you`, so the question starts with `Do you`.
- `Are` - `Are you work` mixes `are` with an action verb. For the action `work`, use `Do you work`.
- `Do works` - This adds an extra verb shape. The question needs only `Do` before `you work`.

### 2. Question With He

Sentence: `___ he work on Monday?`

Translation: `Он работает в понедельник?`

Correct: `Does`

Wrong options:

- `Do` - `Do he` is the wrong match. With `he`, the question starts with `Does he`.
- `Is` - `Is he work` mixes `is` with the action `work`. Use `Does he work`.
- `Does works` - After `does`, the next verb must be without `-s`: `Does he work`.

### 3. Negative With I

Sentence: `I ___ like coffee.`

Translation: `Я не люблю кофе.`

Correct: `don't`

Wrong options:

- `doesn't` - `Doesn't` is for `he/she/it`. With `I`, use `don't`.
- `am not` - `Am not like` does not work with the action `like`. Use `I don't like`.
- `don't likes` - After `don't`, the verb stays without `-s`: `I don't like`.

### 4. Negative With He

Sentence: `He ___ like coffee.`

Translation: `Он не любит кофе.`

Correct: `doesn't`

Wrong options:

- `don't` - `Don't` is for `I/you/we/they`. With `he`, use `doesn't`.
- `isn't` - `Isn't like` does not work with the action `like`. Use `He doesn't like`.
- `doesn't likes` - After `doesn't`, the verb must be without `-s`: `He doesn't like`.

### 5. Question With You And Live

Sentence: `___ you live near here?`

Translation: `Ты живешь рядом?`

Correct: `Do`

Wrong options:

- `Does` - `Does` does not match `you`. Use `Do you live`.
- `Are` - `Are you live` uses the wrong helper for the action `live`. Use `Do you live`.
- `Do lives` - `Do lives` puts `-s` where it does not belong. Use `Do you live`.

### 6. Question With He And Live

Sentence: `___ he live near here?`

Translation: `Он живет рядом?`

Correct: `Does`

Wrong options:

- `Do` - `Do he` is not the right match. With `he`, use `Does he`.
- `Is` - `Is he live` is not the pattern for the action `live`. Use `Does he live`.
- `Does lives` - After `does`, do not add `-s` to `live`: `Does he live`.

### 7. Negative With I And Know

Sentence: `I ___ know the answer.`

Translation: `Я не знаю ответ.`

Correct: `don't`

Wrong options:

- `doesn't` - `Doesn't` is for `he/she/it`. With `I`, use `don't`.
- `am not` - `Am not know` does not work with the action `know`. Use `I don't know`.
- `don't knows` - After `don't`, the verb stays without `-s`: `I don't know`.

### 8. Negative With He And Know

Sentence: `He ___ know the answer.`

Translation: `Он не знает ответ.`

Correct: `doesn't`

Wrong options:

- `don't` - `Don't` does not match `he`. Use `He doesn't`.
- `isn't` - `Isn't know` does not work with the action `know`. Use `He doesn't know`.
- `doesn't knows` - After `doesn't`, the verb must be `know`, not `knows`.

### 9. Choose The Correct Question

Prompt: `Выбери правильный вопрос.`

Correct: `Do you understand?`

Wrong options:

- `Does you understand?` - `Does` does not match `you`; use `Do you`.
- `Are you understand?` - `Are` does not build this question with `understand`; use `Do you understand`.
- `Do you understands?` - After `do`, the verb must be `understand`, not `understands`.

### 10. Choose The Correct Question

Prompt: `Выбери правильный вопрос.`

Correct: `Does he understand?`

Wrong options:

- `Do he understand?` - `Do` does not match `he`; use `Does he`.
- `Is he understand?` - `Is` does not build this question with `understand`; use `Does he understand`.
- `Does he understands?` - After `does`, the verb must be `understand`, not `understands`.

### 11. Choose The Correct Negative

Prompt: `Выбери правильное отрицание.`

Correct: `I don't need help.`

Wrong options:

- `I doesn't need help.` - `Doesn't` does not match `I`; use `I don't`.
- `I am not need help.` - `Am not` does not work before the action `need`; use `I don't need`.
- `I don't needs help.` - After `don't`, the verb must be `need`, not `needs`.

### 12. Choose The Correct Negative

Prompt: `Выбери правильное отрицание.`

Correct: `He doesn't need help.`

Wrong options:

- `He don't need help.` - `Don't` does not match `he`; use `He doesn't`.
- `He isn't need help.` - `Isn't` does not work before the action `need`; use `He doesn't need`.
- `He doesn't needs help.` - After `doesn't`, the verb must be `need`, not `needs`.

### 13. Mixed Pair

Prompt: `Выбери правильную пару.`

Correct: `Do you work? / I don't work.`

Wrong options:

- `Does you work? / I don't work.` - The second part is right, but `Does you` is wrong; use `Do you`.
- `Do you works? / I don't work.` - The second part is right, but after `do`, use `work`, not `works`.
- `Do you work? / I doesn't work.` - The question is right, but `I` needs `don't`, not `doesn't`.

### 14. Mixed Pair

Prompt: `Выбери правильную пару.`

Correct: `Does he work? / He doesn't work.`

Wrong options:

- `Do he work? / He doesn't work.` - The negative is right, but `he` needs `does` in the question.
- `Does he works? / He doesn't work.` - The negative is right, but after `does`, use `work`, not `works`.
- `Does he work? / He don't work.` - The question is right, but `he` needs `doesn't` in the negative.

### 15. Error Recognition

Prompt: `Что нужно исправить в "Does he works here?"`

Correct: `works -> work`

Wrong options:

- `Does -> Do` - `Does` is correct for `he`; the error is `works` after `does`.
- `he -> you` - The subject can stay `he`; the error is the `-s` on `works`.
- `here -> at here` - The place phrase is not the target. The error is `works` after `does`.

### 16. Error Recognition

Prompt: `Что нужно исправить в "He doesn't works here."`

Correct: `works -> work`

Wrong options:

- `doesn't -> don't` - `Doesn't` is correct for `he`; the error is `works` after `doesn't`.
- `He -> I` - The subject can stay `he`; the error is the `-s` on `works`.
- `here -> at here` - The place phrase is not the target. The error is `works` after `doesn't`.

## Feedback Policy

- If the learner chooses `do` with `he`, say the subject match is wrong: `he` needs `does`.
- If the learner chooses `does` with `you`, say the subject match is wrong: `you` needs `do`.
- If the learner chooses `don't` with `he`, say the subject match is wrong: `he` needs `doesn't`.
- If the learner chooses `doesn't` with `I`, say the subject match is wrong: `I` needs `don't`.
- If the learner chooses a form with `-s` after `do/does/don't/doesn't`, say the next verb must be without `-s`.
- If the learner chooses `am/is/are` or `am not/isn't`, say that form does not build this sentence with action verbs like `work`, `like`, `live`, `know`, `need`, `understand`.

## Mastery Gate

- Minimum 10 correct answers.
- At least 4 correct in a row.
- Must answer at least one `Do you...?`, one `Does he...?`, one `I don't...`, and one `He doesn't...` item correctly.
- Must correct a previous mistake after seeing feedback.
- Repeat if the learner keeps choosing `does` with `you`, `do` with `he`, `doesn't` with `I`, `don't` with `he`, or `-s` after `does/doesn't`.
