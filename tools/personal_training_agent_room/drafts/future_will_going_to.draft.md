# future_will_going_to draft

Status: Jesse reworked.

Training id: `future_will_going_to`
Category: `verb`
Priority: `27`

Single learner problem:
The learner translates both `will` and `going to` as "буду" / "буду" and misses the real choice: instant decision, promise, prediction, existing plan, intention, or visible evidence.

Core contrast:
- `will` for decisions made now.
- `will` for promises.
- `will` for opinions and predictions.
- `going to` for existing plans.
- `going to` for intentions.
- `going to` for visible evidence.
- Avoid broken forms: `will to call`, `I going to study`, `will calling`, `going to studying`.

What this training must not become:
- Not a broad future-tense overview.
- Not a future arrangements lesson with Present Continuous.
- Not a conditionals lesson.
- Not a generic lesson on every possible nuance of future meaning.

Learner-facing copy rule:
- RU/UK copy explains through meaning: "решил сейчас", "обещаю", "думаю", "план уже есть", "видимые признаки".
- Avoid internal labels such as `subject`, `object`, `main verb`, `auxiliary`, and `modal`.
- One legacy contract anchor still contains `base verb` in a specific feedback string; copy sanitation handles this term.

Required contract anchors:
- `future_easy_001`: wrong `will to` feedback mentions `will help`.
- `future_contrast_001`: wrong `going to` feedback mentions `I am going to study`.
- `future_contrast_004`: wrong `to call` feedback mentions `will call`.
- `future_mixed_001`: wrong `will` feedback mentions visible evidence.
- `future_mixed_006`: wrong `I will calling...` feedback mentions `base verb`.

Final learner outcome:
The learner can choose:
`I will help` for promise.
`I will answer` for a decision now.
`I think it will rain` for opinion/prediction.
`I am going to study` for a plan.
`It is going to rain` for visible evidence.
