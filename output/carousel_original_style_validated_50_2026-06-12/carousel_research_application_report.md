## Carousel Patterns Applied

The research was applied as carousel structure, not as a new format:

- first slide must name a painful real-life English situation or a literal-translation mistake
- first slide must create a reason to continue: `ниже`, `дальше`, `сохрани`, `разбираем`, `проверь`, `держи`
- middle slides must deliver ready phrases or a concrete before/after contrast
- content must be adult-useful: work, doctor, bank, rent, airport, school, cafe, support, interviews, calls
- final slide must stay contextual and explain Phraseman without changing the visual system
