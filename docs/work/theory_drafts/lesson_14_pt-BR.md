# Teoria · Lição 14 do curso novo — «I was reading when you called» (PT-BR)

> Sessão «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Seções 01–10, frases em inglês e exercícios como em
> `lesson_14_ru.md`. Armadilhas de um brasileiro: «lia» vira a forma simples (→ I read a comic em
> vez de I was reading), sujeito omitido (→ Was reading), pergunta com did (→ Did you playing?),
> «quando» e «enquanto» confundidos. Tratamento: você. Precisa de revisão de um falante nativo.

**Título da tela:** Teoria · O que estava acontecendo quando…

---

## 01 · O que você vai aprender a dizer *(aberta de início)*

**body**
Na lição 9 você dizia o que acontece agora: I am reading. Agora vai contar o que estava acontecendo num momento do passado: «eu estava lendo um gibi», «eu estava limpando as botas quando você ligou», «ela cheirava as flores enquanto eu falava».

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was reading a comic book. | Eu estava lendo um gibi. | was reading |
| They were doing a jigsaw puzzle. | Eles estavam montando um quebra-cabeça. | were doing |
| I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called. | Eu estava limpando minhas botinhas quando você ligou. | when |
| She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking. | Ela cheirava as flores enquanto eu falava. | while |

---

## 02 · was / were + ação com -ing *(aberta de início)*

**body**
Aqui o português ajuda: «estava lendo» é I was reading. was / were fazem o papel de «estava», e -ing de «-ando / -endo». Atenção: o «lia» do português também costuma ser I was reading quando é sobre o que acontecia naquele momento.

**formula**
`quem` · `was / were` · `ação + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was reading a comic book. | Eu estava lendo um gibi. | was reading |
| She was using a colouring book. | Ela estava usando um livro de colorir. | was using |
| I was cooking fried rice. | Eu estava fazendo arroz frito. | was cooking |
| We were eating a spring roll. | A gente estava comendo um rolinho primavera. | were eating |
| They were watching the soap bubbles. | Eles estavam olhando as bolhas de sabão. | were watching |

**fix**
- ~~Was reading a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~I was read a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~They was doing a jigsaw puzzle.~~ → They were doing a jigsaw puzzle.

**drill · choice**
- frase: `They ___ doing a jigsaw puzzle.`
- opções: `was` · `were` · `are`
- resposta: `were`
- por quê: Com they no passado, were; o -ing já está lá.

---

## 03 · «Não»: wasn't, weren't

**body**
O «não» vai como sempre, depois da peça: I wasn't playing, they weren't watching. wasn't é was not, weren't é were not. O -ing fica.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle. | Eu não estava brincando no pula-pula. | wasn't |
| I wasn't writing in the homework diary. | Eu não estava escrevendo na agenda de lição de casa. | wasn't |
| I wasn't watching the robin. | Eu não estava olhando o pisco-de-peito-ruivo. | wasn't |

**fix**
- ~~I no was playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.
- ~~I didn't playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.

---

## 04 · Pergunta: Was…? Were…?

**body**
A pergunta: a peça na frente, como nas lições 2 e 9. Were you playing in the paddling pool? Was he watching the owl? Aqui não vai did: Did you playing é erro.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `quem` · `ação + ing` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Were you playing in the paddling pool? | Você estava brincando na piscininha? | Were |
| Was he jumping on the trampoline? | Ele estava pulando na cama elástica? | Was |
| Were you using the workbook? | Você estava usando a apostila? | Were |
| Was he watching the owl? | Ele estava olhando a coruja? | Was |

**fix**
- ~~Did you playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~You were playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?

**drill · binary**
- pergunta: Pergunte se ele estava olhando a coruja. Qual está certa?
- A: `Did he watching the owl?`
- B: `Was he watching the owl?`
- certa: B
- explicação: Com -ing, a pergunta começa com was ou were, não com did.

## 05 · O pano de fundo da história

**body**
Essa forma serve para pintar o fundo: o que as pessoas estavam fazendo quando a história começou. I was changing a flat tyre — «eu estava trocando um pneu furado». Não é um fato separado, é algo em andamento.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was changing a flat tyre. | Eu estava trocando um pneu furado. | was changing |
| He was checking the windscreen wiper. | Ele estava conferindo o limpador de para-brisa. | was checking |
| We were cleaning the rear-view mirror. | A gente estava limpando o retrovisor. | were cleaning |
| She was packing the nappy bag. | Ela estava arrumando a bolsa de fraldas. | was packing |

---

## 06 · when — algo aconteceu no meio

**body**
Se algo aconteceu no meio de um processo, o processo vai com was / were + -ing, e o fato no passado simples depois de when: I was cleaning my boots when you called. A ligação foi curta e interrompeu.

**formula**
`was / were + ing` · `when` · `quem` · `fato no passado`

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called. | Eu estava limpando minhas botinhas quando você ligou. | when you called |
| She was packing her ballet flats when I called. | Ela estava guardando as sapatilhas quando eu liguei. | when I called |
| We were looking for an insole when you arrived. | A gente estava procurando uma palmilha quando você chegou. | when you arrived |
| She was drawing a piece of driftwood when I arrived. | Ela estava desenhando um pedaço de madeira da praia quando cheguei. | when I arrived |

**fix**
- ~~I cleaned my ankle boots when you were calling.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.
- ~~I was cleaning my ankle boots when you were call.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.

**drill · word_bank**
- tarefa: Diga: «A gente estava procurando uma palmilha quando você chegou».
- resposta: `We` · `were` · `looking` · `for` · `an` · `insole` · `when` · `you` · `arrived`
- rótulos: nenhum (a frase é longa)
- fichas a mais: `was` · `arriving`

---

## 07 · while — dois processos ao mesmo tempo

**body**
while é «enquanto». Usa-se quando duas coisas estavam acontecendo ao mesmo tempo, e as duas levam was / were + -ing: She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking.

**formula**
`was / were + ing` · `while` · `quem` · `was / were + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was looking up while you were taking a photo. | Eu olhava para cima enquanto você tirava uma foto. | while |
| She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking. | Ela cheirava as flores enquanto eu falava. | while |
| We were giggling while he was telling the story. | A gente dava risadinhas enquanto ele contava a história. | while |
| I was cutting celery while you were cooking. | Eu cortava salsão enquanto você cozinhava. | while |

**fix**
- ~~She was sniffing the flowers while I talked.~~ *(o tempo todo)* → She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking.

---

## 08 · when ou while?

**body**
Um fato curto que interrompe: when + passado simples — when you called («quando você ligou»). Um processo longo ao mesmo tempo: while + was / were + -ing — while you were cooking («enquanto você cozinhava»).

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We were watching a wasp when you called. | A gente estava olhando uma vespa quando você ligou. | when |
| I was watching a butterfly while you were taking a photo. | Eu olhava uma borboleta enquanto você tirava uma foto. | while |
| I was turning around when you called my name. | Eu estava me virando quando você me chamou. | when |

**drill · choice**
- frase: `I was cutting celery ___ you were cooking.`
- opções: `when` · `while` · `did`
- resposta: `while`
- por quê: Dois processos ao mesmo tempo: while.

## 09 · Os erros que quase todo mundo comete

**fix**
- ~~Was reading a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~I was read a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~They was doing a jigsaw puzzle.~~ → They were doing a jigsaw puzzle.
- ~~I didn't playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.
- ~~Did you playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~I cleaned my ankle boots when you were calling.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.

**tip**
O que estava acontecendo: was / were + -ing. O fato curto que interrompeu: passado simples depois de when. Did não combina com -ing.

---

## 10 · O que levar para a prática

**body**
Algo em andamento: was / were + -ing, sempre com quem. «Não»: wasn't / weren't. Pergunta: Was…? Were…? Interrompeu: when + passado simples. Ao mesmo tempo: while + was / were + -ing.

**examples** 🔊
| en | pt-BR | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was filling a spray bottle. | Eu estava enchendo um borrifador. | was filling |
| Were you taking a photo of the woodpecker? | Você estava tirando foto do pica-pau? | Were |
| I was looking at a starfish when you called. | Eu estava olhando uma estrela-do-mar quando você ligou. | when |
| I was using a glue stick while you were drawing. | Eu usava a cola bastão enquanto você desenhava. | while |
