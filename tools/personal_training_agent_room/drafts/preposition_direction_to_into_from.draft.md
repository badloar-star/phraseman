# Draft: preposition_direction_to_into_from

## Scope

One concrete mistake type: the learner confuses the movement route behind
`to`, `into`, `from`, `out of`, and nearby `in` / `towards` choices.

This is not a broad preposition lesson. It repairs one decision:

> Draw the movement arrow first, then choose the small word.

## User-Facing Diagnosis

The user is likely translating "в", "из", "к", "на" too directly. The training
does not explain formal grammar categories. It teaches four pictures:

1. `to` - movement to a place or receiver.
2. `into` - movement inside.
3. `from` - starting point or source.
4. `out of` - from inside to outside.
5. `in` - already inside, no entry movement.

## Exercise Arc

1. Easy destination and receiver: `to work`, `to Dublin`, `to me`.
2. Inside movement: `into the room`, `into the bag`, `in` vs `into`.
3. Source: `from work`, `from Ukraine`, `from my friend`.
4. Exit from inside: `out of the car`, `out of the bag`.
5. Mixed review pairs and one full correction sentence.

## Beginner Standard

- No unexplained grammar jargon in RU/UK learner text.
- Every wrong answer has feedback tied to that option.
- Tone is calm: "почти", "стрелка", "готовый кусок".
- One mistake, one pattern, repeated through similar phrases.
