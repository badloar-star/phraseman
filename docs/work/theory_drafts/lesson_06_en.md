# Theory · Lesson 6 of the new course — «I work, she works» (EN)

> Session «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Same sections (01–11), English phrases and drills as
> `lesson_06_ru.md`. Plain English for learners whose own language is not in the app. Common traps:
> I am work, He drive, I works, She teachs, She studys, She is cooks, I start in two. The second
> column explains the meaning instead of translating. Needs a native speaker's read-through.

**Screen title:** Theory · What people do

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
So far the sentences were about what people are like and where they are: I am ready, it is here. Now they are about what people do: «I work in a pharmacy», «we mow the lawn», «he drives to work». These are things that happen again and again: jobs, habits, daily life.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I work in a pharmacy. | My job is in a pharmacy. | work |
| We mow the lawn. | We cut the grass. | mow |
| He drives to work. | He goes to work by car. | drives |
| She teaches English. | English is her job. | teaches |

---

## 02 · Who + action *(open at start)*

**body**
Here you don't need am, is or are. The action comes right after who: I work, we bake. The most common mistake is to keep the clip out of habit: I am work. That's wrong.

**formula**
`who` · `action` · `what / where / when`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I work in a pharmacy. | My job is in a pharmacy. | work |
| I bake bread. | I make bread in the oven. | bake |
| You sweep the floor. | You clean the floor with a brush. | sweep |
| We trim the hedge. | We cut the hedge to make it neat. | trim |
| They build a wall. | They make a wall. | build |

**fix**
- ~~I am work in a pharmacy.~~ → I work in a pharmacy.
- ~~We are trim the hedge.~~ → We trim the hedge.

**drill · binary**
- question: You say where you work. Which is correct?
- A: `I am work in a pharmacy.`
- B: `I work in a pharmacy.`
- correct: B
- explanation: With an action, you don't need am: I work.

---

## 03 · I, you, we, they — the action as in the dictionary

**body**
With I, you, we and they, the action doesn't change. It's the same as in the dictionary: work, wash, carry.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I wash the plates. | I clean the plates. | wash |
| You carry the trays. | You hold the trays and walk. | carry |
| We pick pears. | We take pears from the tree. | pick |
| They dig in the yard. | They make holes in the ground. | dig |
| We play football in the yard. | We kick a ball outside. | play |
| I ride a bicycle. | I go on a bike. | ride |

**fix**
- ~~We picks pears.~~ → We pick pears.
- ~~They digs in the yard.~~ → They dig in the yard.

---

## 04 · He, she, it — add s at the end

**body**
With he, she, it, or one person — the host, my brother — the action gets s: he drives, she waits, the host greets. This is the only change, so it's easy to forget. He drive is the most common mistake in this lesson.

**formula**
`he / she / it / one person` · `action + s`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| He drives to work. | He goes to work by car. | drives |
| She waits at the bus stop. | She stays there until the bus comes. | waits |
| She smiles at the baby. | She looks at the baby with a smile. | smiles |
| He tastes the soup. | He tries a little soup. | tastes |
| The host greets the guests. | The host says hello to the guests. | greets |
| It sleeps on the quilt. | The cat sleeps on the quilt. | sleeps |

**fix**
- ~~He drive to work.~~ → He drives to work.
- ~~She wait at the bus stop.~~ → She waits at the bus stop.
- ~~I works in a pharmacy.~~ → I work in a pharmacy.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `She ___ at the bus stop.`
- options: `wait` · `waits` · `is wait`
- answer: `waits`
- why: With she, the action gets s.

---

## 05 · When you need es

**body**
If the action ends in sh, ch, s or x, you can't just add s. Add es, and the word gets one more beat: wash → washes, catch → catches, teach → teaches, fish → fishes, mix → mixes.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She teaches English. | English is her job. | teaches |
| She catches the bus. | She gets on the bus in time. | catches |
| He fishes with a rod. | He catches fish with a long stick and line. | fishes |
| She washes the saucers. | She cleans the small plates. | washes |

**fix**
- ~~She teachs English.~~ → She teaches English.
- ~~He fishs with a rod.~~ → He fishes with a rod.

---

## 06 · When y becomes ies

**body**
If the action ends in y and there is a consonant before it, change y to ies: study → studies, carry → carries. If there is a vowel before y, just add s: play → plays.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She studies with a tutor. | A teacher helps her learn. | studies |
| My brother carries a bundle. | He holds a bundle and walks. | carries |
| He plays football. | He kicks a ball in a game. | plays |

**fix**
- ~~She studys with a tutor.~~ → She studies with a tutor.
- ~~He plaies football.~~ → He plays football.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `She ___ with a tutor.`
- options: `studys` · `studies` · `study`
- answer: `studies`
- why: There's a consonant (d) before y, so y becomes ies.

## 07 · For things that happen again and again

**body**
These sentences are about jobs, habits and things that repeat: I walk to work — usually, every day. They are not about what is happening right now, this second.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I walk to work. | I go to work on foot. | walk |
| I cycle to work. | I go to work by bike. | cycle |
| I exercise at home. | I do sport at home. | exercise |
| She lives in a flat. | Her home is a flat. | lives |
| They sell lamps. | Lamps are what they sell. | sell |

---

## 08 · Where and when — at the end

**body**
Where and when usually go at the end: I start at two, She reads in the armchair. Times go with at: at two, at five.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I start at two. | My work begins at two o'clock. | at |
| I arrive at five. | I get there at five o'clock. | at |
| She leaves at four. | She goes away at four o'clock. | at |
| She reads in the armchair. | She sits in the armchair with a book. | in |
| We jog at sunrise. | We run early, when the sun comes up. | at |

**fix**
- ~~I start in two.~~ → I start at two.
- ~~At two I start.~~ → I start at two.

---

## 09 · Action or clip — not both

**body**
If there is an action, there is no clip: She cooks. If there is no action and you say who or what someone is, you need the clip and a: She is an artist. Both together are wrong: She is cooks.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She is an artist. | Art is her job. | is |
| This artist paints portraits. | He makes pictures of people. | paints |
| He is a baker. | Making bread is his job. | is |
| The tailor repairs uniforms. | The tailor fixes uniforms. | repairs |
| I am a beginner. | I'm new at this. | am |

**fix**
- ~~She is cooks at home.~~ → She cooks at home.
- ~~He baker.~~ → He is a baker.

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Say that the tailor fixes uniforms.
- answer: `The` · `tailor` · `repairs` · `uniforms`
- slot labels: none (the sentence is long)
- extra tiles: `is` · `repair`

---

## 10 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I am work in a pharmacy.~~ → I work in a pharmacy.
- ~~He drive to work.~~ → He drives to work.
- ~~I works at home.~~ → I work at home.
- ~~She teachs English.~~ → She teaches English.
- ~~She studys with a tutor.~~ → She studies with a tutor.
- ~~She is cooks at home.~~ → She cooks at home.
- ~~I start in two.~~ → I start at two.

**tip**
Two checks. Is there an action? Then remove am, is, are. Is it he, she, it or one person? Then add s.

## 11 · What to take into practice

**body**
Who + action, no clip. I, you, we, they — the action as in the dictionary. He, she, it — add s, sometimes es or ies. Where and when at the end; times with at.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We pack the bags. | We put things into the bags. | pack |
| He coughs. | He has a cough. | coughs |
| She catches the bus. | She gets on the bus in time. | catches |
| She studies with a tutor. | A teacher helps her learn. | studies |
| I arrive at five. | I get there at five o'clock. | arrive |
