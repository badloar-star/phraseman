# past_continuous_basic draft

Status: Jesse reworked.

Training id: `past_continuous_basic`
Category: `verb`
Priority: `42`

Single learner problem:
The learner wants to say that an action was in progress at a past moment, but misses `was/were`, chooses the wrong one, or forgets `-ing`.

Core live chunks:
- `I was working at 8 yesterday.`
- `She was sleeping when I called.`
- `They were waiting outside.`
- `Were you working yesterday evening?`

Main contrast:
- `was + -ing` with `I/he/she/it`
- `were + -ing` with `you/we/they`
- process at a past moment vs simple past fact

What this training must not become:
- Not the full Past Simple vs Past Continuous contrast lesson.
- Not a broad past tense lesson.
- Not a question/negative-only lesson.
- Not a grammar-label lecture.

Learner-facing copy rule:
- RU/UK copy explains with "процесс в конкретный момент прошлого" / "процес у конкретний момент минулого".
- Avoid internal labels such as `subject`, `object`, `base verb`, `main verb`, `auxiliary`, and `modal`.
- Keep the visible chunks `was working`, `were waiting`, `at 8 yesterday`.

Required contract anchors:
- `past_cont_easy_001`: wrong `were` feedback mentions `was`.
- `past_cont_contrast_004`: wrong `I working at that moment.` feedback mentions `was`.
- `past_cont_contrast_005`: wrong `They were wait when I arrived.` feedback mentions `were waiting`.
- `past_cont_mixed_005`: wrong pair feedback mentions `At 8`.

Final learner outcome:
The learner can choose:
`I was working`
`they were waiting`
`I worked yesterday` for a fact
`I was working at 8 yesterday` for a process at a past moment.
