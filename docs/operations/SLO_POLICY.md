# SLO Policy

Numeric values in `CRITICAL_JOURNEYS.json` are proposed targets, not measured performance. A journey becomes enforceable only after a privacy-reviewed signal, a named human owner, a runbook, and at least two weeks of baseline evidence exist. Error budgets are consumed by user-visible failures, not by missing telemetry. Changes to a target require a dated decision record and an explicit debt follow-up when the baseline is unavailable.
