## Authoring contract

Each language contains the full A1–C2 staircase. Every level has 40 ordered, unique items with one stored answer, three documented distractors, English and Russian review metadata, a declared construct, CEFR rationale, ambiguity note, and deterministic difficulty prior. C1/C2 contain direct upper-band evidence required by the adaptive engine.

The manual pass checked the assessed-language stimulus and answer for grammar, idiomaticity, semantic correctness, register, attribution, temporal logic, and agreement with the declared construct. Defects found during the pass were corrected in source data and rebuilt; representative corrections included unnatural calques, article/case errors, misleading causal wording, incomplete decision conditions, and answer-length shortcuts.

## Release blockers

2. Apply reviewer corrections in source files, rebuild affected levels, and rerun every gate above.
3. Owner approval of the reviewed banks and UI-language/product release behavior.

Until those conditions are met, the correct safe state is the current one: complete candidate banks, reproducible and audited, but unpublished and isolated.

## External-review handoff readiness

Four self-contained, hash-bound work orders are available under `review-work-orders/`, one per language and 240 decisions each. Every row embeds the complete question snapshot and requires a unique external `reviewEvidenceId`. `scripts/language_test_external_review.mjs` generates a fresh work order from the exact candidate revision and rejects incomplete reviewer identity, missing external identity or qualification evidence, absent independence declaration or attestation, stale hashes, edited snapshots, missing or duplicate question decisions, missing or duplicate per-item evidence IDs, blank notes, and any status other than `approved`. Its focused contract passes 10/10 tests.
