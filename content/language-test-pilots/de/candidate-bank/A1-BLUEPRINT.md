## A1 boundary

- Each stimulus is short, concrete, and recoverable without specialist or cultural knowledge.
- Reading keys depend on one or two explicit facts, not hidden inference or distractor length.
- Pragmatic keys must be natural in the stated relationship and setting; wrong options fail the situation rather than merely sounding absurd.
- Grammar items test high-frequency beginner control in a meaningful utterance, not terminology recall.
- Vocabulary items use common internationally taught standard German and enough context to select one answer.
- EN/RU service copy and individually authored Russian distractor rationales are mandatory.
- `reviewStatus` remains `self_checked`; qualified external review remains `pending`.
- A1 cannot enter `completedLevels` before item-by-item linguistic review, structural gates, and adaptive-fit review pass.
