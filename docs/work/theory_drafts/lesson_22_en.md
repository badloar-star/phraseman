## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open from the start)*

**body**
This lesson is about short words that replace people and things: him and her after an action, it for a thing, mine for “belongs to me”, yourself, the … one, and another.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I can advise him. | give advice to a man | him |
| This phone case is mine. | it belongs to me | mine |
| You can introduce yourself. | say who you are | yourself |
| I want the hooded one. | the item with a hood | one |
| I need another payment method. | a different way to pay | another |

---

## 02 · him, her, them — after the action *(open from the start)*

**body**
When a person comes after the action, he, she, they change to him, her, them: I can advise him. Me and you can also come after the action: I do not want to disappoint you.

**formula**
`action` · `me / you / him / her / us / them`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I can advise him. | give him advice | him |
| I want to congratulate her. | say well done to her | her |
| I admire them. | I think they are great | them |
| I do not want to disappoint you. | I do not want you to feel let down | you |
| This story can amuse them. | make them laugh | them |

**fix**
- ~~I can advise he.~~ → I can advise him.
- ~~I can advise his.~~ → I can advise him.
- ~~I want to congratulate she.~~ → I want to congratulate her.

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `I want to support ___.`
- options: `her` · `she` · `hers`
- answer: `her`
- why: After the action, a woman is her.

## 03 · it and them — for things

**body**
After the action, one thing is it and several things are them: Could I unscrew it? Could I untangle them? Not its, not they.

**formula**
`action` · `it (one thing) / them (several)`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Could I unscrew it? | take the screw out | it |
| Could I untangle them? | separate the knotted things | them |
| Could I strain it? | remove the water | it |
| I can calculate it. | work out the number | it |
| I can round it down. | make the number lower | it |

**fix**
- ~~Could I untangle they?~~ → Could I untangle them?
- ~~Could I unscrew its?~~ → Could I unscrew it?

## 04 · mine, yours, ours — with no thing after

**body**
When the thing is already named and you say who it belongs to, use mine, yours, his, hers, ours: This phone case is mine. my always needs a thing after it.

**formula**
`this` · `is` · `mine / yours / his / hers / ours`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This phone case is mine. | it belongs to me | mine |
| This travel mug is yours. | it belongs to you | yours |
| This rain jacket is ours. | it belongs to us | ours |
| This yoga mat is hers. | it belongs to her | hers |
| This electric razor is his. | it belongs to him | his |

**fix**
- ~~This phone case is my.~~ → This phone case is mine.
- ~~This phone case is me.~~ → This phone case is mine.

**drill · binary**
- question: You point at your friend's mat. How do you say it belongs to her?
- A: `This yoga mat is her.`
- B: `This yoga mat is hers.`
- correct: B
- explain: Belonging to her, with no thing after: hers.

## 05 · yourself — the same person

**body**
When the action goes back to the same person, use yourself: You can introduce yourself. Just you is wrong here.

**formula**
`you` · `action` · `yourself`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| You can introduce yourself. | say your own name | yourself |
| You need to look after yourself. | take care of your own health | yourself |
| You should not blame yourself. | it is not your fault | yourself |
| You can be yourself. | act naturally | yourself |
| You can forgive yourself. | stop feeling bad | yourself |

**fix**
- ~~You can introduce you.~~ → You can introduce yourself.
- ~~You need to look after you.~~ → You need to look after yourself.

## 06 · one and ones — not repeating the thing

**body**
To avoid naming the thing again, say the + describing word + one (one thing) or ones (several): I want the hooded one. it does not work here.

**formula**
`the` · `describing word` · `one / ones`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I want the hooded one. | the jacket with a hood | one |
| I like the patterned ones. | the ones with a pattern | ones |
| I prefer the padded one. | the soft, thick one | one |
| I want the slip-on ones. | shoes with no laces | ones |
| I want the ready-to-eat one. | you can eat it now | one |

**fix**
- ~~I want the hooded it.~~ → I want the hooded one.

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Say that you like the ones with a pattern.
- answer: `I` · `like` · `the` · `patterned` · `ones`
- slot labels: none (long phrase)
- extra tiles: `one` · `it`

## 07 · another — one more, a different one

**body**
When you need one new option instead of this one: another + one thing. I need another payment method. Do not use other alone with one thing.

**formula**
`another` · `one thing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I need another payment method. | a different way to pay | another |
| I need another meeting place. | a different place to meet | another |
| We use another departure gate. | a different gate | another |
| I want another room type. | a different kind of room | another |

**fix**
- ~~I need other payment method.~~ → I need another payment method.
- ~~I need others payment method.~~ → I need another payment method.

## 08 · Review: Could I…? and to — the reason

**body**
From earlier lessons: Could I…? asks politely; to + action gives the reason. Could I book a table? I use an umbrella to stay dry.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Could I book a table? | a polite request | Could I |
| Could I get some fresh air? | go outside for a moment | Could I |
| I use an umbrella to stay dry. | the reason | to stay |
| I fill the kettle to boil the water. | the reason | to boil |

## 09 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I can advise he.~~ → I can advise him.
- ~~Could I untangle they?~~ → Could I untangle them?
- ~~This phone case is my.~~ → This phone case is mine.
- ~~You can introduce you.~~ → You can introduce yourself.
- ~~I need other payment method.~~ → I need another payment method.

**tip**
After the action — him, her, them, it. Belongs to me, no thing after — mine. The same person — yourself. Do not repeat the thing — one or ones. A different one — another.
