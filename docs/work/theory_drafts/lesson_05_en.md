## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
In this lesson you learn to say who has what and whose things are: «I have a brother», «this is my mug», «the helmet is mine», «the mug is my husband's», «whose keyboard is this?». English uses a few different words for this.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She has a brother. | There is a brother in her family. | has |
| This is my mug. | The mug belongs to me. | my |
| The helmet is mine. | The helmet belongs to me. | mine |
| The mug is my husband's. | The mug belongs to my husband. | 's |
| Whose keyboard is this? | Who does the keyboard belong to? | Whose |

## 02 · I have *(open at start)*

**body**
To say something belongs to you, say I have: I have a key. Don't use there is for this — there is means something is in a place, not that it's yours.

**formula**
`who` · `have / has` · `what`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I have a key. | The key is mine. | have |
| We have a sister. | There is a sister in our family. | have |
| I have a computer. | A computer is mine. | have |
| They have wallets. | Each of them has a wallet. | have |
| I have a guest. | Someone is visiting me. | have |

**fix**
- ~~There is a key to me.~~ → I have a key.
- ~~I have key.~~ → I have a key.

---

## 03 · have or has

**body**
With he, she, it, or one person or thing, use has: she has, the hotel has. With everyone else, use have: I have, you have, we have, they have. It's the same pair as is and are.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She has a baby. | A baby is hers. | has |
| He has a cat. | A cat lives with him. | has |
| The hotel has a dog. | A dog lives at the hotel. | has |
| The desk has paper. | There is paper on the desk. | has |
| The twins have a dog. | The two twins share a dog. | have |
| My nephew has a puppy. | A puppy belongs to my nephew. | has |

**fix**
- ~~She have a brother.~~ → She has a brother.
- ~~I has a key.~~ → I have a key.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `She ___ a baby.`
- options: `have` · `has` · `is`
- answer: `has`
- why: With she, use has.

## 04 · my, your, his, her — before the thing

**body**
my, your, his and her go before the thing and never change: my mug, my pencils — for one mug or many pencils. There is no mys.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This is my mug. | The mug belongs to me. | my |
| Your tissue is in my pocket. | I have the tissue that belongs to you. | Your |
| Her lunchbox is in the fridge. | The lunchbox that belongs to her. | Her |
| His tattoo is small. | The tattoo on him is small. | His |
| These are my pencils. | The pencils belong to me. | my |

**fix**
- ~~This is mine mug.~~ → This is my mug.
- ~~These are mys pencils.~~ → These are my pencils.

---

## 05 · his or her

**body**
his is for a man, her is for a woman. Look at who owns the thing, not at the thing itself.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| His tattoo is small. | The tattoo belongs to a man. | His |
| Her ponytail is long. | The ponytail belongs to a woman. | Her |
| This is her puppy. | The puppy belongs to a woman. | her |
| It is her bookcase. | The bookcase belongs to a woman. | her |

**drill · binary**
- question: You're talking about a girl's ponytail. Which is correct?
- A: `His ponytail is long.`
- B: `Her ponytail is long.`
- correct: B
- explanation: It belongs to a girl, so her.

---

## 06 · our, their, its

**body**
our is for you and others, their is for other people. its is for a thing or an animal: its collar — the puppy's collar. its has no apostrophe. It's with an apostrophe means it is.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Our printer is next to the window. | The printer we share. | Our |
| Its collar is red. | The puppy's collar is red. | Its |
| Its tail is short. | The puppy's tail is short. | Its |
| Where is my laptop? | I can't find the laptop that belongs to me. | my |

**fix**
- ~~It's collar is red.~~ → Its collar is red.
- ~~Its red.~~ *(meaning «it is red»)* → It's red.

**tip**
Test: can you say it is? Then write it's. If not, write its.

---

## 07 · mine, yours, hers — when the thing was already said

**body**
If the thing was already said, put mine at the end instead: The helmet is mine. Compare: my helmet, but The helmet is mine. After mine, don't repeat the thing.

**formula**
`my → mine` · `your → yours` · `his → his` · `her → hers` · `our → ours` · `their → theirs`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| The helmet is mine. | It belongs to me. | mine |
| The guitar is yours. | It belongs to you. | yours |
| The razor is his. | It belongs to him. | his |
| The perfume is hers. | It belongs to her. | hers |
| The kite is ours. | It belongs to us. | ours |
| The puzzle is theirs. | It belongs to them. | theirs |

**fix**
- ~~The helmet is my.~~ → The helmet is mine.
- ~~The perfume is her.~~ → The perfume is hers.
- ~~This is mine helmet.~~ → This is my helmet.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `The guitar is ___.`
- options: `your` · `yours` · `you`
- answer: `yours`
- why: The thing was already said, so at the end it's yours.

## 08 · Someone's thing: my husband's

**body**
To say a thing belongs to a person, add 's to the person: my husband's mug. First the person, then the thing. If the thing was already said, you can leave it out: The mug is my husband's.

**formula**
`person + 's` · `thing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| The mug is my husband's. | It belongs to my husband. | 's |
| This is my sister's thermos. | The thermos belongs to my sister. | 's |
| The dog is the neighbour's. | It belongs to the neighbour. | 's |
| My aunt's piano is old. | The piano that belongs to my aunt is old. | 's |
| The baby's bib is in my sister's bag. | The baby's bib is in the bag that belongs to my sister. | 's |

**fix**
- ~~The mug of my husband.~~ → My husband's mug.
- ~~The dog is the neighbour.~~ → The dog is the neighbour's.

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Say the thermos belongs to your sister: «This is …»
- answer: `This` · `is` · `my` · `sister's` · `thermos`
- slot labels: none (the sentence is long)
- extra tiles: `sister` · `of`

## 09 · Whose

**body**
To ask who something belongs to, start with Whose and put the thing right after it: Whose keyboard is this? For more than one thing, use are these: Whose gloves are these?

**formula**
`Whose` · `thing` · `is this / are these` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Whose keyboard is this? | Who does it belong to? | Whose |
| Whose gloves are these? | Who do they belong to? | Whose |
| Whose monitor is that? | Who does the monitor over there belong to? | Whose |
| It is hers. | It belongs to her. | hers |
| They are mine. | They belong to me. | mine |

**fix**
- ~~Whose is this keyboard?~~ → Whose keyboard is this?
- ~~Who's keyboard is this?~~ → Whose keyboard is this?
- ~~Whose gloves is this?~~ → Whose gloves are these?

**tip**
Who's and whose sound the same, but who's means who is. When you ask who something belongs to, always write whose.

## 10 · Short answers to «whose?»

**body**
People usually answer Whose…? briefly: It is hers. They are mine. It is my brother's. You don't repeat the thing — it's already in the question.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Whose badge is this? — It is mine. | It belongs to me. | mine |
| Whose pendant is this? — It is hers. | It belongs to her. | hers |
| Whose binoculars are these? — They are mine. | They belong to me. | mine |
| Is this pendant yours? — No, it isn't mine. | It doesn't belong to me. | isn't |

**fix**
- ~~It is my.~~ → It is mine.
- ~~It is my brother.~~ *(meaning it belongs to him)* → It is my brother's.

---

## 11 · Two kinds of 's

**body**
my sister's bag — here 's means the bag belongs to her. But My cousin's here — here 's is short for is. Easy check: if a thing comes after 's, it means «belongs to». If a place or a description comes after it, it's is.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| My friend's flute is here. | The flute that belongs to my friend is here. | 's |
| My cousin's here. | My cousin is here. | 's |
| The rabbit's bowl is empty. | The rabbit's bowl has nothing in it. | 's |

## 12 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~She have a brother.~~ → She has a brother.
- ~~This is mine mug.~~ → This is my mug.
- ~~The helmet is my.~~ → The helmet is mine.
- ~~It's collar is red.~~ → Its collar is red.
- ~~The mug of my husband.~~ → My husband's mug.
- ~~Whose is this keyboard?~~ → Whose keyboard is this?
- ~~Who's keyboard is this?~~ → Whose keyboard is this?

**tip**
Before the thing: my, your, his, her, our, their. Without the thing, at the end: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. For a person: 's — my sister's.

**drill · binary**
- question: You point at a helmet and say it's yours. Which is correct?
- A: `The helmet is my.`
- B: `The helmet is mine.`
- correct: B
- explanation: At the end, without the thing — mine.
