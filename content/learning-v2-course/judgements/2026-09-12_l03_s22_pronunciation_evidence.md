## Targets and primary sources

- [bottle](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/bottle): count container for liquid, usually glass/plastic, narrow neck; бутылка / пляшка. UK /ˈbɒt.əl/, US /ˈbɑː.t̬əl/. Not necessarily glass, not liquid itself. Weak final syllable; do not invent two pronounced t sounds from spelling.
- [bowl](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/bowl): rounded open-topped container deep enough for food or small things; миска / миска. UK /bəʊl/, US /boʊl/. One syllable, not bow-el. Physical vessel sense.
- [plate](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/plate): flat, usually round dish with a slightly raised edge for food; тарелка / тарілка. UK/US /pleɪt/. Dish sense.
- [Plural rule](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/nouns-form): bottles, bowls, plates. Derived /z/ after voiced l in bottles/bowls, /s/ after t in plates; no extra /ɪz/ syllable. Derived IPA, not heard clips.
