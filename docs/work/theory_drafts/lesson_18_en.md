## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open from the start)*

**body**
This lesson is about comparing: cheaper than ours, the shortest delivery time, as strong as yours, too big, such a good relationship. Almost all of it is a small change to the describing word.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This hotel room is cheaper than ours. | it costs less than ours | cheaper than |
| This shop has the shortest delivery time. | it delivers faster than all others | the shortest |
| This instant coffee is as strong as yours. | the same strength | as strong as |
| This cabin bag is too big. | it is a problem, it will not fit | too big |
| We have such a good relationship. | a very good one | such a |

## 02 · -er than — comparing two *(open from the start)*

**body**
To say one thing has more of a quality, add -er to the describing word and use than: cheaper than ours. Do not put more before an -er word: cheaper already means more cheap.

**formula**
`what` · `is` · `word + er` · `than` · `compared with what`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This hotel room is cheaper than ours. | it costs less | cheaper than |
| The shared bathroom is cleaner than before. | less dirty now | cleaner than |
| The train journey is shorter than ours. | it takes less time | shorter than |
| The airport bus is slower than a taxi. | the taxi is faster | slower than |
| These leather shoes are softer than that pair. | nicer to touch | softer than |

**fix**
- ~~This hotel room is more cheaper than ours.~~ → This hotel room is cheaper than ours.
- ~~This hotel room is cheap than ours.~~ → This hotel room is cheaper than ours.
- ~~The shared bathroom is cleaner that before.~~ → The shared bathroom is cleaner than before.

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `The top floor is ___ than this floor.`
- options: `quieter` · `more quieter` · `quiet`
- answer: `quieter`
- why: Comparing two: word + -er, no more.

## 03 · the -est — one out of all

**body**
When you choose one out of all of them, add -est and always put the before it: the shortest delivery time, the nearest shop. -er compares two things; the -est compares one with all the others.

**formula**
`the` · `word + est` · `(thing)`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This shop has the shortest delivery time. | faster than every other shop | the shortest |
| This clinic has the longest waiting time. | you wait longer here than anywhere | the longest |
| This local shop is the nearest. | no shop is closer | the nearest |
| The plain yoghurt is the cheapest option. | every other choice costs more | the cheapest |
| We booked the earliest home visit. | no visit is earlier | the earliest |

**fix**
- ~~This shop has shortest delivery time.~~ → This shop has the shortest delivery time.
- ~~This shop has the shorter delivery time.~~ *(out of all)* → This shop has the shortest delivery time.

**drill · binary**
- question: You are looking at three shops. This one is bigger than both others. What do you say?
- A: `This chain store is the larger.`
- B: `This chain store is the largest.`
- correct: B
- explain: One out of all: the + -est.

## 04 · as … as — the same

**body**
When there is no difference, put the describing word between two as: as strong as yours. The word stays plain, with no -er, and the second word is as, not than.

**formula**
`what` · `is` · `as` · `word` · `as` · `compared with what`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This instant coffee is as strong as yours. | the same strength | as strong as |
| This green tea is as cheap as that one. | the same price | as cheap as |
| These reading glasses are as light as mine. | the same weight | as light as |
| This electric kettle is as fast as ours. | it boils in the same time | as fast as |

**fix**
- ~~This instant coffee is as stronger as yours.~~ → This instant coffee is as strong as yours.
- ~~This instant coffee is as strong than yours.~~ → This instant coffee is as strong as yours.
- ~~This ground coffee is fresh as yours.~~ → This ground coffee is as fresh as yours.

---

## 05 · too and enough

**body**
too means more than is OK — a problem: This cabin bag is too big. enough means it is OK: This checked bag is light enough. too comes before the word; enough comes after it.

**formula**
`problem: too + word` · `OK: word + enough`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This cabin bag is too big. | it will not fit | too big |
| This checked bag is light enough. | it is under the limit | light enough |
| The monthly rent is too high. | we cannot pay it | too high |
| The rental deposit is low enough. | we can pay it | low enough |
| This driveway is too narrow. | the car will not fit | too narrow |

**fix**
- ~~This checked bag is enough light.~~ → This checked bag is light enough.
- ~~This cabin bag is big too.~~ → This cabin bag is too big.

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `This window lock is strong ___.` *(the lock is fine, no need to change it)*
- options: `enough` · `too` · `so`
- answer: `enough`
- why: It is OK: enough, after the word.

---

## 06 · so and such — very, with feeling

**body**
With no thing after the describing word, use so: My close friend is so kind. When a thing follows, use such: such a helpful person, such hard work. The order is such a, not a such.

**formula**
`no thing: so + word` · `with a thing: such a + word + thing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| My close friend is so kind. | very kind | so |
| Our mutual friend is such a helpful person. | a very helpful person | such a |
| This is such hard work. | very hard work | such |
| The live music is so loud. | very loud | so |
| You have such a good memory. | a very good memory | such a |

**fix**
- ~~Our mutual friend is so a helpful person.~~ → Our mutual friend is such a helpful person.
- ~~My close friend is such kind.~~ → My close friend is so kind.
- ~~Our mutual friend is a such helpful person.~~ → Our mutual friend is such a helpful person.

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Say that your relationship is very good.
- answer: `We` · `have` · `such` · `a` · `good` · `relationship`
- slot labels: none (long phrase)
- extra tiles: `so` · `much`

## 07 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~This hotel room is more cheaper than ours.~~ → This hotel room is cheaper than ours.
- ~~This shop has shortest delivery time.~~ → This shop has the shortest delivery time.
- ~~This instant coffee is as strong than yours.~~ → This instant coffee is as strong as yours.
- ~~This checked bag is enough light.~~ → This checked bag is light enough.
- ~~Our mutual friend is so a helpful person.~~ → Our mutual friend is such a helpful person.

**tip**
Two things — -er than. One out of all — the -est. The same — as … as. A problem — too before; OK — enough after. No thing — so; with a thing — such a.

## 08 · What to take into practice

**body**
-er than to compare two. the -est to choose one out of all. as … as for the same. too / enough for a problem or OK. so / such: so with no thing after, such with a thing.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| The sale price is lower than before. | it costs less now | lower than |
| This dining table is the widest. | no table is wider | the widest |
| This is such a big improvement. | a very big change for the better | such a |
