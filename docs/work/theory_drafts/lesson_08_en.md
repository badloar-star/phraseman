# Theory · Lesson 8 of the new course — «I can, can't, Can you…?, Plug it in, Don't rush» (EN)

> Session «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Same sections (01–11), English phrases and drills as
> `lesson_08_ru.md`. Plain English for learners whose own language is not in the app. Common traps:
> I can to sew, He can dances, I am can, I not can / I don't can, You can swim?, Do you can…?,
> Not rush / No panic. The second column explains the meaning instead of translating.
> Needs a native speaker's read-through.

**Screen title:** Theory · Can, can you, do it

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
In this lesson you learn to say what you can and can't do: «I can sew», «I can't skate». You also learn to ask for things — «can you lend me a pen?» —, to give short instructions — «plug it in» — and to say «don't»: «don't rush».

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I can sew. | I know how to make clothes with a needle. | can |
| I can't skate. | I don't know how to skate. | can't |
| Can you lend me a pen? | Please give me your pen for a while. | Can you |
| Plug it in. | Connect it to the power. | Plug |
| Don't rush. | Take your time. | Don't |

---

## 02 · can *(open at start)*

**body**
can goes between who and the action: I can sew. After can, the action is like in the dictionary: no to and no s. You don't need am, is or are here either.

**formula**
`who` · `can` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I can sew. | I know how to sew. | can |
| He can dance. | He knows how to dance. | can |
| I can speak English. | English is a language I speak. | can |
| He can edit photos. | He knows how to change photos on a computer. | can |
| She can polish shoes. | She knows how to make shoes shiny. | can |

**fix**
- ~~I can to sew.~~ → I can sew.
- ~~I can sews.~~ → I can sew.
- ~~I am can translate.~~ → I can translate.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `I can ___ English.`
- options: `speak` · `to speak` · `speaks`
- answer: `speak`
- why: After can, the action has no to and no s.

---

## 03 · can never changes

**body**
In lesson 6, after he and she the action got s. Not with can: can stays the same, and so does the action after it. He can dance — not He cans dance and not He can dances.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| He can dance. | He knows how to dance. | can |
| She can polish shoes. | She knows how to make shoes shiny. | can |
| She can check the rating. | She is able to look at the score. | can |
| She can give feedback. | She is able to say what she thinks. | can |

**fix**
- ~~He cans dance.~~ → He can dance.
- ~~He can dances.~~ → He can dance.
- ~~She can to climb.~~ → She can climb.

**drill · spot_slip**
- tiles: `He` · `can` · `dances`
- wrong: `dances` (index 2)
- hint: One word has an extra letter. Tap it.
- explanation: After can, the action has no s: He can dance.

## 04 · can't

**body**
To say you don't know how or aren't able, use can't instead of can. Everything else stays the same: no to and no s. I can't skate. Not I not can, not I don't can.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I can't skate. | I don't know how to skate. | can't |
| She can't ski. | She doesn't know how to ski. | can't |
| He can't dive. | He doesn't know how to dive. | can't |
| I can't make the bed. | I'm not able to make the bed. | can't |
| She can't do the ironing. | She's not able to iron the clothes. | can't |

**fix**
- ~~I not can skate.~~ → I can't skate.
- ~~I don't can skate.~~ → I can't skate.
- ~~She can't skis.~~ → She can't ski.

**drill · binary**
- question: You don't know how to skate. Which is correct?
- A: `I don't can skate.`
- B: `I can't skate.`
- correct: B
- explanation: With can, «not» is can't.

---

## 05 · Can you…?

**body**
To ask if someone knows how to do something, put can first: Can you sew? You don't need Do — can makes the question by itself. Short answer: Yes, I can.

**formula**
`Can` · `who` · `action` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Can you sew? | Do you know how to sew? | Can |
| Can you hike? | Are you able to walk long distances? | Can |
| Yes, I can. | Yes, I know how. | can |

**fix**
- ~~You can swim?~~ → Can you swim?
- ~~Do you can swim?~~ → Can you swim?
- ~~Yes, I can swim it.~~ → Yes, I can.

---

## 06 · Asking for help: Can you…?

**body**
The same question — Can you…? — is also a polite way to ask for something: Can you lend me a pen? People say this all the time.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Can you lend me a pen? | Please give me your pen for a while. | Can you |
| Can you unfasten the strap? | Please open the strap. | Can you |
| Can you check the buckle? | Please look at the buckle. | Can you |
| Can you explain it? | Please tell me how it works. | Can you |
| Can you explain the diagram? | Please tell me what the drawing means. | Can you |

**fix**
- ~~Can you to explain it?~~ → Can you explain it?
- ~~You can lend me a pen?~~ → Can you lend me a pen?

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Ask someone to explain the diagram.
- answer: `Can` · `you` · `explain` · `the` · `diagram`
- slot labels: none (the sentence is long)
- extra tiles: `to` · `Do`

## 07 · Short instructions: the action first

**body**
To tell someone to do something, start straight with the action, without who: Plug it in. Buy semi-skimmed milk. This is a normal everyday request, not rude.

**formula**
`action` · `what`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Plug it in. | Connect it to the power. | Plug |
| Plug in the humidifier. | Connect the humidifier to the power. | Plug |
| Turn on the shredder. | Start the paper-cutting machine. | Turn on |
| Buy low-fat yoghurt. | Get yoghurt with little fat. | Buy |
| Buy semi-skimmed milk. | Get milk with some fat taken out. | Buy |

**fix**
- ~~You plug it in.~~ *(as a request)* → Plug it in.
- ~~To plug it in.~~ → Plug it in.

---

## 08 · «Don't»

**body**
To tell someone not to do something, put Don't before the action: Don't rush. Not rush and No panic are wrong. To sound softer, put Please first: Please don't interrupt.

**formula**
`(Please)` · `Don't` · `action`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Don't rush. | Take your time. | Don't |
| Don't lean on it. | Don't put your weight on it. | Don't |
| Please don't interrupt. | Please let me finish. | Please don't |
| Don't spill it. | Be careful, keep it in the cup. | Don't |
| Don't panic. | Stay calm. | Don't |

**fix**
- ~~Not rush.~~ → Don't rush.
- ~~No panic.~~ → Don't panic.
- ~~Don't please interrupt.~~ → Please don't interrupt.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `___ panic.`
- options: `No` · `Not` · `Don't`
- answer: `Don't`
- why: To say «don't do it», put Don't before the action.

## 09 · can for offering help

**body**
can isn't only about skills. You also use it to offer help and talk about what is possible: I can manage the list. We can share the workload.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I can evaluate it. | I'm able to judge how good it is. | can |
| We can choose a criterion. | We are able to pick one rule to judge by. | can |
| I can manage the list. | I'm able to take care of the list. | can |
| We can share the workload. | We are able to split the work. | can |
| I can improve it. | I'm able to make it better. | can |

## 10 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I can to sew.~~ → I can sew.
- ~~He can dances.~~ → He can dance.
- ~~He cans dance.~~ → He can dance.
- ~~I am can translate.~~ → I can translate.
- ~~I don't can skate.~~ → I can't skate.
- ~~Do you can swim?~~ → Can you swim?
- ~~Not rush.~~ → Don't rush.

**tip**
can is like a lock on the action: after it, the action is always as in the dictionary. No to, no s, no do.

## 11 · What to take into practice

**body**
Able to: I can + action as in the dictionary. Not able: can't. Asking: Can you…? Telling someone to do it: the action first. Telling someone not to: Don't…, politely: Please don't…

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I can speak English. | English is a language I speak. | can |
| She can't ski. | She doesn't know how to ski. | can't |
| Can you check the buckle? | Please look at the buckle. | Can you |
| Turn on the shredder. | Start the paper-cutting machine. | Turn on |
| Please don't interrupt. | Please let me finish. | Please don't |
