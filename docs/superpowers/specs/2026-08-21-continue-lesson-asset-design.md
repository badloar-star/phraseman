# Continue Lesson themed asset design

## Decision

The approved master is variant 3: a compact top-spiral workbook/study pad with the current unfinished page, a horizontal pen holder, and visible progress marks.

## Meaning

The asset represents the most recent unfinished lesson on Home. It must read as “resume this specific task,” not as the Lessons catalogue, Practice, a diagnostic test, or a cultural destination.

## Fixed geometry across themes

- One compact softly rounded top-spiral workbook in a slight three-quarter view.
- A colored cover folded behind one warm-ivory current page, with a subtly lifted page corner.
- Six or seven refined top rings.
- Three short embossed exercise lines; the final line is visibly half-finished and ends in an open circle.
- Two filled progress dots followed by one hollow dot; no percentage or readable UI text.
- One pen seated horizontally in a lower holder without obscuring the page.
- One small bookmark ribbon.
- Compact, centered silhouette with generous transparent padding and a contained contact shadow.

The workbook outline, cover and page placement, ring count, exercise layout, progress dots, pen holder, bookmark, camera angle, padding, and overall visual mass remain consistent across every live interface theme.

## Allowed theme variation

- Palette and lighting.
- Cover, rings, paper-edge, pen, holder, and bookmark materials.
- A very small botanical or ornamental accent associated with the interface theme.
- Surface embossing that does not change the asset's meaning.

## Prohibited content

- No standalone architecture or landmark scene.
- No stack of books; books belong to the Lessons tile.
- No flashcards or large language words; they belong to the Flashcards tile.
- No microphone, speaker, audio token, or Practice symbolism.
- No diagnostic-test symbolism.
- No MAX artwork changes.
- No trophy, shield, gift, daily challenge, character, logo, or UI container.

## Production requirements

- Approved soft tactile 3D family.
- Real alpha transparency, verified from image metadata.
- Readable at Home-card thumbnail size.
- Preview outside `assets/images/**` until approved.
- Final asset compressed to WebP and wired 1:1 to an existing static slot before bundling.

## Initial variants

- Ember: terracotta cover, warm-ivory page, antique-brass/dark-cocoa rings, dark-cocoa pen, muted-olive detail, warm ribbon.
- Indigo: deep-indigo cover, warm-ivory page, antique-silver/dark-cocoa rings, dark-indigo pen with silver hardware, muted-lavender ribbon and progress dots.
