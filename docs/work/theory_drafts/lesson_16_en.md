## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open from the start)*

**body**
In this lesson one small word, will, does a lot of work: a guess about tomorrow, a decision you make on the spot, a promise, an offer of help. At the end you will choose between will, going to and the -ing form from lesson 15.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I think the heatwave will end tomorrow. | my guess about tomorrow | will end |
| I'll get the plunger. | I decide right now | I'll |
| I'll keep in touch. | a promise | I'll |
| Shall I cool down the soup? | an offer of help | Shall I |

---

## 02 · will — «I think it will happen» *(open from the start)*

**body**
When you guess how things will be, say will + the action as in the dictionary. Will is the same for everyone: I will, he will, they will. It often comes after I think: this is your opinion, not a plan.

**formula**
`I think` · `who` · `will` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I think the humidity will rise. | the air will get more damp | will rise |
| I think the mist will clear soon. | we will see clearly soon | will clear |
| I think I will oversleep tomorrow. | I will wake up too late | will oversleep |
| I think they will overcharge us. | we will pay too much | will overcharge |

**fix**
- ~~I think the humidity will to rise.~~ → I think the humidity will rise.
- ~~I think the humidity will rises.~~ → I think the humidity will rise.

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `I think visibility will ___.`
- options: `improve` · `improves` · `to improve`
- answer: `improve`
- why: After will comes the action as in the dictionary: no to, no -s.

## 03 · will be — how something will be

**body**
When it is not an action but a state — the beach full of people, the tea not hot enough — put be after will: will be overcrowded, will be lukewarm. Do not leave be out and do not use is.

**formula**
`who / what` · `will be` · `what it is like`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I think it will be overcast tomorrow. | grey sky, no sun | will be |
| I think the beach will be overcrowded. | far too many people | will be |
| I think the tea will be lukewarm. | only a little warm | will be |
| I think the soup will be scalding. | hot enough to burn | will be |

**fix**
- ~~I think the beach will overcrowded.~~ → I think the beach will be overcrowded.
- ~~I think the beach will is overcrowded.~~ → I think the beach will be overcrowded.

---

## 04 · I'll — I decide right now

**body**
I'll is the short form of I will. The water is not going down the sink, you have just noticed, and you decide on the spot: I'll get the plunger. You did not plan it before; the decision is made this second.

**formula**
`I'll` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I'll get the plunger. | the sink is blocked | I'll |
| I'll get a tablespoon. | we need a big spoon | I'll |
| I'll roll up my sleeves. | they are in the way | I'll |
| I'll mop up the water. | there is water on the floor | I'll |
| I'll fill up the water bottle. | it is empty | I'll |

**fix**
- ~~I get the plunger.~~ *(decided now)* → I'll get the plunger.
- ~~I'll to get the plunger.~~ → I'll get the plunger.

**drill · binary**
- question: The water is not going down the sink. You have just noticed. What do you say?
- A: `I get the plunger.`
- B: `I'll get the plunger.`
- correct: B
- explain: A decision about the future, made right now: I'll.

## 05 · I'll — I promise

**body**
The same I'll makes a promise: your friend is going away and is worried about their mother, so you reassure them — I'll check in on your mother. The form is the same; the situation gives the meaning.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I'll check in on your mother. | I will see that she is OK | I'll |
| I'll keep in touch. | I will call or write | I'll |
| I'll drop by tomorrow. | I will visit for a short time | I'll |
| I'll hand in the form tomorrow. | I will give it in | I'll |
| I'll keep an eye on your bag. | I will watch it for you | I'll |

**fix**
- ~~I keep in touch.~~ *(a promise)* → I'll keep in touch.
- ~~I'll to keep in touch.~~ → I'll keep in touch.

## 06 · I'll and Shall I…? — offering help

**body**
You can offer help in two ways. Just do it: I'll top up your glass. Or ask if it is wanted: Shall I cool down the soup? After Shall I comes the action as in the dictionary, with no to and no second will.

**formula**
`Shall I` · `action as in the dictionary` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I'll top up your glass. | I will add more to it | I'll |
| Shall I cool down the soup? | do you want me to? | Shall I |
| Shall I clear away the plates? | do you want me to? | Shall I |
| Shall I put up the shelf? | do you want me to? | Shall I |
| Shall I come round this evening? | do you want me to visit? | Shall I |

**fix**
- ~~Shall I to cool down the soup?~~ → Shall I cool down the soup?
- ~~Shall I will cool down the soup?~~ → Shall I cool down the soup?

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Offer to cut the bread into pieces.
- answer: `Shall` · `I` · `slice` · `up` · `the` · `bread`
- slot labels: none (long phrase)
- extra tiles: `to` · `will`

## 07 · will, going to or -ing?

**body**
Three ways to talk about the future, each with its own shade. You think it will happen: will. You decided earlier: going to. You agreed with someone and there is a time: -ing. Sometimes they overlap; choose the one that says what you mean.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I think the task will be manageable. | my opinion | will |
| I am going to cancel an unnecessary trip. | I decided earlier | going to |
| We are joining a worthwhile activity tomorrow. | it is agreed and booked | are joining |
| I think Tom will show up on time. | my opinion | will |
| We are heading out at eight tomorrow. | the time is fixed | are heading |

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `We ___ a compact tent tomorrow.` *(we agreed with the seller, the time is set)*
- options: `are collecting` · `collect` · `are collect`
- answer: `are collecting`
- why: There is an agreement and a time: am / is / are + -ing.

## 08 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I think the humidity will to rise.~~ → I think the humidity will rise.
- ~~I think the humidity will rises.~~ → I think the humidity will rise.
- ~~I think the beach will overcrowded.~~ → I think the beach will be overcrowded.
- ~~I get the plunger.~~ *(decided now)* → I'll get the plunger.
- ~~Shall I to cool down the soup?~~ → Shall I cool down the soup?

**tip**
After will, I'll and Shall I always comes the action as in the dictionary: no to, no -s, no -ing. If a describing word follows, put be first.

## 09 · What to take into practice

**body**
You think it will happen — I think … will. You decide now or promise — I'll. You offer help — I'll or Shall I…? You decided earlier — going to; agreed a time — -ing.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I think the holiday will be memorable. | we will remember it | will be |
| I'll wipe down the table. | I decide now | I'll |
| Shall I go over the list with you? | an offer to check it together | Shall I |
