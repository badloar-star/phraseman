# Teori · Pelajaran 10 kursus baru — «I usually buy… / I am buying… today» (ID)

> Sesi «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Bagian 01–13, kalimat bahasa Inggris, dan latihan sama dengan
> `lesson_10_ru.md`. Jebakan penutur bahasa Indonesia: kata kerja tidak berubah sehingga -ing
> terlupa saat bilang «hari ini» (→ I buy today), «aku setuju denganmu» (→ I agree you),
> «tenggorokanku sakit» (→ I am sick throat), «seminggu sekali» (→ one time a week), «Selamat malam»
> untuk menyapa dan berpamitan (→ Good night sebagai sapaan). Sapaan: kamu.
> Perlu dibaca ulang oleh penutur asli.

**Judul layar:** Teori · Biasanya atau sedang

## 01 · Apa yang akan kamu pelajari *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Di pelajaran ini kamu menggabungkan yang sudah kamu tahu dan belajar memilih: «aku biasanya beli tomat kaleng» atau «hari ini aku beli makanan jadi». Juga cara bilang apa yang sakit, cuacanya bagaimana, dan cara meminta dengan sopan.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I usually buy tinned tomatoes. | Aku biasanya beli tomat kaleng. | usually buy |
| I am buying a ready-made meal today. | Hari ini aku beli makanan jadi. | am buying |
| I have a sore throat. | Tenggorokanku sakit. | have |
| It is quite cold. | Cukup dingin. | quite |

---

## 02 · Biasanya — atau sedang, untuk sementara *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Kalau kebiasaan — bentuk dari pelajaran 6: I often travel abroad. Kalau sedang terjadi atau hanya untuk sementara — di perjalanan ini, hari ini — bentuk dari pelajaran 9: We are staying at the seaside — «kami sedang menginap di pantai».

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I often travel abroad. | Aku sering ke luar negeri. | travel |
| We are staying at the seaside. | Kami sedang menginap di pantai. | are staying |
| I usually buy tinned tomatoes. | Aku biasanya beli tomat kaleng. | buy |
| I am buying a ready-made meal today. | Hari ini aku beli makanan jadi. | am buying |

**fix**
- ~~I buy a ready-made meal today.~~ → I am buying a ready-made meal today.
- ~~I am often travelling abroad.~~ *(kebiasaan)* → I often travel abroad.

**drill · binary**
- pertanyaan: Biasanya kamu masak sendiri, tapi hari ini beli makanan jadi. Kamu bilang apa?
- A: `I buy a ready-made meal today.`
- B: `I am buying a ready-made meal today.`
- benar: B
- penjelasan: today tentang hari ini, bukan kebiasaan: am buying.

## 03 · Petunjuk di dalam kalimat

**body**
Sering kalimatnya sendiri memberi petunjuk. often, usually, once a week, twice a week adalah kebiasaan — tanpa -ing. today, now adalah sekarang — am / is / are + -ing. «Seminggu sekali» adalah once a week.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I swim once a week. | Aku berenang seminggu sekali. | once a week |
| I swim twice a week. | Aku berenang dua kali seminggu. | twice a week |
| I often get lost. | Aku sering tersesat. | often |
| I am checking the forecast. | Aku sedang cek ramalan cuaca. | am checking |

**fix**
- ~~I swim one time a week.~~ → I swim once a week.
- ~~I am swimming once a week.~~ → I swim once a week.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `I ___ twice a week.`
- pilihan: `swim` · `am swimming` · `swimming`
- jawaban: `swim`
- kenapa: twice a week adalah kebiasaan, tanpa -ing.

## 04 · need, know, agree — tanpa am dan tanpa -ing

**body**
Beberapa kata hampir tidak pernah memakai -ing dan am: need — «perlu», know — «tahu», agree — «setuju». Walaupun saat ini, cukup I need, I know, I agree. «Setuju dengan» pakai with: I agree with you.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I need a return ticket. | Aku perlu tiket pulang-pergi. | need |
| I only need milk. | Aku cuma perlu susu. | need |
| I know the address. | Aku tahu alamatnya. | know |
| I agree with you. | Aku setuju denganmu. | agree |

**fix**
- ~~I agree you.~~ → I agree with you.
- ~~I am need a bin bag.~~ → I need a bin bag.
- ~~I am know the address.~~ → I know the address.

**drill · spot_slip**
- kartu: `I` · `am` · `agree` · `with` · `you`
- berlebih: `am` (indeks 1)
- petunjuk: Ada satu kata yang berlebih. Ketuk kata itu.
- penjelasan: agree adalah kegiatan, tanpa am: I agree with you.

## 05 · «Sakit» — have atau has

**body**
Kita bilang «tenggorokanku sakit». Bahasa Inggris bilang «aku punya tenggorokan yang sakit»: I have a sore throat. Tanpa am. Dengan he dan she — has: She has earache, He has backache.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I have a sore throat. | Tenggorokanku sakit. | have |
| She has earache. | Telinganya sakit. | has |
| He has backache. | Punggungnya sakit. | has |
| I have toothache. | Gigiku sakit. | have |

**fix**
- ~~I am sick throat.~~ → I have a sore throat.
- ~~He have backache.~~ → He has backache.
- ~~He is has backache.~~ → He has backache.

## 06 · Kata kecil: also, only, just, almost

**body**
also — «juga», only — «cuma», just — «hanya / cuma», almost — «hampir». Dengan kegiatan — di depan kegiatan: I also need bread. Dengan am / is / are — sesudahnya: I am just looking, It is almost ready.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I also need bread. | Aku juga perlu roti. | also |
| I only need milk. | Aku cuma perlu susu. | only |
| I am just looking. | Aku cuma lihat-lihat. | just |
| It is almost ready. | Hampir siap. | almost |

**fix**
- ~~I need bread also.~~ → I also need bread.
- ~~I just am looking.~~ → I am just looking.

---

## 07 · probably dan perhaps — «mungkin», «barangkali»

**body**
probably — «mungkin / kemungkinan besar», sesudah is: He is probably at home. perhaps — «barangkali», di awal: Perhaps he is on the bus. quite — «cukup»: It is quite cold.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| He is probably at home. | Dia mungkin di rumah. | probably |
| Perhaps he is on the bus. | Barangkali dia di bus. | Perhaps |
| It is quite cold. | Cukup dingin. | quite |

**fix**
- ~~He probably is at home.~~ → He is probably at home.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `He is ___ at home.`
- pilihan: `probably` · `perhaps` · `quite`
- jawaban: `probably`
- kenapa: «Mungkin» sesudah is adalah probably; perhaps di awal kalimat.

## 08 · Kapan: at lunchtime, before bedtime

**body**
Untuk waktu tertentu dalam sehari pakai at: at nine, at lunchtime, at midnight. «Sebelum» adalah before: before bedtime. «Siang hari» adalah in the daytime. Waktu di akhir kalimat.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I walk at lunchtime. | Aku jalan-jalan saat jam makan siang. | at lunchtime |
| I read before bedtime. | Aku membaca sebelum tidur. | before bedtime |
| I work in the daytime. | Aku bekerja siang hari. | in the daytime |
| We reach the station at nine. | Kami sampai di stasiun jam sembilan. | at nine |

**fix**
- ~~I walk in lunchtime.~~ → I walk at lunchtime.
- ~~I work at the daytime.~~ → I work in the daytime.

## 09 · Cuaca dan suhu: It is…

**body**
Kita cukup bilang «minus dua» atau «dingin». Bahasa Inggris selalu mulai dengan It is: It is quite cold, It is one degree, It is minus two.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It is one degree. | Satu derajat. | It is |
| It is minus two. | Minus dua derajat. | It is |
| It is quite cold. | Cukup dingin. | It is |

**fix**
- ~~Is minus two.~~ → It is minus two.
- ~~It is one degrees.~~ → It is one degree.

## 10 · Sopan: Can I…? Can you…?

**body**
Meminta untuk diri sendiri — Can I…?: Can I have your phone number? Meminta orang lain — Can you…?: Can you repeat that? Menanyakan harga — How much does it cost? Sesudah can dan does, kegiatan tanpa to dan s.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Can I have your phone number? | Boleh minta nomor teleponmu? | Can I |
| Can you repeat that? | Bisa ulangi? | Can you |
| How much does it cost? | Ini harganya berapa? | does |

**fix**
- ~~Can I to have your phone number?~~ → Can I have your phone number?
- ~~Can you repeats that?~~ → Can you repeat that?
- ~~How much does it costs?~~ → How much does it cost?

## 11 · Salam menurut waktu

**body**
Good morning — pagi, Good afternoon — siang dan sore, Good evening — malam, saat bertemu. «Selamat malam» bisa untuk menyapa dan berpamitan, tapi Good night hanya untuk berpamitan sebelum tidur.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Good morning. | Selamat pagi. | morning |
| Good afternoon. | Selamat siang. | afternoon |
| Good evening. | Selamat malam. *(saat bertemu)* | evening |
| Good night. | Selamat tidur. | night |

**fix**
- ~~Good night.~~ *(saat bertemu malam hari)* → Good evening.
