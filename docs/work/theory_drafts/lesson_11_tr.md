## 01 · Neler söylemeyi öğreneceksin *(baştan açık)*

**body**
Şimdiye kadar şu an olanı söyledin: I am at home. Şimdi ne olduğunu anlatmayı öğreneceksin: «dün evdeydim», «Noel'de birlikteydik», «çantada iki mandal vardı». Bunun için bağlayıcının geçmiş biçimi var.

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Dün evdeydim. | was |
| We were together at Christmas. | Noel'de birlikteydik. | were |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | Çantada iki mandal vardı. | There were |
| Was it warm in spring? | İlkbaharda hava sıcak mıydı? | Was |

## 02 · was ve were *(baştan açık)*

**body**
Türkçede geçmiş «-di» ekiyle yapılır: «evdeydim». İngilizcede bağlayıcının iki geçmiş biçimi var. am ve is yerine was: I was, she was. are yerine were: you were, we were, they were. Sıra aynı kalır: kim + was + yer.

**formula**
`I / he / she / it` · `was` — `you / we / they` · `were`

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Dün evdeydim. | was |
| She was in London last year. | Geçen yıl Londra'daydı. | was |
| We were at home last night. | Dün akşam evdeydik. | were |
| They were in London at New Year. | Yılbaşında Londra'daydılar. | were |
| The hairdresser was busy yesterday. | Dün kuaför meşguldü. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday I home.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~I were at home yesterday.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.

**drill · choice**
- cümle: `We ___ at the train station yesterday.`
- seçenekler: `was` · `were` · `are`
- cevap: `were`
- neden: we ile geçmişte were.

## 03 · Geçmiş işaretleri: yesterday, last night, on Sunday

**body**
Cümlede genelde geçmişi gösteren bir kelime olur: yesterday — «dün», last night — «dün akşam», last year — «geçen yıl». Bayramlar at ile: at Christmas, at New Year. Günler on ile: on Sunday.

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home on Sunday. | Pazar günü evdeydim. | on Sunday |
| We were together at Christmas. | Noel'de birlikteydik. | at Christmas |
| They were in London at New Year. | Yılbaşında Londra'daydılar. | at New Year |
| The sports centre was closed yesterday. | Dün spor merkezi kapalıydı. | yesterday |

**fix**
- ~~I was at home in Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.
- ~~We were together in Christmas.~~ → We were together at Christmas.

## 04 · Dün — was, şimdi — is

**body**
Dünden bahsediyorsan was ya da were. Şu andan bahsediyorsan is ya da are, eskisi gibi. yesterday ya da now kelimesi hemen söyler: Yesterday my towel was wet. The shower curtain is dry now.

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday my beach towel was wet. | Dün plaj havlum ıslaktı. | was |
| Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty. | Dün terliklerim kirliydi. | were |
| The shower curtain is dry now. | Duş perdesi şimdi kuru. | is |
| The bathroom mirror was dirty yesterday. | Dün banyo aynası kirliydi. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~Yesterday my flip-flops was dirty.~~ → Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty.

**drill · binary**
- soru: Dün havlu ıslaktı. Nasıl söylersin?
- A: `Yesterday my beach towel is wet.`
- B: `Yesterday my beach towel was wet.`
- doğru: B
- açıklama: Dün geçmiştir, bu yüzden was.

## 05 · Tane tane sayılmayanlar — was

**body**
4. dersteki gibi, tane tane sayılmayan şeyler — krem, yemek, meyve suyu — tekil biçimi alır: was, were değil. Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag. | Dün güneş kremi çantamdaydı. | was |
| The apple juice was cold yesterday. | Dün elma suyu soğuktu. | was |
| The hot chocolate was too sweet yesterday. | Dün sıcak çikolata çok tatlıydı. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday the sun cream were in my bag.~~ → Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.
- ~~The baby food were in the cupboard.~~ → The baby food was in the cupboard.

## 06 · «Değildi»: was not, were not

**body**
Türkçede olumsuzluk ekin içindedir: «değildi». İngilizcede not, was ya da were'den hemen sonra gelir: It was not my day off. The fried egg was not cold.

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It was not my day off. | O gün izinli değildim. | was not |
| The fried egg was not cold. | Sahanda yumurta soğuk değildi. | was not |
| The peanut butter was not on the table. | Fıstık ezmesi masada değildi. | was not |

**fix**
- ~~It not was my day off.~~ → It was not my day off.
- ~~The fried egg didn't cold.~~ → The fried egg was not cold.

## 07 · Soru: Was…? Were…?

**body**
Türkçede soru «mıydı?» ekiyle sona gelir. İngilizcede was ya da were başa geçer: Was it warm in spring? Were you at home in autumn? Kısa cevap da bağlayıcıyla: Yes, it was. do ve did burada yok.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `kim / ne` · `…` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Was it warm in spring? | İlkbaharda hava sıcak mıydı? | Was |
| Were you at home in autumn? | Sonbaharda evde miydin? | Were |
| Was your pancake hot? | Krepin sıcak mıydı? | Was |
| Were the scrambled eggs hot? | Çırpılmış yumurta sıcak mıydı? | Were |
| Yes, it was. | Evet, sıcaktı. | was |

**fix**
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Were your pancake hot?~~ → Was your pancake hot?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.

**drill · word_bank**
- görev: Sor: «İlkbaharda hava sıcak mıydı?»
- cevap: `Was` · `it` · `warm` · `in` · `spring`
- etiketler: yok (cümle uzun)
- fazla kartlar: `Were` · `Did`

## 08 · «Vardı»: there was, there were

**body**
Türkçede «vardı» sona gelir: «çantada iki mandal vardı». İngilizcede başa gelir: 4. dersteki there is'teki is, was olur. Bir şey için There was, birkaç şey için There were.

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| There was a bed sheet on the bed. | Yatakta bir çarşaf vardı. | There was |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | Çantada iki mandal vardı. | There were |
| There was a public toilet near the station. | İstasyonun yakınında umumi tuvalet vardı. | There was |
| There were two taxi drivers near the station. | İstasyonun yakınında iki taksici vardı. | There were |
| There was fruit salad in the bowl. | Kasede meyve salatası vardı. | There was |

**fix**
- ~~Two clothes pegs there were in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~There was two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.

**drill · choice**
- cümle: `There ___ two duvet covers in the cupboard.`
- seçenekler: `was` · `were` · `is`
- cevap: `were`
- neden: İki nevresim var, bu yüzden There were.

**body**
was ile geçmişi değerlendirmek kolaydır: That was a silly idea — «aptalca bir fikirdi», The plan was sensible — «plan mantıklıydı». Birkaç şey için were: The rules were fair.

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| That was a silly idea. | Aptalca bir fikirdi. | was |
| The plan was sensible. | Plan mantıklıydı. | was |

## 10 · Neredeyse herkesin yaptığı hatalar

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday I home.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~Two clothes pegs there were in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.
- ~~I was at home in Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.

**tip**
Dersin bütün yeni dilbilgisi iki biçim: was — I, he, she, it ve tek şey için; were — you, we, they ve birkaç şey için. Gerisi am, is, are ile aynı.
