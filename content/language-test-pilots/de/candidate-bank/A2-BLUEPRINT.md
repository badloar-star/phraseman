## A2 boundary

- Reading tasks contain short connected language and require at most three explicit relations; unstated high-level inference belongs above A2.
- Pragmatic keys combine one or two familiar functions such as acknowledgment plus request, or apology plus repair.
- Grammar is contextualised in complete utterances and tests productive A2 patterns rather than metalanguage.
- Vocabulary remains frequent and broadly standard; specialist terminology and local institutions cannot determine the key.
- Distractors must be plausible A2 errors or situational mismatches, not nonsense inserted merely to make the answer obvious.
- EN/RU service copy and individually authored Russian distractor rationales are mandatory.
- `reviewStatus` remains `self_checked`; qualified external review remains `pending`.
- A2 cannot enter `completedLevels` before item-by-item linguistic review, structural gates, and adaptive-fit review pass.
