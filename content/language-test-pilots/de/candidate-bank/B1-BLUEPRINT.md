## B1 boundary

- Reading keys require connected interpretation or one supported inference, while all decisive evidence remains explicit in a short text.
- Pragmatic keys combine two or three familiar constraints and remain natural in standard professional or everyday German.
- Grammar tasks test reliable clause, case, tense, voice, or mood control in context, not rare exceptions.
- Vocabulary is frequent in work, services, community, media, and public life; specialist knowledge cannot decide the answer.
- Distractors are plausible intermediate errors and each fails one documented relation or communicative component.
- EN/RU service copy and individually authored Russian distractor rationales are mandatory.
- `reviewStatus` remains `self_checked`; qualified external review remains `pending`.
- B1 cannot enter `completedLevels` before item-by-item linguistic review, structural gates, and adaptive-fit review pass.
