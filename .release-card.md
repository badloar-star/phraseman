# Release card

## Type

feature

## Goal

Ship Phraseman 1.6.23 (build 129) with the accumulated work of the current branch: the Learning V2 map fixes (edge fade, full-course dictionary, section no longer dead on a const used before declaration), the reworked legacy-lesson list, rune economy corrections (author is paid for packs, video-watch runes count live, every purchase actually debits), Arena search-exit fixes, the home-screen experience-bar notch with its six audited defects, and the admin QA review panel for published packs. Native store builds for both platforms plus an OTA update published to the matching runtime.

## Allowed changes

- The complete owner-approved workspace snapshot as of 2026-09-21, covering Learning V2 runtime and map, flashcards and transcription, rune economy and author payouts, Arena search, home screen, admin QA panel, guards, and documentation.
- Exact metadata bump in `app.json`: app/runtime 1.6.22 to 1.6.23 and Android/iOS build 128 to 129.
- EAS production build for iOS with automatic App Store Connect submission.
- EAS production build for Android without automatic Google Play submission.
- EAS update published to the production channel on runtime 1.6.23, addressed to the new builds only.

## Forbidden changes

- No Android automatic submission or Google Play rollout; Android upload remains manual.
- No OTA addressed to runtime 1.6.22: installed 1.6.22 builds must not receive a bundle they were not built for.
- No additional Firebase Functions, Firestore rules/indexes, Hosting, App Check, branch, or worktree deployment outside the committed snapshot.
- No deletion or rollback of concurrent owner-approved functionality, in particular the sealed MAX section and the Arena files.

## Risk level

critical — a two-platform native store release carrying a broad shared workspace snapshot plus an OTA update, touching currency (runes, author payouts), purchases, Learning V2 progression, and the home screen.

- Open Learning V2 and confirm the map section opens at all, edges fade without a live blur, and the full-course dictionary opens from the map.
- Confirm the legacy lessons screen shows one continuous list with no A1/A2/B1/B2 chips.
- Watch a partner video and confirm the rune counter grows during playback and the same number is visible on the home screen afterwards.
- Buy a pack for runes and confirm runes are actually debited once, the button cannot be double-tapped, and the pack author receives the payout.
