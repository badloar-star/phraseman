## 01 · Apa yang akan kamu pelajari *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Sampai sekarang kamu bicara tentang saat ini: I am at home. Sekarang kamu belajar bercerita tentang masa lalu: «kemarin aku di rumah», «saat Natal kami bersama», «di tas ada dua jepit jemuran». Bahasa Inggris mengubah penjepitnya ke bentuk lampau.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Kemarin aku di rumah. | was |
| We were together at Christmas. | Saat Natal kami bersama. | were |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | Di tas ada dua jepit jemuran. | There were |
| Was it warm in spring? | Apakah musim semi hangat? | Was |

## 02 · was dan were *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Kita cukup menambah «kemarin», kata lainnya tidak berubah. Bahasa Inggris harus berubah: am dan is jadi was, are jadi were. I was, she was — you were, we were, they were. Jangan bilang Yesterday I am.

**formula**
`I / he / she / it` · `was` — `you / we / they` · `were`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Kemarin aku di rumah. | was |
| She was in London last year. | Tahun lalu dia di London. | was |
| We were at home last night. | Tadi malam kami di rumah. | were |
| They were in London at New Year. | Saat Tahun Baru mereka di London. | were |
| The hairdresser was busy yesterday. | Kemarin penata rambutnya sibuk. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday I am at home.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~I were at home yesterday.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `We ___ at the train station yesterday.`
- pilihan: `was` · `were` · `are`
- jawaban: `were`
- kenapa: Dengan we di masa lalu — were.

## 03 · Tanda masa lalu: yesterday, last night, on Sunday

**body**
Biasanya ada kata yang menunjukkan masa lalu: yesterday — «kemarin», last night — «tadi malam», last year — «tahun lalu». Hari raya pakai at: at Christmas, at New Year. Hari pakai on: on Sunday.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home on Sunday. | Hari Minggu aku di rumah. | on Sunday |
| We were together at Christmas. | Saat Natal kami bersama. | at Christmas |
| They were in London at New Year. | Saat Tahun Baru mereka di London. | at New Year |
| The sports centre was closed yesterday. | Kemarin pusat olahraganya tutup. | yesterday |

**fix**
- ~~I was at home in Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.
- ~~We were together in Christmas.~~ → We were together at Christmas.

---

## 04 · Kemarin — was, sekarang — is

**body**
Kalau tentang kemarin — was atau were. Kalau tentang sekarang — is atau are, seperti sebelumnya. Kata yesterday atau now langsung memberi petunjuk: Yesterday my towel was wet. The shower curtain is dry now.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday my beach towel was wet. | Kemarin handuk pantaiku basah. | was |
| Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty. | Kemarin sandal jepitku kotor. | were |
| The shower curtain is dry now. | Tirai kamar mandinya sekarang kering. | is |
| The bathroom mirror was dirty yesterday. | Kemarin cermin kamar mandi kotor. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~Yesterday my flip-flops was dirty.~~ → Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty.

**drill · binary**
- pertanyaan: Kemarin handuknya basah. Kamu bilang apa?
- A: `Yesterday my beach towel is wet.`
- B: `Yesterday my beach towel was wet.`
- benar: B
- penjelasan: Kemarin adalah masa lalu, jadi was.

## 05 · Yang tidak dihitung per buah — was

**body**
Seperti di pelajaran 4, yang tidak dihitung per buah — krim, makanan, jus — memakai bentuk tunggal: was, bukan were. Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag. | Kemarin tabir surya ada di tasku. | was |
| The apple juice was cold yesterday. | Kemarin jus apelnya dingin. | was |
| The hot chocolate was too sweet yesterday. | Kemarin cokelat panasnya terlalu manis. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday the sun cream were in my bag.~~ → Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.
- ~~The baby food were in the cupboard.~~ → The baby food was in the cupboard.

## 06 · «Tidak»: was not, were not

**body**
«Tidak» ditaruh tepat sesudah was atau were: It was not my day off. The fried egg was not cold. Bukan It not was.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It was not my day off. | Itu bukan hari liburku. | was not |
| The fried egg was not cold. | Telur ceploknya tidak dingin. | was not |
| The peanut butter was not on the table. | Selai kacangnya tidak ada di meja. | was not |

**fix**
- ~~It not was my day off.~~ → It was not my day off.
- ~~The fried egg didn't cold.~~ → The fried egg was not cold.

## 07 · Pertanyaan: Was…? Were…?

**body**
Kita bertanya tanpa mengubah urutan. Bahasa Inggris memindahkan was atau were ke depan: Was it warm in spring? Were you at home in autumn? Jawaban singkat juga dengan penjepit: Yes, it was. Tidak pakai did.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `siapa / apa` · `…` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Was it warm in spring? | Apakah musim semi hangat? | Was |
| Were you at home in autumn? | Saat musim gugur kamu di rumah? | Were |
| Was your pancake hot? | Panekukmu panas? | Was |
| Were the scrambled eggs hot? | Telur orak-ariknya panas? | Were |
| Yes, it was. | Ya. | was |

**fix**
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Were your pancake hot?~~ → Was your pancake hot?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.

**drill · word_bank**
- tugas: Tanyakan: «Apakah musim semi hangat?»
- jawaban: `Was` · `it` · `warm` · `in` · `spring`
- label: tidak ada (kalimatnya panjang)
- kartu lebih: `Were` · `Did`

## 08 · «Ada» di masa lalu: there was, there were

**body**
Kita bilang «ada» untuk sekarang dan masa lalu. Bahasa Inggris berubah: There was untuk satu benda, There were untuk banyak. There was a towel rail in the bathroom. There were two clothes pegs in the bag.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| There was a bed sheet on the bed. | Di tempat tidur ada seprai. | There was |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | Di tas ada dua jepit jemuran. | There were |
| There was a public toilet near the station. | Dekat stasiun ada toilet umum. | There was |
| There were two taxi drivers near the station. | Dekat stasiun ada dua sopir taksi. | There were |
| There was fruit salad in the bowl. | Di mangkuk ada salad buah. | There was |

**fix**
- ~~There are two clothes pegs in the bag yesterday.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~There was two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `There ___ two duvet covers in the cupboard.`
- pilihan: `was` · `were` · `is`
- jawaban: `were`
- kenapa: Sarung selimutnya dua, jadi There were.

## 09 · Menilai yang sudah lewat

**body**
Dengan was mudah menilai sesuatu yang sudah lewat: That was a silly idea — «itu ide konyol», The plan was sensible — «rencananya masuk akal». Untuk banyak benda — were: The rules were fair.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| That was a silly idea. | Itu ide konyol. | was |
| The plan was sensible. | Rencananya masuk akal. | was |
| The rules were fair. | Aturannya adil. | were |

## 10 · Kesalahan yang dibuat hampir semua orang

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday I am at home.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~There was two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.
- ~~I was at home in Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.

**tip**
Seluruh tata bahasa baru di pelajaran ini cuma dua bentuk: was untuk I, he, she, it, dan satu benda; were untuk you, we, they, dan banyak benda. Sisanya sama seperti am, is, are.
