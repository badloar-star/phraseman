## Teaching Point

The learner should stop treating `book` and `books` as cosmetic variants. English uses surrounding signals:

- `a book`
- `two books`
- `this lesson`
- `these lessons`
- `more information`

## Copy Rules

- Learner-facing RU/UK copy avoids raw internal grammar labels.
- Keep tested English anchors in feedback where required.
- Explain by visible signals: `a`, `two`, `many`, `this`, `these`, and words like `information`.

## Structure

- Easy: `a book`, `two books`, `many questions`.
- Contrast: `this lesson`, `these lessons`, `few mistakes`, `boxes`, `stories`, `watches`.
- Mixed: `children`, `people`, `information`, pair repairs, full sentence repair.
