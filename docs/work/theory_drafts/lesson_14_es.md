# Teoría · Lección 14 del curso nuevo — «I was reading when you called» (ES)

> Sesión «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Secciones 01–10, frases en inglés y ejercicios como en
> `lesson_14_ru.md`. Trampas de un hispanohablante: «leía» se traduce con la forma simple (→ I read
> a comic en vez de I was reading), sujeto omitido (→ Was reading), pregunta con did (→ Did you
> playing?), «cuando» y «mientras» confundidos. Tratamiento: tú. Necesita revisión de un nativo.

**Título de la pantalla:** Teoría · Lo que estaba pasando cuando…

---

## 01 · Qué vas a aprender a decir *(abierta al inicio)*

**body**
En la lección 9 decías lo que pasa ahora: I am reading. Ahora vas a contar lo que estaba pasando en un momento del pasado: «estaba leyendo un cómic», «estaba limpiando las botas cuando llamaste», «olía las flores mientras yo hablaba».

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was reading a comic book. | Estaba leyendo un cómic. | was reading |
| They were doing a jigsaw puzzle. | Estaban armando un rompecabezas. | were doing |
| I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called. | Estaba limpiando mis botines cuando llamaste. | when |
| She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking. | Olía las flores mientras yo hablaba. | while |

---

## 02 · was / were + acción con -ing *(abierta al inicio)*

**body**
Aquí el español ayuda: «estaba leyendo» es I was reading. was / were hacen de «estaba», y -ing de «-ando / -iendo». Ojo: el «leía» del español también suele ser I was reading cuando hablas de lo que pasaba en ese momento.

**formula**
`quién` · `was / were` · `acción + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was reading a comic book. | Estaba leyendo un cómic. | was reading |
| She was using a colouring book. | Estaba usando un libro para colorear. | was using |
| I was cooking fried rice. | Estaba cocinando arroz frito. | was cooking |
| We were eating a spring roll. | Estábamos comiendo un rollito primavera. | were eating |
| They were watching the soap bubbles. | Estaban mirando las burbujas de jabón. | were watching |

**fix**
- ~~Was reading a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~I was read a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~They was doing a jigsaw puzzle.~~ → They were doing a jigsaw puzzle.

**drill · choice**
- frase: `They ___ doing a jigsaw puzzle.`
- opciones: `was` · `were` · `are`
- respuesta: `were`
- por qué: Con they en pasado, were; el -ing ya está.

---

## 03 · «No»: wasn't, weren't

**body**
El «no» va como siempre, después de la pieza: I wasn't playing, they weren't watching. wasn't es was not, weren't es were not. El -ing se queda.

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle. | No estaba jugando en el castillo inflable. | wasn't |
| I wasn't writing in the homework diary. | No estaba escribiendo en la agenda de tareas. | wasn't |
| I wasn't watching the robin. | No estaba mirando el petirrojo. | wasn't |

**fix**
- ~~I no was playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.
- ~~I didn't playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.

---

## 04 · Pregunta: Was…? Were…?

**body**
La pregunta: la pieza delante, como en las lecciones 2 y 9. Were you playing in the paddling pool? Was he watching the owl? Aquí no va did: Did you playing es un error.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `quién` · `acción + ing` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Were you playing in the paddling pool? | ¿Estabas jugando en la alberca inflable? | Were |
| Was he jumping on the trampoline? | ¿Estaba saltando en el trampolín? | Was |
| Were you using the workbook? | ¿Estabas usando el cuaderno de ejercicios? | Were |
| Was he watching the owl? | ¿Estaba mirando el búho? | Was |

**fix**
- ~~Did you playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~You were playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?

**drill · binary**
- pregunta: Pregunta si él estaba mirando el búho. ¿Cuál es correcta?
- A: `Did he watching the owl?`
- B: `Was he watching the owl?`
- correcta: B
- explicación: Con -ing, la pregunta empieza con was o were, no con did.

## 05 · El fondo de la historia

**body**
Esta forma sirve para pintar el fondo: qué estaba haciendo la gente cuando empezó la historia. I was changing a flat tyre — «estaba cambiando una llanta ponchada». No es un hecho aparte, sino algo que estaba en marcha.

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was changing a flat tyre. | Estaba cambiando una llanta ponchada. | was changing |
| He was checking the windscreen wiper. | Estaba revisando el limpiaparabrisas. | was checking |
| We were cleaning the rear-view mirror. | Estábamos limpiando el retrovisor. | were cleaning |
| She was packing the nappy bag. | Estaba preparando la pañalera. | was packing |

---

## 06 · when — algo pasó en medio

**body**
Si algo pasó en medio de un proceso, el proceso va con was / were + -ing, y el hecho, en pasado simple después de when: I was cleaning my boots when you called. La llamada fue corta y cortó lo otro.

**formula**
`was / were + ing` · `when` · `quién` · `hecho pasado`

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called. | Estaba limpiando mis botines cuando llamaste. | when you called |
| She was packing her ballet flats when I called. | Estaba guardando sus flats cuando llamé. | when I called |
| We were looking for an insole when you arrived. | Estábamos buscando una plantilla cuando llegaste. | when you arrived |
| She was drawing a piece of driftwood when I arrived. | Estaba dibujando un trozo de madera de playa cuando llegué. | when I arrived |

**fix**
- ~~I cleaned my ankle boots when you were calling.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.
- ~~I was cleaning my ankle boots when you were call.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.

**drill · word_bank**
- tarea: Di: «Estábamos buscando una plantilla cuando llegaste».
- respuesta: `We` · `were` · `looking` · `for` · `an` · `insole` · `when` · `you` · `arrived`
- etiquetas: ninguna (la frase es larga)
- fichas de más: `was` · `arriving`

## 07 · while — dos procesos a la vez

**body**
while es «mientras». Se usa cuando dos cosas estaban pasando al mismo tiempo, y las dos llevan was / were + -ing: She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking.

**formula**
`was / were + ing` · `while` · `quién` · `was / were + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was looking up while you were taking a photo. | Yo miraba hacia arriba mientras tomabas una foto. | while |
| She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking. | Olía las flores mientras yo hablaba. | while |
| We were giggling while he was telling the story. | Nos reíamos mientras él contaba la historia. | while |
| I was cutting celery while you were cooking. | Yo cortaba apio mientras tú cocinabas. | while |

**fix**
- ~~She was sniffing the flowers while I talked.~~ *(todo el rato)* → She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking.

---

## 08 · ¿when o while?

**body**
Un hecho corto que interrumpe: when + pasado simple — when you called («cuando llamaste»). Un proceso largo al mismo tiempo: while + was / were + -ing — while you were cooking («mientras cocinabas»).

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We were watching a wasp when you called. | Estábamos mirando una avispa cuando llamaste. | when |
| I was watching a butterfly while you were taking a photo. | Yo miraba una mariposa mientras tomabas una foto. | while |
| I was turning around when you called my name. | Me estaba volteando cuando dijiste mi nombre. | when |

**drill · choice**
- frase: `I was cutting celery ___ you were cooking.`
- opciones: `when` · `while` · `did`
- respuesta: `while`
- por qué: Dos procesos al mismo tiempo: while.

## 09 · Los errores que comete casi todo el mundo

**fix**
- ~~Was reading a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~I was read a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~They was doing a jigsaw puzzle.~~ → They were doing a jigsaw puzzle.
- ~~I didn't playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.
- ~~Did you playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~I cleaned my ankle boots when you were calling.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.

**tip**
Lo que estaba pasando: was / were + -ing. Lo corto que lo interrumpió: pasado simple después de when. Did no va con -ing.

## 10 · Qué llevarte a la práctica

**body**
Algo en marcha: was / were + -ing, siempre con quién. «No»: wasn't / weren't. Pregunta: Was…? Were…? Interrumpió: when + pasado simple. Al mismo tiempo: while + was / were + -ing.

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was filling a spray bottle. | Estaba llenando un atomizador. | was filling |
| Were you taking a photo of the woodpecker? | ¿Estabas tomando una foto del pájaro carpintero? | Were |
| I was looking at a starfish when you called. | Estaba mirando una estrella de mar cuando llamaste. | when |
| I was using a glue stick while you were drawing. | Yo usaba la barra de pegamento mientras dibujabas. | while |
