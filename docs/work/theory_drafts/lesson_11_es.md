# Teoría · Lección 11 del curso nuevo — «I was, they were, there was» (ES)

> Sesión «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Secciones 01–11, frases en inglés y ejercicios como en
> `lesson_11_ru.md`. Trampas de un hispanohablante: «estaba en casa» sin sujeto (→ Was at home),
> «había dos» con una sola forma (→ There was two pinzas), pregunta con la voz (→ It was warm?),
> «en Navidad» (→ in Christmas), «el domingo» (→ the Sunday). Tratamiento: tú.
> Necesita revisión de un nativo.

**Título de la pantalla:** Teoría · Lo que fue ayer

---

## 01 · Qué vas a aprender a decir *(abierta al inicio)*

**body**
Hasta ahora hablabas de lo que es ahora: I am at home. Ahora vas a contar lo que fue: «ayer estaba en casa», «en Navidad estuvimos juntos», «en la bolsa había dos pinzas». Para eso la pieza tiene una forma pasada.

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Ayer estaba en casa. | was |
| We were together at Christmas. | En Navidad estuvimos juntos. | were |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | En la bolsa había dos pinzas. | There were |
| Was it warm in spring? | ¿Hizo calor en primavera? | Was |

---

## 02 · was y were *(abierta al inicio)*

**body**
La pieza tiene dos formas pasadas. Donde iba am o is, ahora va was: I was, she was, it was. Donde iba are, ahora were: you were, we were, they were. Sirven para «era», «estaba», «fue», «estuvo»: en inglés no hay que elegir. Y siempre con quién al principio.

**formula**
`I / he / she / it` · `was` — `you / we / they` · `were`

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home yesterday. | Ayer estaba en casa. | was |
| She was in London last year. | El año pasado estuvo en Londres. | was |
| We were at home last night. | Anoche estábamos en casa. | were |
| They were in London at New Year. | En Año Nuevo estaban en Londres. | were |
| The hairdresser was busy yesterday. | Ayer la peluquera estaba ocupada. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Was at home yesterday.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~I were at home yesterday.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.

**drill · choice**
- frase: `We ___ at the train station yesterday.`
- opciones: `was` · `were` · `are`
- respuesta: `were`
- por qué: Con we, en pasado, were.

## 03 · Marcas del pasado: yesterday, last night, on Sunday

**body**
Casi siempre hay una palabra que marca el pasado: yesterday — «ayer», last night — «anoche», last year — «el año pasado». Para fiestas, at: at Christmas, at New Year. Para días, on: on Sunday — sin «the».

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was at home on Sunday. | El domingo estaba en casa. | on Sunday |
| We were together at Christmas. | En Navidad estuvimos juntos. | at Christmas |
| They were in London at New Year. | En Año Nuevo estaban en Londres. | at New Year |
| The sports centre was closed yesterday. | Ayer el polideportivo estaba cerrado. | yesterday |

**fix**
- ~~We were together in Christmas.~~ → We were together at Christmas.
- ~~I was at home the Sunday.~~ → I was at home on Sunday.

---

## 04 · Ayer — was, ahora — is

**body**
Si hablas de ayer, was o were. Si hablas de ahora, is o are, como antes. La palabra yesterday o now ya te lo dice: Yesterday my towel was wet. The shower curtain is dry now.

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday my beach towel was wet. | Ayer mi toalla de playa estaba mojada. | was |
| Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty. | Ayer mis chanclas estaban sucias. | were |
| The shower curtain is dry now. | Ahora la cortina de la regadera está seca. | is |
| The bathroom mirror was dirty yesterday. | Ayer el espejo del baño estaba sucio. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~Yesterday my flip-flops was dirty.~~ → Yesterday my flip-flops were dirty.

**drill · binary**
- pregunta: Ayer la toalla estaba mojada. ¿Cómo lo dices?
- A: `Yesterday my beach towel is wet.`
- B: `Yesterday my beach towel was wet.`
- correcta: B
- explicación: Ayer es pasado: was.

## 05 · Lo que no se cuenta por piezas — was

**body**
Como en la lección 4, lo que no se cuenta por piezas — crema, comida, jugo — va con la forma de uno: was, no were. Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag. | Ayer el bloqueador estaba en mi bolsa. | was |
| The apple juice was cold yesterday. | Ayer el jugo de manzana estaba frío. | was |
| The hot chocolate was too sweet yesterday. | Ayer el chocolate caliente estaba demasiado dulce. | was |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday the sun cream were in my bag.~~ → Yesterday the sun cream was in my bag.
- ~~The baby food were in the cupboard.~~ → The baby food was in the cupboard.

---

## 06 · «No estaba»: was not, were not

**body**
En español «no» va delante: «no estaba». En inglés not va después de la pieza: It was not my day off. The fried egg was not cold. Para varios, were not.

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It was not my day off. | No era mi día libre. | was not |
| The fried egg was not cold. | El huevo frito no estaba frío. | was not |
| The peanut butter was not on the table. | La crema de cacahuate no estaba en la mesa. | was not |

**fix**
- ~~It no was my day off.~~ → It was not my day off.
- ~~The fried egg didn't cold.~~ → The fried egg was not cold.

---

## 07 · Pregunta: Was…? Were…?

**body**
La pregunta se arma como en la lección 2: la pieza delante. Was it warm in spring? Were you at home in autumn? La respuesta corta también con la pieza: Yes, it was. No, it was not. Aquí no van do ni did.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `quién / qué` · `…` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Was it warm in spring? | ¿Hizo calor en primavera? | Was |
| Were you at home in autumn? | ¿Estabas en casa en otoño? | Were |
| Was your pancake hot? | ¿Tu hot cake estaba caliente? | Was |
| Were the scrambled eggs hot? | ¿Los huevos revueltos estaban calientes? | Were |
| Yes, it was. | Sí. | was |

**fix**
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Were your pancake hot?~~ → Was your pancake hot?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.

**drill · word_bank**
- tarea: Pregunta: «¿Hizo calor en primavera?»
- respuesta: `Was` · `it` · `warm` · `in` · `spring`
- etiquetas: ninguna (la frase es larga)
- fichas de más: `Were` · `Did`

---

## 08 · «Había»: there was, there were

**body**
En español «había» sirve para uno y para muchos. En inglés no: There was para una cosa, There were para varias. There was a towel rail in the bathroom. There were two clothes pegs in the bag.

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| There was a bed sheet on the bed. | En la cama había una sábana. | There was |
| There were two clothes pegs in the bag. | En la bolsa había dos pinzas. | There were |
| There was a public toilet near the station. | Cerca de la estación había un baño público. | There was |
| There were two taxi drivers near the station. | Cerca de la estación había dos taxistas. | There were |
| There was fruit salad in the bowl. | En el tazón había ensalada de frutas. | There was |

**fix**
- ~~There was two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~Had two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.

**drill · choice**
- frase: `There ___ two duvet covers in the cupboard.`
- opciones: `was` · `were` · `is`
- respuesta: `were`
- por qué: Son dos fundas, así que There were.

## 09 · Opinar sobre lo que pasó

**body**
Con was es fácil opinar sobre algo pasado: That was a silly idea — «fue una idea tonta», The plan was sensible — «el plan era sensato». Para varias cosas, were: The rules were fair.

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| That was a silly idea. | Fue una idea tonta. | was |
| The plan was sensible. | El plan era sensato. | was |
| The rules were fair. | Las reglas eran justas. | were |

## 10 · Los errores que comete casi todo el mundo

**fix**
- ~~Was at home yesterday.~~ → I was at home yesterday.
- ~~We was at home last night.~~ → We were at home last night.
- ~~Yesterday my beach towel is wet.~~ → Yesterday my beach towel was wet.
- ~~There was two clothes pegs in the bag.~~ → There were two clothes pegs in the bag.
- ~~It was warm in spring?~~ → Was it warm in spring?
- ~~Yes, it did.~~ → Yes, it was.
- ~~We were together in Christmas.~~ → We were together at Christmas.

**tip**
Toda la gramática nueva son dos formas: was para I, he, she, it y una cosa; were para you, we, they y varias. Lo demás, igual que con am, is, are.

## 11 · Qué llevarte a la práctica

**body**
Fue, estaba: was o were, siempre con quién. No estaba: was not, were not. Pregunta: Was…? Were…?, respuesta: Yes, it was. Había: There was / There were. Ayer: was; ahora: is.

**examples** 🔊
| en | es | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She was in London last year. | El año pasado estuvo en Londres. | was |
| It was not my day off. | No era mi día libre. | was not |
| Were you at home in autumn? | ¿Estabas en casa en otoño? | Were |
| There was a soap dish next to the washbasin. | Junto al lavabo había una jabonera. | There was |
