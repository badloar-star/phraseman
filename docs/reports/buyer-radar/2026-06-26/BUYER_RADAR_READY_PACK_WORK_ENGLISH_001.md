```text
I keep seeing the same pattern with English learners who work in international teams:

They can read Slack.
They can understand colleagues.
They can watch technical videos.

But when someone says, "Can you explain the issue quickly?", their English suddenly collapses into very basic sentences.

My current theory: the problem is not always grammar knowledge. It is phrase access under pressure.

For work calls, it helps to train reusable sentence blocks:

- "Quick update from my side..."
- "The main issue is..."
- "Let me clarify one thing."
- "I can check that and get back to you."
- "What I mean is..."

These phrases are not impressive, but they reduce panic because they give your brain a starting point.

Curious: if you use English at work, which sentence do you wish came out automatically?
```
