# Teori · Pelajaran 18 kursus baru — «cheaper than, the cheapest, as … as, too / enough, so / such» (ID)

> Sesi «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 22.10.2026. Bagian 01–08, kalimat bahasa Inggris dan latihan sama seperti
> `lesson_18_ru.md`. Jebakan penutur Indonesia: «lebih murah» → more cheap / more cheaper,
> «paling murah» → most cheap tanpa the, «sama kuatnya dengan» → as strong with / same strong,
> «cukup ringan» → enough light, «sangat» untuk so dan such (→ so a helpful person). Sapaan: kamu.
> Perlu dibaca penutur asli.

**Judul layar:** Teori · Membandingkan

---

## 01 · Apa yang akan kamu pelajari *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Pelajaran ini tentang membandingkan: «kamar ini lebih murah dari kamar kita», «toko ini pengirimannya paling cepat», «kopi ini sekuat punyamu», «tasnya terlalu besar», «hubungan kita baik sekali». Hampir semuanya cuma tambahan kecil pada kata sifat.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This hotel room is cheaper than ours. | Kamar hotel ini lebih murah dari kamar kita. | cheaper than |
| This shop has the shortest delivery time. | Pengiriman toko ini paling cepat. | the shortest |
| This instant coffee is as strong as yours. | Kopi instan ini sekuat punyamu. | as strong as |
| This cabin bag is too big. | Tas kabin ini terlalu besar. | too big |
| We have such a good relationship. | Hubungan kita baik sekali. | such a |

---

## 02 · -er than — «lebih murah dari» *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Untuk «lebih murah», «lebih tenang», «lebih panjang», kata sifat diberi -er, dan «dari / daripada» adalah than: cheaper than ours. «Lebih» sudah ada di dalam -er, jadi jangan tambah more: bukan more cheaper.

**formula**
`apa` · `is` · `kata sifat + er` · `than` · `dibanding apa`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This hotel room is cheaper than ours. | Kamar hotel ini lebih murah dari kamar kita. | cheaper than |
| The shared bathroom is cleaner than before. | Kamar mandi bersama lebih bersih dari sebelumnya. | cleaner than |
| The train journey is shorter than ours. | Perjalanan kereta lebih singkat dari perjalanan kita. | shorter than |
| The airport bus is slower than a taxi. | Bus bandara lebih lambat daripada taksi. | slower than |
| These leather shoes are softer than that pair. | Sepatu kulit ini lebih lembut dari pasangan yang itu. | softer than |

**fix**
- ~~This hotel room is more cheaper than ours.~~ → This hotel room is cheaper than ours.
- ~~This hotel room is cheap than ours.~~ → This hotel room is cheaper than ours.
- ~~The shared bathroom is cleaner that before.~~ → The shared bathroom is cleaner than before.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `The top floor is ___ than this floor.`
- pilihan: `quieter` · `more quieter` · `quiet`
- jawaban: `quieter`
- kenapa: «Lebih tenang» — kata sifat + -er, tanpa more.

---

## 03 · the -est — «paling …»

**body**
Kalau memilih satu dari semuanya — «paling murah», «paling dekat» — kata sifat diberi -est dan di depannya wajib the: the shortest delivery time, the nearest shop. «Paling» sudah ada di -est: bukan most cheapest.

**formula**
`the` · `kata sifat + est` · `(benda)`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This shop has the shortest delivery time. | Pengiriman toko ini paling cepat. | the shortest |
| This clinic has the longest waiting time. | Di klinik ini antrenya paling lama. | the longest |
| This local shop is the nearest. | Warung dekat rumah ini yang paling dekat. | the nearest |
| The plain yoghurt is the cheapest option. | Yoghurt tawar pilihan paling murah. | the cheapest |
| We booked the earliest home visit. | Kami pesan kunjungan rumah paling awal. | the earliest |

**fix**
- ~~This shop has shortest delivery time.~~ → This shop has the shortest delivery time.
- ~~This shop has the most shortest delivery time.~~ → This shop has the shortest delivery time.
- ~~This shop has the shorter delivery time.~~ *(dari semuanya)* → This shop has the shortest delivery time.

**drill · binary**
- pertanyaan: Kamu melihat tiga toko. Yang ini paling besar. Kamu bilang apa?
- A: `This chain store is the larger.`
- B: `This chain store is the largest.`
- benar: B
- penjelasan: Satu dari semuanya — the + -est.

## 04 · as … as — «se- …»

**body**
Kalau tidak ada bedanya — «sekuat punyamu» — kata sifat diletakkan di antara dua as: as strong as yours. Bahasa Indonesia cukup awalan «se-», bahasa Inggris perlu as di depan dan di belakang, bukan with atau same.

**formula**
`apa` · `is` · `as` · `kata sifat` · `as` · `dibanding apa`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This instant coffee is as strong as yours. | Kopi instan ini sekuat punyamu. | as strong as |
| This green tea is as cheap as that one. | Teh hijau ini semurah yang itu. | as cheap as |
| These reading glasses are as light as mine. | Kacamata baca ini seringan punyaku. | as light as |
| This electric kettle is as fast as ours. | Teko listrik ini secepat punya kita. | as fast as |

**fix**
- ~~This instant coffee is as strong with yours.~~ → This instant coffee is as strong as yours.
- ~~This instant coffee is as strong than yours.~~ → This instant coffee is as strong as yours.
- ~~This ground coffee is fresh as yours.~~ → This ground coffee is as fresh as yours.

---

## 05 · too dan enough — «terlalu» dan «cukup»

**body**
too — «terlalu», mengganggu: This cabin bag is too big. enough — «cukup», pas: This checked bag is light enough. Bahasa Indonesia bilang «cukup ringan», bahasa Inggris menaruh enough di belakang: light enough.

**formula**
`mengganggu: too + kata sifat` · `pas: kata sifat + enough`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| This cabin bag is too big. | Tas kabin ini terlalu besar. | too big |
| This checked bag is light enough. | Bagasi ini cukup ringan. | light enough |
| The monthly rent is too high. | Sewa bulanannya terlalu mahal. | too high |
| The rental deposit is low enough. | Uang jaminan sewanya cukup rendah. | low enough |
| This driveway is too narrow. | Jalan masuk mobil ini terlalu sempit. | too narrow |

**fix**
- ~~This checked bag is enough light.~~ → This checked bag is light enough.
- ~~This cabin bag is big too.~~ → This cabin bag is too big.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `This window lock is strong ___.` *(kuncinya pas, tidak perlu diganti)*
- pilihan: `enough` · `too` · `so`
- jawaban: `enough`
- kenapa: «Cukup» — enough, letaknya setelah kata sifat.

---

## 06 · so dan such — «… sekali»

**body**
Kalau tidak ada benda sesudahnya — so: My close friend is so kind («baik sekali»). Kalau sesudah kata sifat ada benda — such: such a helpful person, such hard work. Urutannya such a, bukan a such.

**formula**
`tanpa benda: so + kata sifat` · `dengan benda: such a + kata sifat + benda`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| My close friend is so kind. | Sahabatku baik sekali. | so |
| Our mutual friend is such a helpful person. | Teman kita bersama orangnya suka menolong sekali. | such a |
| This is such hard work. | Ini pekerjaan yang berat sekali. | such |
| The live music is so loud. | Musik langsungnya keras sekali. | so |
| You have such a good memory. | Ingatanmu bagus sekali. | such a |

**fix**
- ~~Our mutual friend is so a helpful person.~~ → Our mutual friend is such a helpful person.
- ~~My close friend is such kind.~~ → My close friend is so kind.
- ~~Our mutual friend is a such helpful person.~~ → Our mutual friend is such a helpful person.

**drill · word_bank**
- tugas: Katakan: «Hubungan kita baik sekali».
- jawaban: `We` · `have` · `such` · `a` · `good` · `relationship`
- label kotak: tidak ada (kalimat panjang)
- kartu lebih: `so` · `much`

## 07 · Kesalahan yang dibuat hampir semua orang

**fix**
- ~~This hotel room is more cheaper than ours.~~ → This hotel room is cheaper than ours.
- ~~This shop has shortest delivery time.~~ → This shop has the shortest delivery time.
- ~~This instant coffee is as strong with yours.~~ → This instant coffee is as strong as yours.
- ~~This checked bag is enough light.~~ → This checked bag is light enough.
- ~~Our mutual friend is so a helpful person.~~ → Our mutual friend is such a helpful person.

**tip**
Dua hal — -er than. Satu dari semua — the -est. Sama — as … as. Mengganggu — too di depan, pas — enough di belakang. Tanpa benda — so, dengan benda — such a.

## 08 · Bekal untuk latihan

**body**
-er than — membandingkan dua hal. the -est — memilih satu dari semua. as … as — «sama». too / enough — «terlalu» dan «cukup». so / such — so tanpa benda, such dengan benda.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| The sale price is lower than before. | Harga diskonnya lebih rendah dari sebelumnya. | lower than |
| This dining table is the widest. | Meja makan ini paling lebar. | the widest |
| This is such a big improvement. | Ini kemajuan yang besar sekali. | such a |
