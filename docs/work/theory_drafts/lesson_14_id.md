## 01 · Apa yang akan kamu pelajari *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Di pelajaran 9 kamu bilang apa yang sedang terjadi sekarang: I am reading. Sekarang kamu bercerita apa yang sedang terjadi pada suatu saat di masa lalu: «waktu itu aku sedang membaca komik», «aku sedang membersihkan sepatu bot waktu kamu menelepon», «dia mencium bunga sementara aku bicara».

## 02 · was / were + kegiatan dengan -ing *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Kita cukup bilang «waktu itu» dan «sedang». Bahasa Inggris perlu dua bagian: was atau were, dan -ing. Ini pelajaran 9 yang dipindah ke masa lalu: am / is / are jadi was / were. I was reading — bukan I reading atau I am reading.

**formula**
`siapa` · `was / were` · `kegiatan + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was reading a comic book. | Waktu itu aku sedang membaca komik. | was reading |
| She was using a colouring book. | Dia sedang mewarnai buku mewarnai. | was using |
| I was cooking fried rice. | Aku sedang memasak nasi goreng. | was cooking |
| We were eating a spring roll. | Kami sedang makan lumpia. | were eating |
| They were watching the soap bubbles. | Mereka sedang melihat gelembung sabun. | were watching |

**fix**
- ~~I reading a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~I am reading a comic book.~~ *(cerita kemarin)* → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~They was doing a jigsaw puzzle.~~ → They were doing a jigsaw puzzle.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `They ___ doing a jigsaw puzzle.`
- pilihan: `was` · `were` · `are`
- jawaban: `were`
- kenapa: Dengan they di masa lalu — were; -ing sudah ada.

## 03 · «Tidak»: wasn't, weren't

**body**
«Tidak» seperti biasa, sesudah was atau were: I wasn't playing, they weren't watching. wasn't adalah was not, weren't adalah were not. -ing tetap ada.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle. | Waktu itu aku tidak sedang main di istana balon. | wasn't |
| I wasn't writing in the homework diary. | Aku tidak sedang menulis di buku PR. | wasn't |
| I wasn't watching the robin. | Aku tidak sedang melihat burung robin. | wasn't |

**fix**
- ~~I not playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.
- ~~I didn't playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.

## 04 · Pertanyaan: Was…? Were…?

**body**
Kita bertanya tanpa mengubah urutan. Bahasa Inggris memindahkan was / were ke depan: Were you playing in the paddling pool? Tidak pakai did: Did you playing itu salah.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `siapa` · `kegiatan + ing` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Were you playing in the paddling pool? | Waktu itu kamu sedang main di kolam karet? | Were |
| Was he jumping on the trampoline? | Dia sedang lompat di trampolin? | Was |
| Were you using the workbook? | Kamu sedang memakai buku latihan? | Were |
| Was he watching the owl? | Dia sedang melihat burung hantu? | Was |

**fix**
- ~~Did you playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~You were playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?

**drill · binary**
- pertanyaan: Tanyakan apakah waktu itu dia sedang melihat burung hantu. Mana yang benar?
- A: `Did he watching the owl?`
- B: `Was he watching the owl?`
- benar: B
- penjelasan: Dengan -ing, pertanyaan dimulai dengan was atau were, bukan did.

Bentuk ini dipakai untuk menggambar latar: apa yang sedang dilakukan orang saat cerita dimulai. I was changing a flat tyre — «aku sedang mengganti ban kempes». Ini bukan kejadian tersendiri, tapi sesuatu yang sedang berlangsung.

## 06 · when — sesuatu terjadi di tengahnya

**body**
Kalau sesuatu terjadi di tengah kegiatan: kegiatannya — was / were + -ing, kejadiannya — bentuk lampau biasa sesudah when: I was cleaning my boots when you called. Teleponnya singkat dan memotong kegiatan.

**formula**
`was / were + ing` · `when` · `siapa` · `kejadian lampau`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called. | Aku sedang membersihkan sepatu botku waktu kamu menelepon. | when you called |
| She was packing her ballet flats when I called. | Dia sedang mengemas sepatu balet waktu aku menelepon. | when I called |
| We were looking for an insole when you arrived. | Kami sedang mencari sol dalam waktu kamu datang. | when you arrived |
| She was drawing a piece of driftwood when I arrived. | Dia sedang menggambar kayu hanyut waktu aku datang. | when I arrived |

**fix**
- ~~I cleaned my ankle boots when you were calling.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.
- ~~I was cleaning my ankle boots when you call.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.

**drill · word_bank**
- tugas: Katakan: «Kami sedang mencari sol dalam waktu kamu datang».
- jawaban: `We` · `were` · `looking` · `for` · `an` · `insole` · `when` · `you` · `arrived`
- label: tidak ada (kalimatnya panjang)
- kartu lebih: `was` · `arriving`

## 07 · while — dua kegiatan bersamaan

**body**
while artinya «sementara». Dipakai kalau dua kegiatan berlangsung bersamaan, dan kedua bagian memakai was / were + -ing: She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking.

**formula**
`was / were + ing` · `while` · `siapa` · `was / were + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was looking up while you were taking a photo. | Aku melihat ke atas sementara kamu memotret. | while |
| She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking. | Dia mencium bunga sementara aku sedang bicara. | while |
| We were giggling while he was telling the story. | Kami cekikikan sementara dia bercerita. | while |
| I was cutting celery while you were cooking. | Aku memotong seledri sementara kamu memasak. | while |

**fix**
- ~~She was sniffing the flowers while I talked.~~ *(selama itu)* → She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking.

## 08 · when atau while?

**body**
Kita sering pakai «waktu» atau «saat» untuk keduanya. Bahasa Inggris membedakan: kejadian singkat yang memotong — when + lampau biasa: when you called. Kegiatan panjang yang bersamaan — while + was / were + -ing.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We were watching a wasp when you called. | Kami sedang melihat tawon waktu kamu menelepon. | when |
| I was watching a butterfly while you were taking a photo. | Aku melihat kupu-kupu sementara kamu memotret. | while |
| I was turning around when you called my name. | Aku sedang menoleh waktu kamu memanggil namaku. | when |

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `I was cutting celery ___ you were cooking.`
- pilihan: `when` · `while` · `did`
- jawaban: `while`
- kenapa: Dua kegiatan berlangsung bersamaan: while.

## 09 · Kesalahan yang dibuat hampir semua orang

**fix**
- ~~I reading a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~They was doing a jigsaw puzzle.~~ → They were doing a jigsaw puzzle.
- ~~I didn't playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.
- ~~Did you playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~I am reading a comic book.~~ *(cerita kemarin)* → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~I cleaned my ankle boots when you were calling.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.

**tip**
«Sedang» di masa lalu punya dua bagian: was / were dan -ing. Kejadian singkat yang memotong — lampau biasa sesudah when. Did tidak dipakai dengan -ing.
