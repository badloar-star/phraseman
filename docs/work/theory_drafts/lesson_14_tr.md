# Teori · Yeni kursun 14. dersi — «I was reading when you called» (TR)

> «12 · ТЕОРИЯ» oturumu, 21.10.2026. Bölümler 01–10, İngilizce cümleler ve alıştırmalar
> `lesson_14_ru.md` ile aynı. Türkçe konuşanların tuzakları: «-iyordu» tek ek, İngilizcede iki parça
> (→ I was read / I reading), soru «-iyor muydun?» sıra değişmeden ya da did ile (→ Did you
> playing?), «-ken» ile while'ın yeri. Hitap: sen. Yayından önce ana dili Türkçe olan biri okumalı.

**Ekran başlığı:** Teori · …-ken ne oluyordu

## 01 · Neler söylemeyi öğreneceksin *(baştan açık)*

**body**
9. derste şu an olanı söyledin: I am reading. Şimdi geçmişte bir anda ne olduğunu anlatacaksın: «çizgi roman okuyordum», «sen aradığında botlarımı temizliyordum», «ben konuşurken çiçekleri kokluyordu».

**examples** 🔊

|---|---|---|

## 02 · was / were + -ing'li eylem *(baştan açık)*

**body**
Türkçede tek ek yeter: «okuyordum». İngilizcede iki parça gerekir: was ya da were ve -ing'li eylem. Bu, 9. dersin geçmişe taşınmış hali: am / is / are yerine was / were. I was reading.

**formula**
`kim` · `was / were` · `eylem + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was reading a comic book. | Çizgi roman okuyordum. | was reading |
| She was using a colouring book. | Boyama kitabı boyuyordu. | was using |
| I was cooking fried rice. | Kızarmış pilav yapıyordum. | was cooking |
| We were eating a spring roll. | Çin böreği yiyorduk. | were eating |
| They were watching the soap bubbles. | Sabun köpüklerini izliyorlardı. | were watching |

**fix**
- ~~I was read a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~I reading a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~They was doing a jigsaw puzzle.~~ → They were doing a jigsaw puzzle.

**drill · choice**
- cümle: `They ___ doing a jigsaw puzzle.`
- seçenekler: `was` · `were` · `are`
- cevap: `were`
- neden: they ile geçmişte were; -ing zaten var.

## 03 · «-mıyordu»: wasn't, weren't

**body**
Türkçede olumsuzluk ekin içindedir: «oynamıyordum». İngilizcede not, was ya da were'den sonra gelir: I wasn't playing, they weren't watching. -ing kalır.

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle. | Şişme kalede oynamıyordum. | wasn't |
| I wasn't writing in the homework diary. | Ödev defterine yazmıyordum. | wasn't |
| I wasn't watching the robin. | Kızılgerdanı izlemiyordum. | wasn't |

**fix**
- ~~I didn't playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.
- ~~I wasn't play on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.

## 04 · Soru: Was…? Were…?

**body**
Türkçede soru sondaki «mu» ile yapılır: «oynuyor muydun?». İngilizcede was ya da were başa geçer: Were you playing in the paddling pool? did burada yok: Did you playing yanlış.

**formula**
`Was / Were` · `kim` · `eylem + ing` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Were you playing in the paddling pool? | Şişme havuzda oynuyor muydun? | Were |
| Was he jumping on the trampoline? | Trambolinde zıplıyor muydu? | Was |
| Were you using the workbook? | Alıştırma kitabını kullanıyor muydun? | Were |
| Was he watching the owl? | Baykuşu izliyor muydu? | Was |

**fix**
- ~~Did you playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~You were playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?

**drill · binary**
- soru: O sırada baykuşu izleyip izlemediğini sor. Hangisi doğru?
- A: `Did he watching the owl?`
- B: `Was he watching the owl?`
- doğru: B
- açıklama: -ing ile soru was ya da were ile başlar, did ile değil.

## 06 · when — bir şey araya girdi

**body**
Süren bir işin ortasında bir şey olduysa: süren iş was / were + -ing, olay ise when'den sonra basit geçmiş: I was cleaning my boots when you called. Arama kısaydı, temizliği böldü.

**formula**
`was / were + ing` · `when` · `kim` · `geçmiş olay`

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called. | Sen aradığında botlarımı temizliyordum. | when you called |
| She was packing her ballet flats when I called. | Ben aradığımda babetlerini topluyordu. | when I called |
| We were looking for an insole when you arrived. | Sen geldiğinde tabanlık arıyorduk. | when you arrived |
| She was drawing a piece of driftwood when I arrived. | Ben geldiğimde bir parça deniz odunu çiziyordu. | when I arrived |

**fix**
- ~~I cleaned my ankle boots when you were calling.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.
- ~~When you called I cleaning my boots.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.

**drill · word_bank**
- görev: Söyle: «Sen geldiğinde tabanlık arıyorduk».
- cevap: `We` · `were` · `looking` · `for` · `an` · `insole` · `when` · `you` · `arrived`
- etiketler: yok (cümle uzun)
- fazla kartlar: `was` · `arriving`

## 07 · while — iki iş aynı anda

**body**
while — Türkçedeki «-ken». İki iş aynı anda sürüyorsa kullanılır ve iki tarafta da was / were + -ing olur: She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking — «ben konuşurken çiçekleri kokluyordu».

**formula**
`was / were + ing` · `while` · `kim` · `was / were + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I was looking up while you were taking a photo. | Sen fotoğraf çekerken ben yukarı bakıyordum. | while |
| She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking. | Ben konuşurken çiçekleri kokluyordu. | while |
| We were giggling while he was telling the story. | O hikâyeyi anlatırken kıkırdıyorduk. | while |
| I was cutting celery while you were cooking. | Sen yemek yaparken ben kereviz doğruyordum. | while |

**fix**
- ~~She was sniffing the flowers while I talked.~~ *(bütün süre boyunca)* → She was sniffing the flowers while I was talking.

## 08 · when mi, while mı?

**body**
Süren işi bölen kısa olay — when + basit geçmiş: when you called («sen aradığında»). Aynı anda süren uzun iş — while + was / were + -ing: while you were cooking («sen yemek yaparken»).

**examples** 🔊
| en | tr | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We were watching a wasp when you called. | Sen aradığında bir eşek arısını izliyorduk. | when |
| I was watching a butterfly while you were taking a photo. | Sen fotoğraf çekerken ben bir kelebeği izliyordum. | while |
| I was turning around when you called my name. | Adımı seslendiğinde arkamı dönüyordum. | when |

**drill · choice**
- cümle: `I was cutting celery ___ you were cooking.`
- seçenekler: `when` · `while` · `did`
- cevap: `while`
- neden: İki iş aynı anda sürüyordu: while.

## 09 · Neredeyse herkesin yaptığı hatalar

**fix**
- ~~I was read a comic book.~~ → I was reading a comic book.
- ~~They was doing a jigsaw puzzle.~~ → They were doing a jigsaw puzzle.
- ~~I didn't playing on the bouncy castle.~~ → I wasn't playing on the bouncy castle.
- ~~Did you playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~You were playing in the paddling pool?~~ → Were you playing in the paddling pool?
- ~~I cleaned my ankle boots when you were calling.~~ → I was cleaning my ankle boots when you called.

**tip**
Geçmişte süren iş — was / were + -ing. Onu bölen kısa olay — when'den sonra basit geçmiş. did, -ing ile birlikte kullanılmaz.
