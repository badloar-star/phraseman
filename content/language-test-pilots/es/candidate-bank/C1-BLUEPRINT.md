## C1 boundary

- Reading keys must depend on discourse relations, evidential scope, stance, or multi-step inference explicitly recoverable from the text.
- Pragmatic keys must satisfy every named component; distractors remain sophisticated and locally plausible while failing one decisive component.
- Grammar tasks must require discourse-sensitive selection, not isolated conjugation that already belongs to B1/B2.
- Vocabulary must be precise but internationally interpretable; no literary rarity, local bureaucracy, or specialist law is allowed to create artificial difficulty.
