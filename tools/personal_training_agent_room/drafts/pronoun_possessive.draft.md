# pronoun_possessive

Jesse-reworked personal training for the logged mistake: learner confuses the
word before a thing (`my phone`, `your bag`, `their tickets`) with the word that
stands alone after the thing is already known (`mine`, `yours`, `theirs`).

Scope:

- One category only: "word before the thing" vs "word that replaces the thing".
- Beginner-safe RU/UK/ES copy.
- No learner-facing English grammar jargon in RU/UK explanations.
- Every wrong option has specific feedback tied to that option.
- Includes the common `its` vs `it's` trap without mixing in broader pronoun case.

Implementation:

- App file: `app/diagnosis_training_pronoun_possessive.ts`
- Registry id: `pronoun_possessive`
- Marker: `JESSE_REWORKED_PERSONAL_TRAINING`
