## C2 boundary

- Every key must require exact integration of discourse scope, stance, evidence, register, or interactional constraints. A rare word alone is never a C2 construct.
- Reading tasks must remain answerable from the supplied text; specialist legal, scientific, political, or cultural knowledge cannot decide the key.
- Pragmatic keys must satisfy every named constraint. Distractors should sound competent and fail one decisive component rather than become rude caricatures.
- Grammar tasks must test discourse-sensitive form selection or compression; isolated conjugation and terminology recall are insufficient.
- Vocabulary must be internationally interpretable standard Spanish. Regionalisms may appear only when explicitly glossed and cannot determine correctness.
- EN/RU service copy and individually authored Russian distractor rationales remain mandatory.
- `reviewStatus` remains `self_checked`; qualified external review remains `pending`.
- C2 cannot enter `completedLevels` before item-by-item linguistic review, structural gates, adaptive-fit review, and cross-level boundary review all pass.
