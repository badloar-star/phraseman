# verb_present_perfect_basic

Jesse rework pass: Present Perfect basic.

Scope:
- One narrow repair: build Present Perfect with `have/has + V3`.
- Keep `have` with `I/you/we/they` and `has` with `he/she/it`.
- Train the high-frequency V3 traps: `seen`, `been`, `bought`, `done`, `lost`.
- Add only a small boundary with `yesterday -> Past Simple`; the full contrast belongs to `present_perfect_vs_past_simple`.

Required traps:
- `have saw` -> `have seen`
- `she have` -> `she has`
- negative `already` -> `yet`
- `I have lost my keys yesterday.` -> `Past Simple`
- `have already did` -> `have already done`

Learner-facing copy rule:
- Russian and Ukrainian text avoids internal grammar jargon such as `subject`, `object`, `base verb`, `main verb`, `auxiliary`, and `modal`.
