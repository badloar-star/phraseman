# Theory · New course, lesson 20 — «must, have to, should, ought to, mustn't, don't have to, needn't» (EN)

> Session «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 22.10.2026. Sections 01–10, English phrases and drills as in
> `lesson_20_ru.md`. Plain English for learners whose language is not in the list; the second
> column explains the meaning instead of translating. Common slips: to after must or should, no to
> after ought or have, mustn't used to mean «not necessary».

**Screen title:** Theory · Must, should, mustn't

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open from the start)*

**body**
This lesson is about rules and advice: what is required, what you need to do, what is a good idea, what is not allowed, and what is not necessary. The key point: mustn't and don't have to mean very different things.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She must show identification. | it is required | must |
| I have to read the contract. | I need to, because of the situation | have to |
| You should consult a teacher. | it is a good idea | should |
| You mustn't ignore the rules. | it is not allowed | mustn't |
| I don't have to buy supplies. | it is not necessary | don't have to |

---

## 02 · must — it is required *(open from the start)*

**body**
must + the action as in the dictionary means it is required: You must take your belongings. must is the same for everyone, has no -s, and never has to after it.

**formula**
`who` · `must` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| You must take your belongings. | do not leave them | must |
| She must show identification. | an ID is required | must |
| We must check the expiry date. | it is important to check | must |
| We must obey the rules. | rules are required | must |
| You must be respectful. | polite behaviour is required | must be |

**fix**
- ~~You must to take your belongings.~~ → You must take your belongings.
- ~~You musts take your belongings.~~ → You must take your belongings.

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `She must ___ identification.`
- options: `show` · `shows` · `to show`
- answer: `show`
- why: After must: the action as in the dictionary, no -s, no to.

---

## 03 · have to — I need to

**body**
have to + action means you need to, often because of the situation: I have to read the contract. With he / she use has to. Without to the meaning changes: I have read means it is already done.

**formula**
`I / you / we / they: have to + action` · `he / she: has to + action`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I have to read the contract. | before the meeting | have to |
| She has to enter her occupation. | the form asks for her job | has to |
| I occasionally have to work late. | sometimes it is necessary | have to |
| He has to follow the diversion. | the road is closed | has to |
| I have to show proof of purchase. | the shop asks for the receipt | have to |

**fix**
- ~~I has to read the contract.~~ → I have to read the contract.
- ~~She have to enter her occupation.~~ → She has to enter her occupation.
- ~~I have read the contract.~~ *(it is needed)* → I have to read the contract.

---

## 04 · should — a good idea

**body**
should + action is advice: it is a good idea. You should consult a teacher. should also has no -s and no to.

**formula**
`who` · `should` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| You should consult a teacher. | ask a teacher for advice | should |
| You should sleep on it. | decide tomorrow | should |
| He should get some rest. | he is tired | should |
| We should use up the milk. | finish it before it goes bad | should |
| She should spend sensibly. | not waste money | should |

**fix**
- ~~You should to consult a teacher.~~ → You should consult a teacher.
- ~~You shoulds consult a teacher.~~ → You should consult a teacher.

---

## 05 · ought to — also advice

**body**
ought to is also advice, almost like should, but with to: You ought to set a budget. Here to is needed, and there is no -s.

**formula**
`who` · `ought to` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| You ought to set a budget. | plan your spending | ought to |
| We ought to find the cause. | find why it happened | ought to |
| You ought to keep in mind the departure time. | remember when it leaves | ought to |
| We ought to express our gratitude. | say thank you | ought to |

**fix**
- ~~You ought read the national news.~~ → You ought to read the national news.
- ~~You oughts to read the national news.~~ → You ought to read the national news.

**drill · choice**
- phrase: `You ought ___ set a budget.`
- options: `to` · `—` · `for`
- answer: `to`
- why: ought always needs to, unlike should and must.

---

## 06 · mustn't — not allowed

**body**
mustn't means it is not allowed: You mustn't ignore the rules. It is a strong no. Do not use it for “not necessary” — that is don't have to. No to after mustn't.

**formula**
`who` · `mustn't` · `action as in the dictionary`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| You mustn't ignore the rules. | breaking them is not allowed | mustn't |
| We mustn't reveal the answer. | keep it secret | mustn't |
| You mustn't harm the animals. | hurting them is forbidden | mustn't |
| We mustn't offend our neighbours. | do not upset them | mustn't |

**fix**
- ~~You mustn't to ignore the rules.~~ → You mustn't ignore the rules.

---

## 07 · don't have to and needn't — not necessary

**body**
Not necessary, you can choose: don't / doesn't have to or needn't. I don't have to buy supplies; You needn't reply straight away. This is not a rule against it — that is mustn't. No to after needn't; to after don't have.

**formula**
`not necessary: don't / doesn't have to + action` · `no need: needn't + action`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I don't have to buy supplies. | they are given to us | don't have to |
| She doesn't have to bring a photocopy. | it is not needed | doesn't have to |
| He doesn't have to head back yet. | there is still time | doesn't have to |
| You needn't reply straight away. | later is fine | needn't |
| We needn't dress formally. | normal clothes are OK | needn't |

**fix**
- ~~She don't have to bring a photocopy.~~ → She doesn't have to bring a photocopy.
- ~~I don't has to buy supplies.~~ → I don't have to buy supplies.
- ~~You needn't to collect it personally.~~ → You needn't collect it personally.

**drill · binary**
- question: At the art class the organiser gives out the paints. Buying them is not needed, but it is not forbidden either. What do you say?
- A: `I mustn't buy supplies.`
- B: `I don't have to buy supplies.`
- correct: B
- explain: Not necessary: don't have to. mustn't means not allowed.

---

## 08 · Could I…? — review

**body**
The polite request from lesson 19 also appears here: Could I pay separately? Could first, then I and the action with no to.

**formula**
`Could I` · `action as in the dictionary` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Could I pay separately? | each person pays for themselves | Could I |
| Could I take a look? | a quick look | Could I |
| Could I pay on arrival? | pay when I get there | Could I |
| Could I send it by post? | by mail | Could I |

**fix**
- ~~Could I to pay separately?~~ → Could I pay separately?

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Ask politely if you can try it.
- answer: `Could` · `I` · `give` · `it` · `a` · `try`
- slot labels: none (long phrase)
- extra tiles: `to` · `did`

## 09 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I mustn't buy supplies.~~ *(not necessary)* → I don't have to buy supplies.
- ~~You must to take your belongings.~~ → You must take your belongings.
- ~~I has to read the contract.~~ → I have to read the contract.
- ~~You ought read the national news.~~ → You ought to read the national news.
- ~~You needn't to collect it personally.~~ → You needn't collect it personally.

**tip**
to appears only in have to and ought to. After must, should, mustn't and needn't the action comes straight away. Not allowed — mustn't; not necessary — don't have to or needn't.

---

## 10 · What to take into practice

**body**
Required — must. Needed because of the situation — have / has to. Advice — should or ought to. Not allowed — mustn't. Not necessary — don't have to or needn't.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We must go through the list. | check every item | must |
| He should stand up for his friend. | defend his friend | should |
| You needn't justify your choice. | no need to explain why | needn't |
