# Curriculum Roadmap 33-60

This roadmap keeps Lesson 33 connected to a minimum 60-lesson course expansion.

| Lesson | CEFR | Topic | Role |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 33 | B2 | have/get something done | Focused continuation after Lesson 32 object-result patterns. |
| 34 | B2 | wish / if only | Unreal present and regret without broad C1 overload. |
| 35 | B2 | third conditional basics | Past unreal cause and result. |
| 36 | B2 | mixed conditionals basics | Controlled present result from past condition. |
| 37 | B2 | present perfect continuous | Duration and visible result. |
| 38 | B2 | past perfect continuous | Background duration before a past event. |
| 39 | B2 | future continuous and future perfect | Future process and completion. |
| 40 | B2 | advanced modal deduction | must have, might have, cannot have. |
| 41 | B2+ | permission, obligation, and necessity nuance | have to, need to, be supposed to, be allowed to. |
| 42 | B2+ | concession clauses | although, even though, despite, in spite of. |
| 43 | B2+ | purpose and result clauses | so that, in order to, such that, so/such. |
| 44 | B2+ | reason and contrast connectors | therefore, however, nevertheless, whereas. |
| 45 | B2+ | noun clauses | what I need, where we met, whether it works. |
| 46 | B2+ | reported questions advanced | embedded questions and reporting verbs. |
| 47 | B2+ | reporting verbs with patterns | admit, deny, suggest, warn, promise. |
| 48 | B2+ | passive reporting structures | It is said that, he is believed to. |
| 49 | B2+ | participle clauses basics | Walking home, having finished, seen from here. |
| 50 | B2+ | reduced relative clauses | people living here, documents checked yesterday. |
| 51 | C1 | inversion basics | Never have I, only then did I. |
| 52 | C1 | cleft sentences | What I need is, It was John who. |
| 53 | C1 | emphasis with do and auxiliaries | I do understand, he did call. |
| 54 | C1 | hedging and cautious claims | seems to, appears to, is likely to. |
| 55 | C1 | advanced adjective and noun complements | aware of, responsible for, the fact that. |
| 56 | C1 | advanced preposition patterns | dependent on, associated with, involved in. |
| 57 | C1 | formal cause and consequence | thereby, hence, consequently, due to. |
| 58 | C1 | stance and evaluation | arguably, admittedly, presumably, supposedly. |
| 59 | C1 | advanced mixed review 33-58 | Integrated production with controlled complexity. |
| 60 | C1 | final C1 bridge review | Course capstone and readiness for future C1 modules. |
