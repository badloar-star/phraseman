# Theory · Lesson 2 of the new course — «Is it…? Yes, it is. Where is he?» (EN)

> Session «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Same sections (01–14), English phrases and drills as
> `lesson_02_ru.md`. Plain English for learners whose own language is not in the app. Traps are
> the common ones: asking with the voice only (It is possible?), dropping it (Is possible?),
> Where he is?, Yes, I'm. The second column explains the meaning instead of translating.
> Needs a native speaker's read-through.

**Screen title:** Theory · Questions and short answers

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
In lesson 1 you learned to tell things: I am ready, it is open. Now you will learn to ask and answer: «Is it possible?», «Yes», «No», «Where is he?», «What is it?». You will also learn the short way to say «not», the way people really talk.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It's not far. | You can walk there quickly. | not |
| Is it possible? | Can this happen? | Is |
| Yes, she is. | A short «yes» about a woman. | is |
| Where is he? | You want to know his place. | Where |

---

## 02 · «Not» for anyone and anything *(open at start)*

**body**
The rule is the same as in lesson 1: not goes right after am, is or are. Now it works for everyone, not only I am: he is not, it is not, they are not.

**formula**
`who / what` · `am / is / are` · `not` · `a word about them`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am not interested. | I don't want this. | not |
| It is not included. | You pay extra for it. | not |
| He is not aware. | He doesn't know about it. | not |
| It is not far. | You can walk there quickly. | not |
| They are not real. | They are fake. | not |

**fix**
- ~~It not far.~~ → It is not far.
- ~~He no aware.~~ → He is not aware.

---

## 03 · Short «not»: I'm not, it's not, they're not

**body**
When people talk, they almost always shorten the word before not: I am not → I'm not, it is not → it's not. That's how you answer quickly and calmly. The full form is also correct; it just sounds firmer.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I'm not confident. | I don't feel sure of myself. | I'm not |
| I'm not convinced. | I still don't believe it. | I'm not |
| It's not clear. | I don't understand it. | It's not |
| We're not concerned. | We are not worried. | We're not |
| They're not stale. | The bread is still fresh. | They're not |

**fix**
- ~~I'm no convinced.~~ → I'm not convinced.
- ~~It not clear.~~ → It's not clear.

**drill · spot_slip**
- tiles: `I'm` · `no` · `convinced`
- wrong: `no` (index 1)
- hint: One word is wrong. Tap it.
- explanation: After I'm you need not, not no: I'm not convinced.

---

## 04 · How to ask a question

**body**
In many languages you ask a question just with your voice and keep the same order. In English the order changes: is moves to the front. It is possible → Is it possible? If you keep It is possible? and only raise your voice, it sounds like surprise, not a real question.

**formula**
`am / is / are` · `who / what` · `a word about them` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Is it possible? | Can this happen? | Is |
| Are they close? | Are the seats near? | Are |
| Is it urgent? | Must it happen now? | Is |
| Are you well? | Do you feel okay? | Are |
| Am I welcome? | Do they want me here? | Am |

**fix**
- ~~It is possible?~~ → Is it possible?
- ~~Is possible?~~ → Is it possible?

**drill · word_bank**
- task: You see two free seats. Ask your friend if they are near the stage.
- answer: `Are` · `they` · `close`
- slot labels: clip · who · what like
- extra tiles: `Is` · `am`

---

## 05 · Which clip goes in the question

**body**
A question doesn't change who goes with which clip. I still takes am, he, she and it take is, you, we and they take are. Only the place changes: now the clip comes first.

**examples** 🔊
| en | example |
|---|---|
| Am I…? | Am I welcome? |
| Are you…? | Are you well? |
| Is he / she…? | Is he energetic? |
| Is it…? | Is it shady? |
| Are we / they…? | Are they extra? |

**fix**
- ~~Are it possible?~~ → Is it possible?
- ~~Is they close?~~ → Are they close?

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `___ they close?`
- options: `Am` · `Is` · `Are`
- answer: `Are`
- why: They always takes are, in questions too.

---

## 06 · Short answers: Yes, I am. No, it is not.

**body**
People usually don't repeat the whole sentence. They say yes or no, then who, then the clip: Yes, she is. You don't need to say the word from the question again.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Are you well? — Yes, I am. | You feel okay. | am |
| Is she carsick? — Yes, she is. | The car ride made her feel sick. | is |
| Are they unpacked? — No, they are not. | The bags are still closed. | not |
| Is it broken? — No, it is not. | It works. | not |

**tip**
A short «yes» never ends with a short form: Yes, I'm and Yes, she's are wrong. After Yes, use the full clip: Yes, I am. Yes, she is.

**fix**
- ~~Yes, I'm.~~ → Yes, I am.
- ~~Yes, she's.~~ → Yes, she is.
- ~~No, they not.~~ → No, they are not.

---

## 07 · They ask about you — answer about yourself

**body**
If someone asks Are you…?, they mean you. So in your answer you say I: Yes, I am. Yes, you are would sound like you're talking about them. Same with he and she: they ask Is he…?, you say Yes, he is.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Are you well? — Yes, I am. | You feel okay. | I am |
| Are you guilty? — No, I am not. | It wasn't you. | I am |
| Is he innocent? — Yes, he is. | He did nothing wrong. | he is |

**drill · binary**
- question: Your friend asks: Are you well? You feel fine. What do you say?
- A: `Yes, you are.`
- B: `Yes, I am.`
- correct: B
- explanation: They are asking about you, so you answer with I am: Yes, I am.

---

## 08 · First «yes» or «no», then the truth

**body**
Yes and No answer the question itself. If someone asks Is it broken? and the lamp is just not plugged in, start with No and then say what's going on: No, it's unplugged. Yes would mean «yes, it's broken».

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Is it broken? — No, it's unplugged. | It works, it just isn't plugged in. | No |
| Is it valid? — Yes, it is. | The ticket still works. | Yes |
| Is it bent? — No, it is not. | It is straight. | No |

**fix**
- ~~Is it broken? — Yes, it's unplugged.~~ → Is it broken? — No, it's unplugged.

---

## 09 · Who, What, Where

**body**
Sometimes yes or no is not enough: you want to know who, what or where. Then the question word goes first, then the clip, then who: Where is he? Many languages say «He is where?», but in English the question word always starts the question.

**formula**
`who / what / where` · `is / are` · `who / what`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Who is missing? | One person isn't here. Which one? | Who |
| Where is he? | You want to know his place. | Where |
| What is it? | You don't know this thing. | What |
| Where are they? | You want to know their place. | Where |
| Who is absent? | Which person didn't come? | Who |

**fix**
- ~~He is where?~~ → Where is he?
- ~~Where he is?~~ → Where is he?
- ~~What it is?~~ → What is it?

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Your friend went out and didn't come back. Ask where he is.
- answer: `Where` · `is` · `he`
- slot labels: question · clip · who
- extra tiles: `Who` · `are`

---

## 10 · Don't mix up who, what and where

**body**
Who is for people. What is for things. Where is for places. Before you ask, think about what you don't know: the person, the thing or the place.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Who is missing? | You don't know which person. | Who |
| What is it? | The thing is in front of you, but it's strange. | What |
| Where is it? | You know the thing, but you can't see it. | Where |

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `___ is it?` *(there is a strange metal thing with a little wheel in front of you)*
- options: `Who` · `What` · `Where`
- answer: `What`
- why: The thing is in front of you, but you don't know what it is: What is it?

## 11 · One word, three sentences

**body**
Later in the lesson there are lots of new words, but the trick is always the same. With each word you can say how it is, say it's not, and ask. Try it with any new word from the session.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It's shady. | There is shade there. | It's |
| It's not shady. | There is no shade there. | not |
| Is it shady? | Is there shade there? | Is |
| It's tricky. | It's not easy. | It's |
| Is it tricky? | Is it hard? | Is |
| He's not stubborn. | He can change his mind. | not |
| Is he stubborn? | Does he never change his mind? | Is |

**tip**
Short forms like It's and He's don't start questions. The full clip goes first: Is it…?, Is he…?

## 13 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~It is possible?~~ → Is it possible?
- ~~Is possible?~~ → Is it possible?
- ~~Is they close?~~ → Are they close?
- ~~Yes, I'm.~~ → Yes, I am.
- ~~He is where?~~ → Where is he?
- ~~It not far.~~ → It is not far.
- ~~Are you well? — Yes, you are.~~ → Yes, I am.

**tip**
Almost every mistake in this lesson is about where the clip goes. In a question it comes first. After who, what or where it comes second. In a short «yes» it comes last, in full.

**drill · binary**
- question: You want to ask where your friend is. Which is correct?
- A: `He is where?`
- B: `Where is he?`
- correct: B
- explanation: The question word comes first, then is: Where is he?

## 14 · What to take into practice

**body**
To say «not»: not after the clip; when you talk, I'm not, it's not. To ask: put the clip first and keep it: Is it…? Short answers: Yes, I am or No, it is not. Who, what, where: the question word first, then the clip.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| It's not far. | You can walk there quickly. | not |
| Is it possible? | Can this happen? | Is |
| Yes, I am. | A short «yes» about you. | am |
| Where is he? | You want to know his place. | Where |
