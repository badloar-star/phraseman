Plan: retrieve known singular/plural forms while sorting kitchen items. New spoon, fork, cup. No new grammar or ending-pronunciation lesson.

- [spoon](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/spoon), [IPA](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/spoon): ложка / ложка, handled utensil with hollow rounded end for eating/serving/mixing. UK/US /spuːn/. Distractors [soon](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/soon) /suːn/, скоро / незабаром, and [swoon](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/swoon) /swuːn/, падать в обморок / непритомніти ([sense](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/swoon)). Soon proceeds from s directly to vowel; spoon has complete lip closure/release p; swoon has smooth rounded w transition rather than p closure. Give positive cues for both sides.
- [fork](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/fork), [IPA](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/fork): вилка / виделка, small eating utensil with handle and usually three/four pointed prongs. UK /fɔːk/, US /fɔːrk/. Distractors [pork](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/pork), свинина / свинина, and [cork](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/cork), пробка / корок (bottle stopper). Shared rhyme within each accent. Fork initial continuous air between lower lip and upper teeth; pork full lip closure/release; cork closure/release at back of tongue. Do not equate English r and Cyrillic р.
- [cup](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/cup), [IPA](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/cup): чашка / чашка, small round drinking container often with handle, especially tea/coffee. UK/US /kʌp/. Distractors [cap](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/cap) /kæp/, кепка / кепка, and [cop](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/cop), UK /kɒp/, US /kɑːp/, полицейский / поліцейський (informal). Same k-p frame; cap front open vowel, cup central unrounded, cop back vowel. Do not give one cop lip-shape claim for both accents.

[Regular written plurals](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/nouns-form): spoons, forks, cups. Derived endings z/s/s, no added syllable. These are derivations from entries, not heard clips. All selected distractors real nonhomophones; none relies only on final consonant or weak h.
