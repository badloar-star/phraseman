# verb_was_were draft

Status: Jesse reworked.

Training id: `verb_was_were`
Category: `verb`
Priority: `26`

Single learner problem:
The learner translates "был/были" or "був/були" as one idea and guesses between `was`, `were`, `is`, and `are`.

Core contrast:
- `I/he/she/it was`.
- `you/we/they were`.
- Past markers such as `yesterday`, `last night`, `in 2020` should not keep `am/is/are`.
- Negative: `wasn't` / `weren't`.
- Question: `Was he...?` / `Were they...?`.
- Normal action: `I worked`, not `I was worked`.

What this training must not become:
- Not a full Past Simple lesson.
- Not a Past Continuous lesson.
- Not a passive voice lesson.
- Not a broad lesson on all forms of `be`.

Learner-facing copy rule:
- RU/UK copy explains through "о ком или о чем фраза" / "про кого або про що фраза".
- Avoid internal labels such as `subject`, `object`, `base verb`, `main verb`, `auxiliary`, and `modal`.
- Keep ready pairs visible: `I was`, `she was`, `they were`, `wasn't`, `weren't`, `Were they...?`.

Required contract anchors:
- `was_were_easy_001`: wrong `were` feedback mentions `was`.
- `was_were_contrast_001`: wrong `was` feedback mentions `were`.
- `was_were_contrast_004`: wrong `is` feedback mentions `was`.
- `was_were_mixed_001`: wrong `weren't` feedback mentions `wasn't`.
- `was_were_mixed_003`: wrong `Did` feedback mentions `was/were`.
- `was_were_mixed_006`: wrong `She was worked...` feedback mentions `worked`.

Final learner outcome:
The learner can choose:
`I/he/she/it` -> `was`
`you/we/they` -> `were`
negative -> `wasn't / weren't`
question -> `Was/Were` first
normal action -> `worked`, not `was worked`
