# infinitive_vs_gerund_basic draft

Status: Jesse reworked.

Training id: `infinitive_vs_gerund_basic`
Category: `verb`
Priority: `33`

Single learner problem:
The learner translates the second action separately and guesses between `to learn` and `learning`.

Core contrast:
- `want/need/decide/plan/agree + to ...`
- `enjoy/finish/avoid/mind + -ing`
- `to learning` is broken.
- `want learning` is broken for the target meaning.
- `enjoy to learn` is broken for the target meaning.

What this training must not become:
- Not a full infinitive/gerund grammar map.
- Not object-complement patterns like `want me to`.
- Not a phrasal verb lesson.
- Not a lesson about every verb that can take both patterns with meaning changes.

Learner-facing copy rule:
- RU/UK copy explains through "готовая связка" / "готова звʼязка".
- Avoid internal labels such as `subject`, `object`, `main verb`, `auxiliary`, and `modal`.
- Contract keeps `to + base verb` in `contrastSet`, but display copy should prefer `кусок с to` / `звʼязка з to`.

Required contract anchors:
- `inf_ger_easy_001`: wrong `learning` feedback mentions `want`.
- `inf_ger_easy_001`: wrong `to learning` feedback mentions `to learn`.
- `inf_ger_contrast_004`: wrong `to learn` feedback mentions `learning`.
- `inf_ger_contrast_005`: wrong `to work` feedback mentions `verb-ing`.
- `inf_ger_mixed_006`: wrong `I want improving...` feedback mentions `want to improve`.

Final learner outcome:
The learner can choose:
`want to learn`
`need to go`
`decide to start`
`plan to study`
`enjoy learning`
`finish working`
`avoid making`
`mind waiting`
