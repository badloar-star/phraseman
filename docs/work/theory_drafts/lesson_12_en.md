# Theory · Lesson 12 of the new course — «I cleaned, I chose, I went» (EN)

> Session «12 · ТЕОРИЯ», 21.10.2026. Same sections (01–10), English phrases and drills as
> `lesson_12_ru.md`. Plain English for learners whose own language is not in the app. Common traps:
> I clean … yesterday, He visits … yesterday, useed, choosed / goed, I was checked, saying -ed as an
> extra syllable or dropping it. The second column explains the meaning instead of translating.
> Needs a native speaker's read-through.

**Screen title:** Theory · What I did yesterday

---

## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
In lesson 11 you said where people were and what they were like: I was at home. Now you'll say what people did: «I cleaned the placemat yesterday», «I chose a bunch of flowers», «we went on a day trip». Actions have a past form for this.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I cleaned the placemat yesterday. | Yesterday I made the placemat clean. | cleaned |
| I chose a bunch of flowers yesterday. | Yesterday I picked some flowers to buy. | chose |
| We went on a day trip last Sunday. | Last Sunday we went somewhere for one day. | went |
| I had breakfast cereal. | I ate cereal for breakfast. | had |

---

## 02 · Past: action + ed *(open at start)*

**body**
To say something already happened, you usually add -ed to the action: clean → cleaned, park → parked, visit → visited. You don't need was here: I cleaned, not I was clean.

**formula**
`who` · `action + ed` · `what / when`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I cleaned the placemat yesterday. | Yesterday I made the placemat clean. | cleaned |
| I parked in the parking space yesterday. | Yesterday I left my car in the space. | parked |
| He visited the car wash yesterday. | Yesterday he went to the car wash. | visited |
| I checked my driving licence. | I looked at my licence to be sure. | checked |
| I thanked the tour guide. | I said «thank you» to the guide. | thanked |

**fix**
- ~~I clean the placemat yesterday.~~ → I cleaned the placemat yesterday.
- ~~I was clean the placemat yesterday.~~ → I cleaned the placemat yesterday.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `He ___ the car wash yesterday.`
- options: `visit` · `visits` · `visited`
- answer: `visited`
- why: Yesterday is the past, so add -ed: visited.

---

## 03 · The past is the same for everyone

**body**
Good news: in the past there's no s for he and she. I cleaned, she cleaned, they cleaned — one form for all.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She unfolded the blanket yesterday. | She opened out the blanket. | unfolded |
| He moved the tea tray. | He put the tray in another place. | moved |
| We used the parking meter. | We paid at the parking machine. | used |
| She washed the car bonnet. | She cleaned the front of the car with water. | washed |

**fix**
- ~~He moves the tea tray yesterday.~~ → He moved the tea tray yesterday.
- ~~She washeds the car bonnet.~~ → She washed the car bonnet.

---

## 04 · Ends in e — just add d

**body**
If the action ends in e, don't add another e: move → moved, use → used, close → closed, untie → untied. In British spelling, travel doubles the l: travelled.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I untied my shoes yesterday. | I opened the laces on my shoes. | untied |
| I used the salad bowl yesterday. | I put salad in that bowl. | used |
| I closed the car boot. | I shut the back of the car. | closed |
| We travelled during rush hour. | We went when the roads were full. | travelled |

**fix**
- ~~I useed the salad bowl.~~ → I used the salad bowl.
- ~~He moveed the tea tray.~~ → He moved the tea tray.

---

## 05 · How -ed sounds

**body**
-ed is almost never an extra syllable. After soft «hissing» sounds it sounds like «t»: washed. After voiced sounds, like «d»: cleaned. Only after t or d does it add «id»: wanted, needed, counted.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| washed | one syllable, ends in «t» | washed |
| cleaned | one syllable, ends in «d» | cleaned |
| wanted | two syllables: «want-id» | wanted |
| I needed a lint roller. | I had to have a roller for fluff. | needed |
| I counted the tent pegs. | I found out how many pegs there were. | counted |

**tip**
Don't drop the ending — it's the only thing that shows the past. And don't add «id» after sounds other than t and d.

## 06 · Special words: chose, went, saw, had

**body**
Some very common actions change completely in the past, with no -ed. Learn them in pairs: choose → chose, go → went, see → saw, have → had, rise → rose, hide → hid, find → found.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I chose a bunch of flowers yesterday. | Yesterday I picked some flowers to buy. | chose |
| I went to the shop. | I walked or drove to the shop. | went |
| The sun rose at six yesterday. | The sun came up at six. | rose |
| She had some soft cheese. | She ate some soft cheese. | had |
| I chose a brand-new coat. | I picked a completely new coat. | chose |

**fix**
- ~~I choosed a bunch of flowers.~~ → I chose a bunch of flowers.
- ~~I goed to the shop.~~ → I went to the shop.
- ~~He hide the toy car yesterday.~~ → He hid the toy car yesterday.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `I ___ to the shop yesterday.`
- options: `goed` · `went` · `go`
- answer: `went`
- why: go has a special past form: went.

## 07 · Finished events: yesterday, last Sunday

**body**
This form is for things that happened and ended: yesterday, last Sunday, that week. The time words are the same as in lesson 11: yesterday, last Sunday, last night.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We went on a day trip last Sunday. | Last Sunday we went somewhere for one day. | went |
| She went on a school trip yesterday. | Yesterday she went out with her school. | went |
| I enjoyed the boat trip. | I liked the trip on the boat. | enjoyed |
| We had a fire drill yesterday. | Yesterday we practised leaving for a fire. | had |

**fix**
- ~~We go on a day trip last Sunday.~~ → We went on a day trip last Sunday.

---

## 08 · Action or was?

**body**
If there was an action in the past, use the -ed form or a special word: I checked the airbed. If there was no action and you say what something was like or where, use was or were from lesson 11. Not both.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I checked the airbed. | I looked at the air mattress to be sure. | checked |
| I packed my sleeping bag. | I put my sleeping bag into the bag. | packed |
| We waited at the meeting point. | We stayed at the place to meet. | waited |

**fix**
- ~~I was checked the airbed.~~ → I checked the airbed.
- ~~I was pack my sleeping bag.~~ → I packed my sleeping bag.

**drill · spot_slip**
- tiles: `I` · `was` · `checked` · `the` · `airbed`
- extra: `was` (index 1)
- hint: One word is extra. Tap it.
- explanation: With a past action you don't need was: I checked the airbed.

## 09 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I clean the placemat yesterday.~~ → I cleaned the placemat yesterday.
- ~~He visits the car wash yesterday.~~ → He visited the car wash yesterday.
- ~~I useed the salad bowl.~~ → I used the salad bowl.
- ~~I choosed a bunch of flowers.~~ → I chose a bunch of flowers.
- ~~I goed to the shop.~~ → I went to the shop.
- ~~I was checked the airbed.~~ → I checked the airbed.

**tip**
If you see yesterday or last Sunday, look for the past form: -ed or a special word. No s, and no was before the action.

## 10 · What to take into practice

**body**
Past actions: -ed — cleaned, used, wanted. One form for everyone. It sounds like «t», «d» or «id». Learn the special words in pairs: chose, went, saw, had, rose, hid, found.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I noticed a cycle lane yesterday. | Yesterday I saw a lane for bikes. | noticed |
| She used her railcard. | She showed her train discount card. | used |
| I went to the shop. | I walked or drove to the shop. | went |
| I packed the unwanted clothes. | I put the clothes I didn't want into bags. | packed |
