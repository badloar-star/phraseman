## Rules

- No Revenue VNext release with an open P0/P1 item related to money, access, migration, privacy, or misleading purchase copy.
- Every temporary compatibility adapter needs an expiry condition and an automated guard.
- Close an item only with a passing command or runtime artifact recorded in Evidence.
- Never delete or weaken a guard to change an item to closed.
- Review this register after every four product tasks and at every epic gate.
