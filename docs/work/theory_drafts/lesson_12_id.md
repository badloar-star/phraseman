## 01 · Apa yang akan kamu pelajari *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Di pelajaran 11 kamu bercerita siapa ada di mana dan bagaimana: I was at home. Sekarang kamu bercerita apa yang dilakukan: «kemarin aku membersihkan alas piring», «aku memilih buket», «kami pergi jalan-jalan». Bahasa Inggris mengubah kegiatannya ke bentuk lampau.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I cleaned the placemat yesterday. | Kemarin aku membersihkan alas piring. | cleaned |
| I chose a bunch of flowers yesterday. | Kemarin aku memilih seikat bunga. | chose |
| We went on a day trip last Sunday. | Minggu lalu kami jalan-jalan seharian. | went |
| I had breakfast cereal. | Aku sarapan sereal. | had |

---

## 02 · Masa lalu: kegiatan + ed *(terbuka sejak awal)*

**body**
Kita cukup menambah «kemarin» atau «sudah», kata kerjanya tetap. Bahasa Inggris harus berubah: biasanya tambah -ed — clean → cleaned, park → parked, visit → visited. Jangan bilang Yesterday I clean.

**formula**
`siapa` · `kegiatan + ed` · `apa / kapan`

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I cleaned the placemat yesterday. | Kemarin aku membersihkan alas piring. | cleaned |
| I parked in the parking space yesterday. | Kemarin aku parkir di tempat parkir. | parked |
| He visited the car wash yesterday. | Kemarin dia ke tempat cuci mobil. | visited |
| I checked my driving licence. | Aku mengecek SIM-ku. | checked |
| I thanked the tour guide. | Aku berterima kasih kepada pemandu wisata. | thanked |

**fix**
- ~~Yesterday I clean the placemat.~~ → I cleaned the placemat yesterday.
- ~~I already clean the placemat.~~ → I cleaned the placemat yesterday.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `He ___ the car wash yesterday.`
- pilihan: `visit` · `visits` · `visited`
- jawaban: `visited`
- kenapa: Kemarin adalah masa lalu, tambah -ed: visited.

## 03 · Masa lalu sama untuk semua

**body**
Kabar baik: di masa lalu tidak ada s untuk he dan she. I cleaned, she cleaned, they cleaned — satu bentuk saja.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She unfolded the blanket yesterday. | Kemarin dia membuka lipatan selimut. | unfolded |
| He moved the tea tray. | Dia memindahkan nampan teh. | moved |
| We used the parking meter. | Kami memakai mesin parkir. | used |
| She washed the car bonnet. | Dia mencuci kap mobil. | washed |

**fix**
- ~~He moves the tea tray yesterday.~~ → He moved the tea tray yesterday.
- ~~She washeds the car bonnet.~~ → She washed the car bonnet.

---

## 04 · Berakhir dengan e — cukup d

**body**
Kalau kegiatan berakhir dengan e, tidak perlu e lagi: move → moved, use → used, close → closed, untie → untied. travel dalam ejaan Inggris menggandakan l: travelled.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I untied my shoes yesterday. | Kemarin aku melepas tali sepatu. | untied |
| I used the salad bowl yesterday. | Kemarin aku memakai mangkuk salad. | used |
| I closed the car boot. | Aku menutup bagasi mobil. | closed |
| We travelled during rush hour. | Kami bepergian saat jam sibuk. | travelled |

**fix**
- ~~I useed the salad bowl.~~ → I used the salad bowl.
- ~~He moveed the tea tray.~~ → He moved the tea tray.

---

## 05 · Cara membaca -ed

**body**
Kalau bunyi akhir hilang, cleaned terdengar seperti clean — dan orang mengira itu sekarang. Ucapkan bunyi akhirnya: sesudah bunyi tak bersuara jadi «t» (washed), sesudah bunyi bersuara «d» (cleaned), sesudah t dan d jadi «id» (wanted).

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| washed | sudah mencuci *(«wosyt»)* | washed |
| cleaned | sudah membersihkan *(«klind»)* | cleaned |
| wanted | dulu ingin *(«won-tid»)* | wanted |
| I needed a lint roller. | Aku butuh roller penghilang bulu. | needed |
| I counted the tent pegs. | Aku menghitung pasak tenda. | counted |

**tip**
Bunyi akhir itulah satu-satunya tanda masa lalu. Kalau hilang, kalimatnya terdengar seperti sekarang.

---

## 06 · Kata khusus: chose, went, saw, had

**body**
Beberapa kegiatan yang sering dipakai berubah total di masa lalu, tanpa -ed. Hafalkan berpasangan: choose → chose, go → went, see → saw, have → had, rise → rose, hide → hid, find → found.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I chose a bunch of flowers yesterday. | Kemarin aku memilih seikat bunga. | chose |
| I went to the shop. | Aku pergi ke toko. | went |
| The sun rose at six yesterday. | Kemarin matahari terbit jam enam. | rose |
| She had some soft cheese. | Dia makan sedikit keju lunak. | had |
| I chose a brand-new coat. | Aku memilih mantel yang masih baru. | chose |

**fix**
- ~~I choosed a bunch of flowers.~~ → I chose a bunch of flowers.
- ~~I goed to the shop.~~ → I went to the shop.
- ~~He hide the toy car yesterday.~~ → He hid the toy car yesterday.

**drill · choice**
- kalimat: `I ___ to the shop yesterday.`
- pilihan: `goed` · `went` · `go`
- jawaban: `went`
- kenapa: go punya bentuk lampau khusus: went.

## 07 · Kejadian yang sudah selesai: yesterday, last Sunday

**body**
Bentuk ini untuk hal yang terjadi dan sudah selesai: kemarin, Minggu lalu, minggu itu. Kata penandanya sama seperti pelajaran 11: yesterday, last Sunday, last night.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| We went on a day trip last Sunday. | Minggu lalu kami jalan-jalan seharian. | went |
| She went on a school trip yesterday. | Kemarin dia ikut karyawisata sekolah. | went |
| I enjoyed the boat trip. | Aku menikmati perjalanan naik perahu. | enjoyed |
| We had a fire drill yesterday. | Kemarin kami latihan kebakaran. | had |

**fix**
- ~~We go on a day trip last Sunday.~~ → We went on a day trip last Sunday.

## 08 · Kegiatan atau was?

**body**
Kalau ada kegiatan di masa lalu — bentuk -ed atau kata khusus: I checked the airbed. Kalau tidak ada kegiatan dan kamu bilang bagaimana atau di mana — was atau were dari pelajaran 11. Jangan dua-duanya.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I checked the airbed. | Aku mengecek kasur angin. | checked |
| I packed my sleeping bag. | Aku mengemas kantong tidurku. | packed |
| We waited at the meeting point. | Kami menunggu di titik kumpul. | waited |

**fix**
- ~~I was checked the airbed.~~ → I checked the airbed.
- ~~I was pack my sleeping bag.~~ → I packed my sleeping bag.

**drill · spot_slip**
- kartu: `I` · `was` · `checked` · `the` · `airbed`
- berlebih: `was` (indeks 1)
- petunjuk: Ada satu kata berlebih. Ketuk kata itu.
- penjelasan: Kalau ada kegiatan di masa lalu, was tidak perlu: I checked the airbed.

## 10 · Bekal untuk latihan

**body**
Kegiatan di masa lalu — -ed: cleaned, used, wanted. Satu bentuk untuk semua. Ucapkan jelas «t», «d», atau «id» di akhir. Kata khusus dihafal berpasangan: chose, went, saw, had, rose, hid, found.

**examples** 🔊
| en | id | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I noticed a cycle lane yesterday. | Kemarin aku melihat jalur sepeda. | noticed |
| She used her railcard. | Dia memakai kartu keretanya. | used |
| I went to the shop. | Aku pergi ke toko. | went |
| I packed the unwanted clothes. | Aku mengemas baju yang tidak dipakai lagi. | packed |
