past form, or `-ing` after `can / should / must / could / would / may / might / will`.

- One category only: helper word + simple action.
- Beginner-safe RU/UK/ES copy.
- No learner-facing English grammar jargon in RU/UK explanations.
- Every wrong option has specific feedback tied to that option.
- 12 single-choice steps with easy, contrast, mixed, and mixed review flow.
