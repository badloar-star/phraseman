## A2 boundary

- Items combine two familiar facts, a short sequence, or one reason with a practical outcome.
- Grammar requires sentence-level control of frequent A2 forms, not isolated paradigm trivia.
- Reading and pragmatics remain concrete; no answer requires hidden cultural or specialist knowledge.
- Target-language stimuli and options contain no technical identifiers, filenames, dossiers, or generated numbering.
- EN/RU service copy and individually authored Russian distractor rationales are mandatory.
- `reviewStatus` remains `self_checked`; qualified independent linguistic review remains `pending`.
- A2 cannot enter `completedLevels` before item-level review, structural gates, adaptive-fit review, and A1/A2/B1 boundary review pass.
