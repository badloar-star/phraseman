## 01 · What you will learn to say *(open at start)*

**body**
In lesson 6 you talked about things that happen regularly: I work, she cooks. Now you'll talk about what is happening right now, in front of you: «I am observing the birds», «the ice is melting», «she is not tasting the jam».

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am observing the birds. | I'm looking at the birds right now. | am observing |
| The ice is melting. | The ice is turning to water now. | is melting |
| She is not tasting the jam. | She isn't trying the jam now. | is not tasting |
| Is the zipper working? | Does the zipper open and close now? | Is |

## 02 · The clip + action with -ing *(open at start)*

**body**
To say what is happening now, take the clip am, is or are and add -ing to the action: I am watching. You need both parts. Without the clip (I watching) or without -ing (I am watch), the sentence breaks.

**formula**
`who` · `am / is / are` · `action + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am observing the birds. | I'm looking at the birds right now. | am observing |
| She is watching a caterpillar. | Her eyes are on a caterpillar now. | is watching |
| They are using a magnifier. | They have a magnifying glass in their hands now. | are using |
| I am knocking at the door. | I'm hitting the door with my hand now. | am knocking |
| A visitor is waiting. | Someone is there, waiting now. | is waiting |

**fix**
- ~~I observing the birds.~~ → I am observing the birds.
- ~~I am observe the birds.~~ → I am observing the birds.
- ~~She watching a caterpillar.~~ → She is watching a caterpillar.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `She ___ watching a caterpillar.`
- options: `is` · `are` · `—`
- answer: `is`
- why: Before an action with -ing you need the clip; with she, it's is.

## 03 · The same clip as in lesson 1

**body**
Which clip to use depends on who, like at the very start of the course. I — am; he, she, it and one thing — is; you, we, they — are: I am sighing, the ice is melting, we are marching.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am sighing with relief. | I'm breathing out because I feel better. | am |
| He is sneezing. | He's going «atchoo» now. | is |
| The firefly is glowing now. | The little insect is giving light now. | is |
| We are marching. | We're walking together in step now. | are |
| The leaves are whirling. | The leaves are turning round and round in the air. | are |

**fix**
- ~~The leaves is whirling.~~ → The leaves are whirling.
- ~~We is marching.~~ → We are marching.

## 04 · How to write -ing

**body**
Usually you just add -ing: watch → watching. If the word ends in a silent e, drop the e: make → making. If it ends in a short vowel and one consonant, double the consonant: sit → sitting, hop → hopping.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am making my portfolio. | I'm putting my portfolio together now. | making |
| I am sitting. | I'm on a chair now. | sitting |
| I am submitting my work. | I'm handing in my work now. | submitting |
| The sparrow is hopping. | The little bird is jumping now. | hopping |

**fix**
- ~~I am makeing my portfolio.~~ → I am making my portfolio.
- ~~I am siting.~~ → I am sitting.
- ~~It is hoping.~~ *(about jumping)* → It is hopping.

**drill · choice**
- sentence: `I am ___.` *(you're on a chair)*
- options: `siting` · `sitting` · `sit`
- answer: `sitting`
- why: sit has a short vowel and one consonant, so the t doubles.

## 05 · «Not» — after the clip

**body**
Put not where it always goes — right after the clip: I am not guessing, she is not tasting the jam. The action with -ing stays the same. Don't use doesn't here.

**formula**
`who` · `am / is / are` · `not` · `action + ing`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I am not guessing. | I'm sure — this isn't a guess. | not |
| She is not tasting the jam. | She isn't trying the jam now. | not |
| They are not looking. | Their eyes are somewhere else now. | not |
| The wax is not melting. | The wax is staying hard. | not |
| The drummer is not marching. | The drummer is standing still. | not |

**fix**
- ~~I not guessing.~~ → I am not guessing.
- ~~She doesn't tasting the jam.~~ → She is not tasting the jam.
- ~~They are not look.~~ → They are not looking.

**drill · spot_slip**
- tiles: `She` · `doesn't` · `tasting` · `the` · `jam`
- wrong: `doesn't` (index 1)
- hint: One word doesn't belong here. Tap it.
- explanation: With -ing, «not» is is not: She is not tasting the jam.

## 06 · Questions: the clip first

**body**
Make the question like in lesson 2: put the clip first. Is the ice melting? Are you inspecting the seam? Don't use do or does here — they are for questions about habits.

**formula**
`Am / Is / Are` · `who` · `action + ing` · `?`

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| Are you inspecting the seam? | Are you looking closely at the stitches now? | Are |
| Is she inspecting the lining? | Is she checking the inside cloth now? | Is |
| Is the zipper working? | Does the zipper open and close now? | Is |
| Is he sighing? | Is he breathing out like that now? | Is |
| Is the chocolate melting? | Is the chocolate getting soft now? | Is |

**fix**
- ~~Do you inspecting the seam?~~ → Are you inspecting the seam?
- ~~The chocolate is melting?~~ → Is the chocolate melting?

**drill · word_bank**
- task: Ask if the chocolate is melting.
- answer: `Is` · `the` · `chocolate` · `melting`
- slot labels: none (the sentence is long)
- extra tiles: `Does` · `melt`

## 07 · Now — or usually?

**body**
I wear a sweatshirt — that's my habit. I am sweating — that's happening now. In many languages one form covers both, but in English they're different sentences. Ask yourself: is it a habit, or is it now?

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| I wear a sweatshirt. | That's what I usually wear. | wear |
| I am sweating. | I'm hot and wet right now. | am sweating |
| The firefly is glowing now. | The little insect is giving light now. | is glowing |

**fix**
- ~~I observe the birds.~~ *(right now)* → I am observing the birds.
- ~~I am usually wearing a sweatshirt.~~ → I usually wear a sweatshirt.

**drill · binary**
- question: Right now, the ice on the steps is turning to water. What do you say?
- A: `The ice melts.`
- B: `The ice is melting.`
- correct: B
- explanation: It's happening now, so is melting.

## 08 · Something is happening — and where

**body**
You can easily add where: She is in the doorway. I am near the carousel. That's the usual clip with a place — no -ing, because there's no action.

**examples** 🔊
| en | meaning | hi |
|---|---|---|
| She is in the doorway. | She's standing in the door. | is |
| I am near the carousel. | I'm next to the merry-go-round. | am |
| She is murmuring to the baby. | She's speaking very softly to the baby now. | is murmuring |
| My hands are trembling. | My hands are shaking now. | are trembling |

**fix**
- ~~I am being near the carousel.~~ → I am near the carousel.

---

## 09 · Mistakes almost everyone makes

**fix**
- ~~I observing the birds.~~ → I am observing the birds.
- ~~I am observe the birds.~~ → I am observing the birds.
- ~~The leaves is whirling.~~ → The leaves are whirling.
- ~~I am siting.~~ → I am sitting.
- ~~I am makeing my portfolio.~~ → I am making my portfolio.
- ~~She doesn't tasting the jam.~~ → She is not tasting the jam.
- ~~Do you inspecting the seam?~~ → Are you inspecting the seam?

**tip**
Two parts always go together: the clip and -ing. If you see one, look for the other. And keep do and does out of these sentences.
