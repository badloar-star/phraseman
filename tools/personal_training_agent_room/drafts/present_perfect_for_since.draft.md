# present_perfect_for_since draft

Status: Jesse reworked.

Training id: `present_perfect_for_since`
Category: `verb`
Priority: `41`

Single learner problem:
The learner wants to say how long something has continued, but mixes up `for three years` and `since 2020`, or uses a past/simple pattern where the situation is still true now.

Main contrast:
- `for + duration`: `for three years`, `for two weeks`, `for a long time`
- `since + starting point`: `since 2020`, `since Monday`, `since I got home`
- `How long have you lived here?`

What this training must not become:
- Not a general Present Perfect lesson.
- Not a broad Past Simple vs Present Perfect review.
- Not a full prepositions lesson.
- Not a lecture about grammar labels.

Learner-facing copy rule:
- RU/UK copy explains with "период времени / точка старта" and "період часу / точка старту".
- Avoid internal grammar labels such as `subject`, `object`, `main verb`, `base verb`, `auxiliary`, and `modal`.
- Keep the live English chunks visible: `for three years`, `since 2020`, `How long have you lived here?`.

Required contract anchors:
- `pp_for_since_easy_001`: wrong `since` feedback mentions `for three years`.
- `pp_for_since_contrast_001`: wrong `for` feedback mentions `since`.
- `pp_for_since_mixed_001`: wrong `did` feedback mentions `have you lived`.
- `pp_for_since_mixed_006`: wrong swapped sentence feedback mentions `2020`.

Final learner outcome:
The learner can quickly decide:
`three years` -> `for three years`
`2020` -> `since 2020`
continuing now -> `I have lived here for three years`
question -> `How long have you lived here?`
