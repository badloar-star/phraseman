# Research Carousel Pack 001

Date: 2026-06-26
Status: requires_codex_dalle_final_generation

## Design

Format: 1080x1350 Instagram carousel
Style: research-led editorial over unique DALL-E scenes

Principles:
- One pain per carousel
- One useful research-backed argument per slide
- DALL-E through Codex generates one unique scene per slide; final text is rendered separately
- Template-only cards are rejected and cannot be called final
- Every carousel uses contradiction as the hook
- No visible pipeline labels, CTA buttons, or brand stamps on final slides
- High contrast article text, no filler copy
