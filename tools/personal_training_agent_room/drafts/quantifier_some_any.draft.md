# quantifier_some_any

Jesse rewrite for `quantifier_some_any`.

## Scope

- Category: `determiner`
- Status: `active`
- Priority: `18`
- Locales: `ru`, `uk`, `es`
- Main contrast: `some`, `any`, `no`, `not any`, `someone/anyone`, `something/anything`

## Learner problem

The learner chooses `some` and `any` by dictionary translation. The rewrite teaches situation-first selection:

- `some` for a positive amount
- `any` after a negative
- `any` in a neutral question
- `some` in offers and requests
- `any` for free choice, as in `any lesson`
- `no` without `don't/not`
- `something` in positive need
- `anything` after a negative or in questions
